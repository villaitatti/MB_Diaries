Sunday Dec. 8. 1900. I Tatti. Settignano
I made my “sgombero” on Friday the 30th, amid rain and all conceivable discomforts. On Saturday dear genial Buonamici came to lunch, and Miss Cracroft came to meet him. We had some divine music. On Sunday Captain Jephson came to lunch and I took him to the Gamberaia. The next days I moved Bernhard’s things, with the blessed interval of one of Miss Cracroft’s Bach recitals. Bernhard is staying at Poggio, well taken care of by Mrs. Ross, the Capable and Energetic. On Wednesday we went with Logan and Lina and Mrs. Erichsen to Monte Guffone, an unimaginably romantic place.
