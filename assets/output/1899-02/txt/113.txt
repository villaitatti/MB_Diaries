Thursday, May 24. 1900
Ascension Day. At High Mass a young deacon sang … but like an angel. He has one of those strange almost female voices, light and pure as a woman’s, full and resonant as a man’s, vibrating as a violin, and - well,  satisfying . It was a real ecstasy to hear him sing the “Salutaris Hostia”. I never in my life heard such a thrilling voice. 
A fierce scirocco kept us at home most of the afternoon, but we walked towards sunset. Lina is very sweet. Senda told us a little about her work. What a sane, sensible, delightful creature she is! And her work, the using of bodily exercise as an aid to spiritual development is wonderful – the real Greek ideal. I do admire her enormously.
