Wednesday February 26. 1902. I Tatti
Mother writes: “Today is a great match at ‘Upfields’ between the Westminster Boys and the Charterhouse Boys, and all the children are going to look on and to help shout. Grace is to chaperon them. Ray looks radiant and beautiful today, and, if English boys and girls ever thought of such things, she would certainly be a belle at the match. But I do not suppose the two sexes will so much as deign to look at one another.” It reminds me of those famous lines –
“Miss Buss and Miss Beale
Cupid’s darts do not feel.
How different from us
Are Miss Beale and Miss Buss!”
Mrs. Pelly and her sister, “Lady Snake”, came to call, also Walter Savage Landor (illegitimate) grandson of  the  Walter Savage Landor, a pleasant, mild sort of man.
