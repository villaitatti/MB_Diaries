We are spending money at a dreadful rate, but I hope getting lovely things for the new home. B. B. is well in his work, and feeling rather better in health. I am not trying to do much besides music. Logan has just sent off to be type-written, an article on Madame de Sevigné.
Now I will try to begin a real journal.
Monday Oct. 29. 1900. Frullino Florence
