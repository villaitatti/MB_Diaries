Thursday Sept. 28.99.
“Discovered” the fascinating “Cappella dei Pellegrini”, and amused ourselves poking about. Drove in the afternoon to Bettona and Torre d’Andrea.
Friday Sept. 29.99 Frullino Florence
Gladly would we have lingered at Assisi, our best-beloved Italian town, but Bernhard has to come back to see Mrs. Gardner. We walked out to S. Damiano in the morning, and got lost on the hill coming back. Took the 4.19 - 8.50 train here. 
Saturday Sept. 30.99.
Mrs. Gardner had mistaken the day, and called yesterday in Bernhard’s absence! We spent the morning trying to settle in. It poured all afternoon, but Bernhard called on Mme. Rasponi, whom he found jumping from subject to subject like a grasshopper. Read the Art Magazines.
