The doctor came today and found his liver inflamed. Poor dear!
Sunday Oct. 29.99.
Bernhard got up, and as it was very warm we sat in the tower. The Frys called – very nice – and Benn. The Lawsons came to dinner and we told them of a fine Basaiti for sale in Venice for £400, and advised them to try to sell it to a rich Glasgow merchant for £750 fr. We gave them the photograph.
Monday Oct. 30.99.
Fry came to lunch, and we drove over to the Gamberaia. It was most beautiful. Alys sent me a letter from  B.F.C.C.  to her saying that now that he is ill, his income is less, and he must give up the Cottage unless  they  will take it for him.
