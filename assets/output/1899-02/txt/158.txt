Tuesday April 30. 1901. I Tatti
A most beautiful day – the sort of day when one has moments of absolute bien-être and even of poetry, as the light comes and goes on the hills. We had an anthropological lunch, with Professor Belucci of Perugia (and his daughter) and M. Hubert, Reinach’s assistant, a very  simpatico  sort of scholarly and thoughtful young man. Then we had a walk through the woods, and I stopped to see Lina and the Rosses. We read Blake in the evening, but we found very little real poetry outside the “Songs of Innocence”.
