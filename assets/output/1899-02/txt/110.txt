Thursday May 17. 1900. Hassler. Rome
It rained, so we could not go on the picnic we planned. They paid last calls, and so did I, ending up together at Mrs. Elliott’s. Richter and Miss Taylor came to dinner. They filled us with despair by being so blind to all the more liberal and humanizing aspects of art.
Hotel Monzù. Sulmona. Friday May 18. 1900.
Came from Rome 9-2.30, but a beautiful road. Sacred is the Hospital here, and very picturesque the whole town. Good hotel.
Croce Bianca. Rieti. Saturday May 19.
