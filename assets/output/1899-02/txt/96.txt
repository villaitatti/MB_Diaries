Saturday Nov. 11.99. Frullino. Florence
Mr. Benn came to lunch and was very amusing. He and Bernhard walked in the quarries. Grace and I visited Antiquity shops. Miss Cruttwell came to tea, and also reported the Strongs, Loeser, Hobson and Co. as very venomous. Finished the Lo Spagna-Perugino articles. 
Sunday Nov. 12.99.
The Frys came to lunch, and, after a call from the Morgans, accompanied us to Poggio..Bernhard met Conte Cagnola at the train, and brought him up to dine. The Lawsons were here, and very nice.
