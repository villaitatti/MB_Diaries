Bernhard went to the Uffizi with Count Stroganoff. We dined at the Rosses, and had a bottle of Johannisberger – divine! Lina’s uncle, Canon Waterton, a dear, simple old Catholic priest, was there. We walked back through the woods – bright moonlight and nightingales. 
Thursday May 25.99.
Miss Lowndes and Mr. Benn came to lunch. Bernhard walked with Benn and called on the Kerr-Lawsons. I worked upon Sogliani. In the evening we went over the chapter of Bernhard’s book on Filippino, Garbo, and Carli. An  awfully  windy day.
