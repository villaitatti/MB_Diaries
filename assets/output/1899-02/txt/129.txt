Saturday June 23. 1900
Academy. Prince Hohenlohe and Zina called. Floated. 
Sunday June 24. 1900
Pictures. Tea with Kinsellas and Miss Priestley. Perkins to dinner.
Monday June 25. 1900
Senda ill. Frari, San Rocco, antiquity hunting in the morning. Called on Bensons. Dined at Hohenlohe’s with Fortuny.
Tuesday June 26. 1900 
Went to Murano. Called on Kinsellas. Looked at photographs of minor masters.  Bernhard’s 35th birthday .
Wednesday, June 27, 1900
Salute and San Salvatore in the morning. Lido with Miss Priestley and the Kinsellas in the afternoon. Call on Prince Hohenlohe in evening. Zina is a delicious creature.
