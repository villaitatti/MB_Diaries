Sunday May 28.99.
Called together at the Gamberaia to see the adorable Kitty Hall. Placci was there too, and the rather lovely Countess Fabbricotti. Later we called on the Lawsons and bought their Venetian Madonna. Mr. Ward came to dine – gentle and gentlemanly – but as a painter, I should say, “asses’-milk-cum-water”. He is the pupil of Richmond and Costa and adores Leighton. We didn’t say a word! 
Monday May 29.99.
Horne came to dinner – I can’t remember anything else – and I can  barely  remember that!
