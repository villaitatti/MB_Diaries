I can’t think of anything special we have said, except that Bernhard, in answer to the Mortons’ gibe that Englishmen lacked humour said, “Yes, they are noted for a lack of  American  humour.” He describes Arthur Symonds as “drippings of George Moore”.
Horne is here, and we went to the Villa  Salviati  with him. I’ve also visited  Castello ,  Petraia  and I  Collazzi .
