[April 3. 1899]
We drove with Percy Feilding [i.e. Fielding], to have tea at the Scott-Barbers, with the Poet Laureate and his family-in-law. I don’t like Mr. Scott-Barber.
Tuesday April 4. 1899 - Saturday, April 8.
I went to Rome to be with the children, and had a most delicious time. I took them out to the Lake of Albano and to Tivoli. They seemed to me lovelier than ever before. They are so good, and so entertaining. Karin seems to have a real talent for acting. The only people I saw were Andersen, the sculptor, and the Robinsons. The latter returned with me.
