Wednesday June 7. 1899. Roma. Venice
Packed, etc., paid bills and, at the last moment, bought another picture from Torrini! Came here with Miss Erichsen – a hot, tiresome journey from 3-11. 
Thursday June 8.99.
Went in the morning to San Giovanni e Paolo and the Scuola di San Marco. The afternoon was most exciting, for we called on the  antiquario  beside St. Mark’s and found some excellent Sienese forgeries, which give us doubts about —---- most of Bernhard’s pictures!!
