Saturday November 9 - November 16. 1901. I Tatti
A quiet week. Grace and Herbert Horne were away and Christina Brenmer arrived. The silly Gravina came to lunch one day, and delightful Carlo Placci returned from his wanderings came and gave us his basket of news. The weather has been very warm, we sit with open windows. I have been rather unwell.
