Tuesday Oct. 31.99. Frullino Florence
Bernhard a little better, perhaps. Reading “ Euphues ”.  Benn and Morgan to tea.
Wednesday Nov. 1.99.
Very quiet nice day. Frys to dinner.
Thursday Nov. 2.’99
It is the anniversary of my darling Evelyne’s death. Sometimes a vague envy of her comes over me. Does she still love and suffer, I wonder? We walked to Poggio and saw Mr. Ross, who, I fear, is failing.
