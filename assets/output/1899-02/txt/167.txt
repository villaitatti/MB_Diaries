Monday May 21. 1901.
We had an enchanting drive to Villamagni – the Spring at its best. 
Tuesday May 22. 1901
Ned Warren came  up to tea, and remained to dinner. He was very agreeable, and told a number of most amusing stories about wild goose chases after bronzes and antiques. 
Wednesday May 23. 1901
A note from poor Jephson, who is ill again. All he could say was that he couldn’t write on account of headache and seediness. B. B. spent part of the afternoon basking in the woods. I went to his old Villa, had a music-lesson, called on dear old Mr. Ross.
