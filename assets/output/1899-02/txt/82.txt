Wednesday Sept. 20. Amorosi. Bibbiena
Met Alys and Bertie at Arezzo at 8.50, saw Arezzo and the stupendous frescoes of Pier dei Franceschi, took train and came here. Drove in the afternoon, or rather, Alys drove, and Bertie, Bernhard and I bicycled to Ortignano, a pretty place with an important picture by Pacchia. 
Thursday. Sept. 21. Bibbiena
All-day excursion to Camaldoli – a  terrible  up-hill tug for bicyclists. Alys and I shared, fastening onto the carriage with a rope. I relapsed into my old illness and came down feeling like the devil. But the place is lovely. Found a horseshoe which Alys said meant  B.F.C.C’s  death, poor man!
