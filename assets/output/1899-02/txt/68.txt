Monday June 5.99. Frullino. Florence 
Day of packing and last things. Horne and the Kerr-Lawsons came to dinner, and we sat out on the lawn enjoying the fireflies.
Tuesday June 6.99
Lina to lunch and she came shopping with me after my music lesson. Blair Fairchild sent a raging note to Bernhard, professing to find an insult to Miss Blood in the attempts Bernhard made on Sunday to warn him that his nocturnal visits to the Gamberaia were observed. Bernhard wrote explaining, and an answer came making up, but confessing to horror and misery that he had given cause for scandal, and saying he would go away at once.
