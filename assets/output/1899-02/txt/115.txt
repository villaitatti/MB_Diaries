Saturday May 26. 1900.
Sights of Perugia and a drive to the Reservoir. Senda told me a good deal about her  most interesting  work in physical training. She has great good sense and character.
Sunday May 27. 1900
Sights. I read two novels by Lucas Malet, but did not think much of them.
Monday May 28. 1900. Nazionale. Cortona 
Still sights, and at 2 we came to Cortona, which was even lovelier than we had remembered. What a site!
