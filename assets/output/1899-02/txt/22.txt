We ended up ourselves at Poggio [Gherardo], with Lina, making a fiery procession through the olives. Then we went in and had dinner, and I found a great bunch of orchids at my plate, and my health was drunk in champagne. Mr. Ross told me stories of crocodile-shooting on the Nile in the days before steamboats, and Mrs. Ross talked scandal to Logan and BB. The walk home in the moonlight was pleasant.
Wednesday Feb. 15. 1899. Frullino. Florence.
That idiotic, sentimental Miss Hamilton to lunch – but Benn  also, and we talked around and beyond her. Bernhard and Benn walked, and I went down and had my nose burnt out, and then shopped a little with Logan, coming home, however, to tea. Finished the  Symposium  in the evening. I began  Trevelyan’s Life of Charles James Fox .  Bernhard is reading  Rohde’s Psyche .
