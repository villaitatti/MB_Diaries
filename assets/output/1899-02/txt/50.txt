Monday May 1st.99. Frullino. Florence.
Horne came up to lunch, and was as boring as usual. Miss Erichsen and I had a nice chat. I like her, not wildly, but thoroughly. 
Tuesday May 2.99.
The day some how frittered by. Bernhard is  miserable  at not getting on better with his work, and feeling so low in health. I feel awfully lazy, and am doing nothing.
Wednesday May 3.99.
Bernhard and I had a long and most enchanting bicycle ride up the river. My tire gave way coming home. I went with Miss Erichsen to see the actor Novelli in “ Papa Lebonnard ”, a filthy melodrama about as agreeable to see as a surgical operation. Lina came to lunch to look at the Giottos. She was very sweet.
