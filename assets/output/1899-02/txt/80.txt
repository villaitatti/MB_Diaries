Friday Sept. 15.99. Cavour Milan
Spent the day, together with the Holroyds, at Vittadini’s museum-villa, Arcore. Found a note from that bore, Lady Edmond Fitzmaurice, asking me to come and see her, as she is ill in this hotel. Called on her, and again after dinner with Bernhard. 
Saturday Sept. 16.99.
Poldi with Vittadini, Noseda, and Prince Pio di Savoia, a Moroni looking man, a great Spanish grandee. They all came to lunch, and most of them stayed on to a visit to the Brera in company with its director, the flowery Corrado Ricci. Called on Lady Edmond and met that rather really remarkable youth, Stickney, and his commonplace sister, and an Italian Jew doctor of Genoa, named De Filippi. Lady Edmond entertains in a rather distinguished manner.
