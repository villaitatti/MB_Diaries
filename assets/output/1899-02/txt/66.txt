Friday June 2.99. Frullino. Florence.
Bernhard went to the Laurenziana in the morning. I cleaned house – the rooms are being done over. Music lesson in the afternoon, and then a pleasant call from Prince Hohenlohe and the beautiful Zina. Lawson called and told us that the Priore, where Blair Fairchild lives, said the young man spent all his time at the Gamberaia, never returning before 2 or 3 in the morning, generally later – “ anche le cinque passate ”. Of course all Settignano knows it. It amused me, remembering Miss Blood’s former boasts of ethereal affections, feeding on air!
