Monday. September 23. 1901. Hotel Bellevue. Cadenabbia
We went to Blevio to lunch with Mrs. McCreevy, a rich American (southern) gran-widow of unenviable fame. I did not like her, and she did not amuse me much. Chatted with the Laboucheres in the evening. 
Tuesday September 24. 1901. La Gazzada. Varese
Spent a rainy day in Milan, seeing Prince Trivulzio’s collection. Bought a Borgognone at Cantoni’s for 7000 lire. Came here after dinner in a  pour .
Wednesday September 25.
A most lovely house. I went all over it with Don Guido. It rained all day.
