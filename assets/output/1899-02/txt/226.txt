Saturday  February 15. 1902. I Tatti
Miss Cracroft too ill for Music. Phillip and Lady Ottiline Morrell came to lunch. She was exquisite. Cust and his train of  putti  came afterwards, and Stein, not at all exquisite, and wore out one afternoon. 
Sunday February 16. 1902
A letter from Mounteney, who says he is suffering such remorse that he cannot write to me. I am so sorry. I know how frightfully he suffers. And I hate to have caused it, and then to be able to afford no helps, not to say even one friendly, affectionate word.  
Monday February 17. 1902.
We dined at Doney’s with the Morrells and were to have gone to see the Duse in “La Gioconda”, but the Play was put off. 
Fabbri called. Am reading “Clarissa”.
