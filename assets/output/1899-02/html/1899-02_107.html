Aspinwall’s. Marconi sang like a bull.

Thursday May 10.

Visited Mr. Nevin’s pictures. They lunched with the Duchessa Grazioli and we all went to the Borghese. Miss Kolb came to dine in the evening.

Friday May 11. 1900

Called on Miss Kolb at the French Embassy and saw the celebrated Caracci ceilings, and some fine Gobelins. Corsini, Farnesina. B.B. called on Mrs. Elliott, the Princess Venosa,the Nortons.

Saturday May 12. 1900

Went to Tivoli and Hadrian’s villa with Rezia Rasponi and the Humphreys Johnstons. I always knew the Villa d’Este would satisfy my ideal, and it did. I had a fierce headache and went to bed. Domenico Anderson came in the evening.
