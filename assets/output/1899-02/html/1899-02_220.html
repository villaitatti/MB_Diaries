Nothing said worth remembering. Bernhard went to the Uffizi, but I came home and found Lovett, as interesting and sympathetic as ever. We talked all afternoon – despairingly of the present self-satisfied vulgarity of America, the way “the present generation” devote all their energy not to reading but to sport etc. Bernhard came in and Lawson. Don Quixotein the evening.

Friday, January 31. 1902 [written: 1901]. I Tatti.

Cold awful. Logan and B.B. went to lunch at the Placci’s. Placci read them verses of a new poet named Orsini, good verses too. They went to a poorish concert and then to call on Rezia Corsini, whom they found charming. I began my reviews for the Gazette – Bernhard’s Lotto, Strutt’s Fra Filippo, Boyer d’Agen’s Pintoricchio and the Mantegnas of Yriarte, Kristeller and Maud Cruttwell.
