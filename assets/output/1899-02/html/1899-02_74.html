Cavenaghi then took us to Crespi’s, and what did I discover there but two more forgeries from the same hand!! It took some little trouble to convince Cavenaghi, but once convinced, he behaved very handsomely about it. In the evening we gave him and Aldo Noseda a dinner at the Sempione.

Sunday June 25. Manin. Milan

Churches in the morning – San Celso, Sant’Ambrogio, Santa Maria Maggiore, Santa Maria delle Grazie. Lunch with Lawsons – a visit to the Castello with Frizzoni, Vittadini, Noseda, Cavenaghi and – Loeser!! The latter very cordial.
