Friday, Oct. 6.99. Frullino Florence

Came home by early train. Found despairing letters from Alys saying all Lady Henry’s pleasure is destroyed by Lord Henry’s having come to Venice. I wired inviting her and Mr. Saunders here, tho’ Alys says Mr. Saunders is an uncultivated, rude, narrow, middle-class prig. Lady Henry, however, adores him. In the evening we had one of the nicest talks we have ever had!

Saturday Oct. 7.’99

Bernhard went to Milan, to go to the Gazzada on a visit. I began my Louvre Guide. Finished Sense and Sensibility.
