Thursday May 11. 1899. Frullino. Florence.

Quiet day. Bernhard finished cataloguing his Leonardo drawings (for his book) and I type-wrote his article on Colvin’s Maso Finiguerra. Called on Miss Lowndes and Mrs. Jeffreason.

Friday May 12.99.

Doctor. Miss Erichsen for the night. Horne to dinner, and the chaplain at Siena, a vulgar journalist named Langton Douglas. A man I hated.

Saturday May 13.99.

Miss Lowndes, Miss Erichsen and ourselves spent the day at Monte Senario. It was very beautiful. I returned with a bad headache.
