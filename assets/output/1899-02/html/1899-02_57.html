Thursday May 18.99.

Music, exercise, massage. Bernhard called on Count Stroganoff. The Houghtons – a sort of Hamstead slummer, Miss Dixie called here. I think from a letter of Blaydes that he is again in love, this time with an actress named Knewstub, who calls herself Kingsley, and who is very thick with that amusing little snipe Rothenstein, said to be his fiancée. He doesn’t say he is in love, but the symptoms are there. I must say, from the sound, it seems worse than Mrs. Heinemann, – who by the way, writes quite sentimentally of ‘the source of all that is hopeful in my life – my husband’s love’.
