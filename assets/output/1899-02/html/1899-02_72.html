Friday June 23.99. Manin. Milan

Called on Frizzoni and went with him to the Poldi, where Loeser ran into us, awfully embarrassed! Bernhard shook hands with him and begged him to be natural. We met him again, later, at Cavenaghi’s, and he had recovered himself so that he stayed quite a while chatting. Aldo Noseda came in. Cavenaghi alas! says our kneeling knight is a forgery. And we could have got nine hundred pounds for it!! But we have suspected it to be the work of our “Aragno da Siena,” and he confirms our suspicions.

The Lawsons telegraphed us to meet them at the train as they were going north, and we found we had so much to talk about that they stayed off. They are keenly interested in the “Aragno.”
