Saturday February 22 /02. I Tatti

The Houghtons and Mr. Rosenheim to lunch. Bach concert afterwards. Horne to dine.

Sunday February 23. 1902

Had a delightful drive to La Quiete, then a walk through the Careggi woods. B.B. then called on Horne and Logan and I on Donna Lucrezia Corsini – nice, fresh, hearty creature.

Monday February 24. 1902.

Music lesson – Concert. Bernhard in Uffizi. I finished Clarissa Harlowe, nine volumes of what comes perilously close to twaddle, but somehow manages to be interesting. Don Quixote is enchanting!
