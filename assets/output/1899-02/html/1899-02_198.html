Thursday October 17. 1901. I Tatti. Settignano

Music lesson. Called on Rosses. B. B. reading L’Opposition sous les Césars.

Friday October 18. 1901

Rain. Benn came to lunch, but nothing special was said. Went to Galli-Dunn’s Antiquity shop but found nothing. Looked over “Who’s who” and found that one man being asked for his wife’s maiden name, for the 2nd ed. replied “I am sorry I cannot give you my wife’s maiden name, but she is at present travelling in Europe”.
