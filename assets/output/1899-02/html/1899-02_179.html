To me, outside of the family, our complete reconciliation with the Michaels, and the further friendship with Mounteney Jephson were the pleasantest personal things. The Mikes are quite adorable, and Mounteney, though in a totally different world of interests, is a thoroughly nice person. I spent 4 days with him in Paris on my way here, he coming up from Hyères to meet me. We looked forward to going to the Louvre, to Chartres, to seeing all sorts of things together; but he was ill in bed the whole time, and suffering a great deal.

In Paris and on the way down I read that really great book Adolphe, and the much over-rated Liaisons Dangereuses. O yes, how could I forget. We read Maeterlinck’s charming Vie des Abeilles. How I enjoyed it!
