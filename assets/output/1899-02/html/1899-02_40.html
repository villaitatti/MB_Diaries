However, baby as he is, she says Blair is an old hand. We called on Mrs. Fairchild, too. Bernhard in the meantime lectured Janet Dodge on her selfishness. (Kerr-Lawson had been telling him that they were often on the point of liking her, and then were repelled by finding her so utterly absorbed in herself.) He made her cry, but she defended herself and said she had lots of devoted friends, that she wasn’t selfish and so on. Afterwards Trevy took her for a long walk, and this no doubt consoled her, especially as they started out for Poggio Gherardo and ended by sitting in the woods instead. I saw some old Scotch acquaintances in Florence, the Birkmyres.

In the evening we read St. John.
