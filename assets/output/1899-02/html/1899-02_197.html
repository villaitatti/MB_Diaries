I should prefer life after death because I have always been very happy and there have been times when I have felt that the happiness, if the conditions continued unchanged, had enough in it to make it last indefinitely. It seems to be only hindering circumstances that prevent continuous joy, and I like to dream of vague conditions under which nothing should impede us. My feeling comes really from a sense of having more capacity for enjoyment and happiness and even love than Life has ever yet been able to draw out, or is likely to.
