Tuesday Feb. 28. 1899. Hassler. Rome

Bernhard went to see some pictures with Ned Warren, and lunched with him. We hunted antiquities in the morning, and went to the Borghese in the afternoon with Mr. Robinson. Dined with the Robinsons and Andersen (the sculptor). Bernhard called on the Duchessa Grazioli and found her fascinating.

Wednesday March 1.99.

Shopped, and Bernhard bought a Cariani portrait of a youth, very Giorgionesque (400 lire), and a Bernardino da Mariotto tabernacolo (1250 lire). He lunched with the Grazioli, Molmenti and the Duca di Camastro. Called to see Andersen’s things (most of which are awful!) and then walked on the Pincian. Tea with Miss Hertz.
