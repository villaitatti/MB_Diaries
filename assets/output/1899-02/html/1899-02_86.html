Sunday Oct. 1.99. Frullino. Florence

Bernhard went to Siena to join Mrs. Gardner. I put things in order, and arranged the summer’s notes.

Monday. Oct. 2.99.

Bernhard went to Monte Oliveto with Mrs. Gardner and Procter. Mrs. Gardner says Miss Fairchild spent the winter writing her violent letters against him, saying how universally hated he was, how Placci (!) couldn’t endure him, etc., etc., until at last Mrs. Gardner wrote to say how could they let Blair go and stay with such a disreputable character. To which Miss Fairchild wrote the whopping lie that he only stayed two days, and that because we both begged and entreated him so that he couldn’t get out of it. They had a laugh over it together. Mrs. Gardner said the Fairchilds are nobodies in Boston.

I spent the day quietly working, etc.
