Sunday Nov. 19.99.

Percy Feilding’s cousin, Miss May Levett, came to lunch – a very ordinary, but agreeable kind of English girl. We called at the Rosses, and the Lawsons came back with us to tea. Count Hochberg was at the Rosses, and a great crowd of Sunday visitors.

Monday Nov. 20. 1899.

Bernhard lunched with the Placcis and their Mexican Bishop, and called on Benn. Grace and I shopped.

x x

Thursday Nov. 23.99

Grace and I have been for two days at Siena with the Frys – two delicious days. Bernhard stayed at home slowly preparing for his chapter on Michelangelo. Lady Edmund came to lunch today.
