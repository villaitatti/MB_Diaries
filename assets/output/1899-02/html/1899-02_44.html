Then we came up and had tea at Bernhard’s with Placci and young Visconti Venosta (such a clever, nice boy) and Guido Marti, a so-called poet, nephew to Nencioni. Then a little stroll, and, in the evening, Jacopo Bellini photographs. Bernhard is reading Boswell, and I Gosse’s Life of Gray.

x Easter Sunday April 2. 1899. Frullino. Florence

Mr. and Mrs. Gutekunst came to lunch and she was really dreadful. Her one idea is an insipid sort of flirting. Otto sang – a very fine voice and nice musical feeling.
