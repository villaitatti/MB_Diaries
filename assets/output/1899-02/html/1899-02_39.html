Friday March 24.99.

After my music lesson I called for Mrs. Hooker (a California friend of my dearest Evelyn) and her daughter and brought them up to tea. Janet Dodge also came. Talked against Christianity and the Papacy all the evening. Read Mrs. Piozzi’s Italian Letters.

Saturday March 25.99.

Windy and cold. Bernhard called on Miss Blood, and I stayed in. Miss Priestley came to dinner, and talked amusingly all the evening.
