* Tuesday May 23.99. Frullino. Florence

Stroganoff came to tea. In the evening we walked up to a party at the Jeffreasons, but Bernhard stayed with Miss Lowndes, who lives at their gate,and I went on with Miss Cruttwell. I saw the Houghtons, but successfully avoided Miss Zimmern, Lady Wade, Dr. Tidey, the Morgans, and various other acquaintances. The walk home with moonlight and fireflies was enchanting.

Wednesday May 24.99
