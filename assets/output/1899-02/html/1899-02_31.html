Her mother was there and drew Bernhard aside to speak against Miss Paget’s selfishness, her grasping ways, her habit of spying on Mme. Pasolini. Mme. Pasolini sang her song of reform in taxation.

Saturday March 4.99

Logan and I went with Andersen to the Terme. We enjoyed the Apollo and liked Andersen. We bought a Balducci! Bernhard took the Grazzioli to the Stanze and she took him to lunch with her “residuary lover”, the Duca di Camastro, who has rooms that made Bernhard sick with envy.
