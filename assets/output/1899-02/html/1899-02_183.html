Thursday September 26. 1901. Gazzada

Chatted and strolled about. Drove in the afternoon to a romantic Villa called Frascarola – yews, cypresses, fountains, desolation – the real Tuscan Stimmung. BB and I both laid low by poisoning – some oysters. I fear, that we ate at Milan. Donna Carmelita Zucchini is here, very warming and delightful. Also an intelligent man, Professor Stucchi.

Friday September 27. 1901.

Logan writes “I have found a quantity of thy letters among these old papers. Shall I send them to thee? There are all thy letters when thee was at Smith College and I was at Haverford 1883-4 – charming letters in which thee tried to wake my soul by pouring out the enthusiasm of thy own. What a charming, simple Arcadian world (see from this distance) these letters create! How I looked up to your brilliant life of intellect at Smith
