Friday May 3. 1901. I Tatti

Quiet day with music lesson. Percy Fielding came up to stay, and Mrs. Cameron and her daughter dined with us.

Saturday May 4. 1901

Maud Cruttwell came to lunch. She says Mrs. Strong was here a fortnight, hanging on Loeser’s lips, and repeating all his phrases. She finds her terribly snobbified – a real social struggler.

We went to Fiesole in the afternoon, called on Gronaus and drove home in enchanting weather by Vincigliata.

I have taken back that rogue Carlo as cook.

Sunday May 5. 1901

Mrs. Robinson, the Houghtons and a Miss Weeks (sent by Mr. Stein) came to tea, and we went to the Gamberaia – a most heavenly afternoon. Pleasant dinner with Percy Fielding and Mrs. Robinson, who spent the night.
