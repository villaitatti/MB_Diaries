A telegram came from Mr. Withers saying that the Chancery Judge has given leave for the children to come, and dismissed as frivolous the “testamentary guardians’” request that we should be forced to have the Governess also. I have caught (from Lina) a bad cold.

Tuesday Nov. 13. 1900.

The wind has sprung up – a cold Tramontana. But at the dear Tatti it is perfectly calm. The place is well sheltered. The workmen are getting on, but rather slowly. I called on the Mortons, and found them as usual in the throes of domestic problems. I am afraid even Rosa won’t suit them. My cold is awful, and I have lost both taste and smell. Bernhard had a glorious walk on the hills.
