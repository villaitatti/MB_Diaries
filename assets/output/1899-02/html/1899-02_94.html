Sunday Nov. 5.99. Frullino. Florence

The Frys came to lunch and we drove to Settignano, met the Lawsons and walked to the Bagazzano! Loeser, the Strongs and Hobson started a little behind us evidently for the same place, but seeing us they did not come in! We returned to tea with the Lawsons. An exquisite day. What a change for Eugenie Sellers, to spend her time with Loeser. It appears he and the Strongs are inseparable.

Monday Nov. 6.99.

Bernhard lunched with the Serristoris, and then went into Bardini’s, where the Strongs were eagerly listening to – Loeser! He called on the Frys and had tea with M. Gustave Dreyfus.
