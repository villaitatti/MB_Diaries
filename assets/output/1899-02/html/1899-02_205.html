Mother writes: “Mr. Thorded told Grace that Lady Mount Temple’s deathbed was a scene of strife – Juliet kept lighting candles, and one of the dear Lady’s nieces kept blowing them out. Then Juliet kept trying to moisten the Lady’s pinched lips, and “Uncle Augustus” kept snatching the sponge from her and saying no one should touch his sister but himself. I tell Alys I shall engage a Policeman to be present at my deathbed, so that if Ray lights candles and Alys blows them out, he may interfere to preserve order.”

Wednesday. October 30. 1901.

A letter from poor Mounteney, written again from the depths of a raging headache. Poor thing! Grace arrived at 7.15, as jolly and delightful as ever.
