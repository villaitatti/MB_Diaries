Friday Nov. 24. 1899. Frulli Florence

Called on the Buttles. Bernhard had Mme. Villari and the Countess Gravina to tea. Picquet in the evening. Bernhard lunched with Count Stroganoff.

Saturday Nov. 25.99.

Grace and I shopped, and we all called on Mrs. Ross.

Sunday Nov. 26.99.

Grace and I called on Lady Wade. Bernhard had a long visit from Herr von Beckerath.

Monday Nov. 27.’99

Grace and I spent the morning sightseeing and the afternoon having a regular gorge of Christmas shopping for Miss Toplady.
