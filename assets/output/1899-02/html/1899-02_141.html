In the morning I took Rosa to install her with the Mortons at the Villa Mezzaratta. She seemed contented. Benn and Maud and Miss Lowndes came to lunch, and after visiting the Tatti we went to hear Miss Cracroft play. It was the first of a series of little recitals I have arranged for her. It went off very well. She gave us the Fantasia and Fugue in G minor, and analyzed them a little. She played gloriously.
