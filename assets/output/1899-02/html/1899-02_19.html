Monday Feb. 13. 1899.

Quiet but most lovely day. Horne came up to tea and he and Bernhard and I walked in the park of the Villa Camerata and saw over the beautiful house which is to let. The weather is perfect. We began to read the Symposium, and I read Macaulay on Frederick the Great.

Tuesday Feb. 14. 1899.
