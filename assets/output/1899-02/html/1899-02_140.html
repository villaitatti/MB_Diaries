Thursday Nov. 8. 1900. Frullino. Florence

Bernhard is getting on well with his work. The new cook came today, and is doing very well. Rosa is in tears and declares she has been calumniated. Poor thing! Maud came to work in the morning, and in the afternoon I went to the Tatti with the donkey, and then to see the Cracrofts, Cobbs, and Mortons at Settignano.

No word about the children, and I counted on hearing today.

x Friday Nov. 9. 1900

Letter saying it was all right about the children’s coming. New cook functioning well as to cooking, everything else volcanic. Villa coming on despairingly slowly! But it looks enchanting there.

Saturday Nov. 10. 1900

Mrs. Gardner has telegraphed that she would take the little Raphael Entombment Bernhard offered her.
