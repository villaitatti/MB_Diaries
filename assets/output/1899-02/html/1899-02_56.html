Went on with Fra Bartolommeo in the evening. Bernhard is reading Robertson’sHistory of Free Thought, which is a poor, second-hand affair, he says, though done by an able man.

x Wednesday May 17.99. Frullino Florence

Saw Janet off – thank heavens! Massage, shopping and home. Bernhard called at the Gamberaia and found Miss Blood, the inevitable Fairchild, the Princess and Placci. Only once did Miss Blood give herself away. Bernhard said, “I left a letter for you, Fairchild.” “Who was it from?
