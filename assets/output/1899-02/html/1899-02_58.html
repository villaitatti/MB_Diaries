Friday, May 19.99. Frullino Florence.

Doctor and music and a quiet day. The Venetian furniture arrived. Count Stroganoff and the Marchesa Incontri had tea with Bernhard, and afterwards he went for a walk and called on Mr. Ross. He finished Robertson’s History of Free Thought.
