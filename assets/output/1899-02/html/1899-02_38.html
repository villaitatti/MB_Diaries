Wednesday March 22.99. Frullino Florence

Walked up to Morgans and had tea – a splendid walk, but cold. Read Luke in evening, interrupted with brayings from Bernhard’s ass of a cousin – poor, well-meaning idiot, with brains enough to make a decent tram-car conductor! Began Morley’s Walpole.

Thursday March 23.99.

A rainy day. Called on Mrs. Ross and Lina. Bernhard took the Trevys to the Pitti. His cousin left.
