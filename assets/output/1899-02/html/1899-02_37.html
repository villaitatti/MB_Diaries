Sunday March 19.99.

Lina called in the morning. Logan and I called at Mr. Fiske’s on Mr. and Mrs. Alfred Austin. I talked such platitudes to him, that I looked round in fear someone else might overhear them. Read St. Luke.

Monday March 20.99.

Walked with Logan and Trevy to Kerr-Lawsons’ pretty new home. Read Plato.
