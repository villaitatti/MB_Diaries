Hassler Rome May 7. 1900 Monday

I stayed at the Frullino from the middle of February till the middle of April. I do not know why I did not write. The chief things in our lives were that Bernhard was working very hard upon Michelangelo, that we took a new Villa, that we met that grand old lady Frau Wagner, that Senda Berenson arrived from America about the middle of March, and that Logan and Mrs. Robinson (whose husband is in South Africa fighting the Boers) stayed with us. Bernhard paid a visit, too, to his friend Donna Laura Gropallo, at Nervi. An awful American named Zug haunted us, engaged in “crocking up art like Berenson”, as he put it.

Then I went home for the Easter Vacation, and had a glorious time, with quantities of children at Friday’s Hill – the pond, the “catacombs”, 3 riding horses – and laughter and laughter all day long. It was delicious.
