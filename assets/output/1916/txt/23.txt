Monday, January 17, 1916, I Tatti
Julia, Geoffey Cecil 
Grey
(5 lunch 9 dinner) 
Nails and dress occupied Julia’s morning, while I interviewed cooks, etc. 
B.B. and I walked from the Tree to Fiesole. 
Prince Paul, Miss Hulton ,  Aunt Janet, Lina, and the boys dined here. Aunt Janet brought her photograph album and the Prince was much interested to meet a person who had known Thacheray and Dickens and Meredith and Kinglake, and de Lesseps and Ferdinaso (as they call him) of Bulgaria.
