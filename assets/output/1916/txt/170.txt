Sunday, July 9, 1916, I Tatti
Nora [Grifeo] G[eoffrey] S[cott] C[ecil] [Pinsent] 
Very hot
Went with Nora and Hutton to see Iris’ boy-scout show. It was very well done. She organizes well. Sybil was in bed feeling so ill that she thought she was going to die. I called on Lady Methuen.
Marcella came up to tea and stayed on to dine. She was like a piece of hard crystal, and Geoffrey felt himself outside a door that had been banged to. Of course she  had to face the fact that he cared more for Nicky, and the idea of her, but she is very self-absorbed and apparently doesn’t give him a thought. 
Nora’s friend, Contessa Cipriani, came up and also stayed to dine. She works among the Italian immigrants in N[ew] Y[ork].
