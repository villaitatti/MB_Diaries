Monday, June 12, 1916, I Tatti
G[eoffrey] S[cott] C[ecil] P[insent] Naima [Löfroth] Don Guido [Cagnola] 
Fine
Walked and chatted with Don Guido in morning. Tea at Sybil’s, though she was in bed and could see only B.B. Called on Yoï, and met Guido later at Placci’s where Elizabeth Henraux was. 
Salvemini and M me  Dauriac (Luchaire) came to dine and Guido went off at 11.20.
