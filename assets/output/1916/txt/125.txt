Friday, May 19, 1916, Hotel Bertolini, Naples
G[eoffrey] S[cott] El[isabetta] Henraux Placci
Saw Gaeta, and went, with Placci’s Military Pass, to the top of the Fortress to see the Roman Tomb which is now a semaphore. We lunched in the grove, looking at the blue sea thro’ the tree stones. When we left a military car pursued us, and there was a great to-do over our having gone up to the fortress. However, it got settled. 
The road near Naples was  too  awful! But we got here __ G. and Geoffrey and Elizabetta arrived soon after.
