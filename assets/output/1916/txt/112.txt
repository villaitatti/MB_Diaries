Tuesday, May 2, 1916, I Tatti
G[eoffrey] S[cott] Mrs. Garrett W[alter] Berry [Leon] Bakst
Rainy then fine
Our guests went to town in the morning. In the afternoon we walked, after the rain had cleared up. 
In the evening M me  Henraux and her sister Jeanne Piccolelli came and danced the Tarantella in Neapolitan costume. The Marainis and Prince Paul were here. It was pleasant.
