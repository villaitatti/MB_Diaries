Friday, July 28, 1916, I Tatti
E[ugénie] S[trong]
Shopped with Eugénie, an awful business, as she is so undecided about everything.
G[eoffrey] had Marcella to dinner and rather raged at her for being neither a girl nor a woman. She is teasing, but cannot somehow really see it. I think she is a little cracked.
B.B. stayed in bed all day.
Eugenie and I called on Mrs. Ross.
