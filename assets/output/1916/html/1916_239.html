Sunday, October 1, 1916, I Tatti

N[aima] L[öfroth]

Fine and then a little rain

Got on well with Index of Vol. III of “Study and Criticism”.

We all 3 went to have tea at the Villa Pazzi with Mr. Eyre and Violaand her husband and took the Arbuthouts home.

Reading George Moore’s “The Brook Kerith” which is perfectly delightful.
