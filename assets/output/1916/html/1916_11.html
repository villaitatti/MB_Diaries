Wednesday, January 5, 1916, I Tatti

Julia, G[eoffrey] S[cott] C[ecil] P[insent]

Misty

(3 lunch 9 dinner)

The boys gave a children’s party to which also Naima and Lina went, butI didn’t. I had a walkalone, trying to composemy mind and soul, and B.B. had a walk with Sybil.If one’s feelingscould only harmonize with one’s judgment.

Capt. Perry came to tea, and Mr. and Mrs. Trench, Mr. Dumonts,the boys, and Lina to dinner.

Geoffrey stayedand talked and talkedtill nearly2. Perhaps it helps him to see clearly, but that does have solittle influence on actions and feelings!
