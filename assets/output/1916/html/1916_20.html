Friday, January 14, I Tatti

Glorious. Warm

Julia, Geoffrey Scott, Cecil Pinsent

(4 lunch 5 dinner)

Prepared villinofor Geoffrey’s pass:the scarlet fever. Lina came along and Gordon climbed on the roof with Julia.

Had a long and glorious walk with B.B. over the Scavito the very top and backby Fiesole. He went to see Sybil and I was picked up by Lady Enniskillenwho brought me home. Sybil was still going on about not being self-absorbed, her very tenacity and volubilityprovingshe was!

Geoffrey had a walk with Byba, who came in to tea and discussed common acquaintances. He had a letter from Marcella Michela and wrote use!
