Friday, February 1, 1916, Ray’s, London

Last furious fight at Dressmakers’. The jacketwas so awful that they saw it would not do, although they “couldn’t understandit - such a thing had never happened before.”

Alys had a farewell tea, but I felt very ill and had to go to a Dr. (MacNaughton 33 upper Belgrave St.) who syringed my ear and gave me things for my neck and shoulder. I saw however Jo Robinson and Louise Kinsellaand the Whitalls. Barbara (the angel) was there. Emily and Ray and I dined, and A. had the inspiration of giving us some warmed-over mutton(horrible)as her farewell feast. The food is the worst any of us encounteredanywhere. She is tooeconomical.

Called on Lawsons, who, as usual, are at their last penny.She gets £__ a week as head of

the Nurses’ hostel.
