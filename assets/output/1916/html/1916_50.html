Saturday, February 12, 1916, Ray’s, London

Alys went off at 8:35 from Euston.She is going to N.Y. second class on a second-class steamer!

Eliza and I had a last row at the dressmaker’sover the blusewhich cost £2.2 and is so badly made that I can hardly wear it. Lunched with Karin and said goodbye.

Called on Mrs. Strong. Went with Ray to see her Exhibition of Vanessa’s pictures, quite horrible. The MacLaganswere there, full of disgust.
