Sunday, January 9, 1916, I Tatti

Julia G[eoffrey] S[cott] C[ecil] P[insent] N[aima] L[öfroth]

Fine and very warm

(8 lunch, 6 dinner)

Went on correcting B.B.’s dull and formlessand slovenly articles.I hate it worse than anything; his writing is a real insult.They are much worse than his letters. But all cloudsrolled away (not war cloud) by a glorious walk which he and I and Naima andGeoffrey took to Bagazzano, under a crystal clear sky, with a bracing wind. Geoffrey talked about Marcella all the way. It is some consolation in their many absences, for she is by no means free to go to see him even on her non-hospital days.The endless things he __ to say and I to listen. For it interests me, and I want him so to be happy.

Mr. and Mrs. Dumonts and Mr. Mitchell came to call. The latter is like a plump wax figure, inside and out.
