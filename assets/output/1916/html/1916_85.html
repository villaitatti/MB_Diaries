Wednesday, March 22, 1916, I Tatti

G.S:

Took Mrs. Ross and Lina to lunch with the Dowdeswells.Ghastly.

Called on Mr. Dumont. Walked with BB, who dined with Sybil. Geoffrey came very late from one of those earth shaking interviewswith Marcella which change everything and put things on an entirely new footing - au fond, changing nothing, but precipitating in downthe inevitable path

-meanwhile, and by the way, making you an impossible memberof ordinary society.
