Tuesday, March 21, 1916, I Tatti

G[eoffrey] S[cott]

G[eoffrey] woke up cured (he thinks!). Speriamo. But I don’t for oneinstantthink it.

We three walked round the top of MonteCeceri, meeting dull Mr. Hultonon the way. Called on Sybil and Geoffrey stayed to dine.

Mme. Henraux dined here. I got an impression of her being rather heavy and less spirituelle than I had thought.
