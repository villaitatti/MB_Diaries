Monday, January 17, 1916, I Tatti

Julia, Geoffey Cecil

Grey

(5 lunch 9 dinner)

Nails and dressoccupied Julia’s morning, while I interviewed cooks, etc.

B.B. and I walked from the Tree to Fiesole.

Prince Paul, MissHulton, Aunt Janet, Lina, and the boys dined here. Aunt Janetbrought her photograph albumand the Prince was much interested to meeta person who had known Thacheray and Dickens and Meredith and Kinglake, and de Lesseps and Ferdinaso (as they call him) of Bulgaria.
