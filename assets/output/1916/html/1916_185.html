Sunday, August 5, Villa Bice, Massa di Marina [sic]

G[eoffrey] S[cott] Sybil [Cutting] Iris ourselves

Started at 7.30 and got here in the car at 12. Found Sybil on the beach - and our lazy life began. All morning in bathing and perhaps bathing again! Sybil is wonderfully better.

I fear I’ve caught a cold.
