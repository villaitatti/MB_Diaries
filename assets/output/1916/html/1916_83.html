Monday, March 20, 1916, I Tatti

G[eoffrey] S[cott]

Grey

Ray telegraphed that her operation is postponed. Though she will have another baby without it. Her perfect nurse-housekeeper, Florence, is leaving her, on account of ill-health. Geoffrey still very much __though he tries to pretend he is not. I foresee great trouble for him unless he recovers.

Miss Flint, a teacher of dancing, and Mrs. Krayl called.

Miss Hulton and her young male Gino Villari dined here. She played, and the music upset G[eoffrey] as it always does people in love
