Wednesday, January 12, 1916, I Tatti

Julia, Geoffrey

Splendid weather

(5 lunch, 3 dinner)

No cook but Amerigomanaged somehow. I went in to the Carrels, takingMrs. Ross to call on Miss Murray, and we then called for Miss Prestleywhom I brought back to lunch. We had a glorious walk over the downfor S. Clemente. Geoffrey had justheardthat Marcella Michela has scarlet fever.He is sure to have caught it. It is a great disappointment. He called there and left flowersand a note,and then went to the Casa di Cura.
