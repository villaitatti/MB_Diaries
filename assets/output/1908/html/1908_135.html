Wednesday, Apr. 22, 1908

Cold but charming. 

Perkins.

Mrs. Flower, Benn and Corwegh came to lunch. Dull. Miss Alexander and I called on Mrs. Stuart and her father, Mr. MacMillan at Rovezzano.Placci was here when I returned, making plans for the Sicilian trip.The Lawsons called. B.B. and I dined at Aunt Janet’s with the Markbys.

Karin’s Ball was tonight. I hope it went off well, and that she could hear.
