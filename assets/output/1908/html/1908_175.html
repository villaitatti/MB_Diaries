Monday, June 1, 1908

Motor Trip. 

Albergo Sirino, Lagonegro.

Fine.

Left at 8.30. Our first stop was Terranova. From S. Francesco the ***view over the plain of Sibari watered by the Coscile and the Crati was too beautiful to be believed — quiet, classic, perfect — worth all the journey. Placci alas did his best to spoil it, fighting with B.B. who was admirable. It ended up with Placci’s saying very rudely, “Tu es le maître de l’auto: tu vas ou tu veux, je dois être très reconnaissant que tu mi permets de t’accompagner” — the sort of thing no well-bred person could say. Lucien grew as black as thunder. 

We stopped a moment at Spezzano Albanese, but the language and costume was better at *Lungro. Then we came through Castelvillari over the *pass to Lagonegro, a beautiful road, beautiful!
