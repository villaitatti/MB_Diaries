Friday, Oct. 30, 1908

Boston.

Dorchester

Rain. Colder.

I am fighting a cold. Bernhard went to the Art Museum with Isabella Gardner and gave her lunch at the Somerset.

Miss Hal, Mrs. Hooker and Miss Whitney came to tea. They had all aged fearfully in these 5 critical years. I daresay they were thinking the same of me!

Reading Anatole France’s latest, L’Ile des pingouins.
