Wednesday, June 24, 1908

I Tatti.

Misty. Scirocco.

Last things.

Called on Mrs. Ross and said goodbye. The Princess and Miss Blood came in their motor and took us down to see the Cupola illuminated in honour of S. Giovanni. It was wonderfully beautiful, looking surprisingly transparent, like a jewel, flaming with thousands of flickering little oil lamps. They threaten electricity for next year, but that will destroy this weird glimmering, glow-worm effect.
