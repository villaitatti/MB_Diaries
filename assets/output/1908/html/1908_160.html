Sunday, May 17, 1908 

Albergo Bixio, Castelvetrano.

Very hot.

Started at 8.30 and saw Sciacca, with a very interesting Madonna by Laurana, very wicked looking, and some Gaginis. Got to Selinunte for tea, and had a gorgeous sunset and view. I felt rather sick, and must have had a coup de soleil, for it was frightfully hot, and we did not lunch till 2.
