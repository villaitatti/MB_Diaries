Thursday, Mar. 12, 1908

Fine.

Logan.

I put that picture of dear Alberto in yesterday. He came up at the same time with Miss Kelly and took us all to the Gamberaia, where we saw the most glorious storm. Afterwards it was almost too beautiful. Alberto enjoyed it à la limite de dilatation.Miss Kelly stayed on to dine, and we all liked her awfully.
