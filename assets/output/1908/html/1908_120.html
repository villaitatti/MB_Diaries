Tuesday, Apr. 7, 1908

I Tatti. 

Scott.

We reached Milan at 7.20. I was frightfully unwell and did not know how to get on, but somehow pulled through. We went for half an hour to the Duomo. We both slept most of the way to Pistoia. Crossed the Appenines in the rain, but the sun came out for our arrival. 

Bernhard was at his Committee, and did not get back till after Algar had arrived. We had amusing talk at dinner. Algar is awfully keen on the Elixir “El Zair”.
