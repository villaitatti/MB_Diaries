Sunday, Apr. 26, 1908 

Windy. Cold. Clear.

Somehow did nothing. Took Britten to Gamberaia whe

re I had a most dull conversation with Mrs. Acton. 

The Serristori, Rembielinski, Baron Stumm, Mme. de Cossé-Brissac, her mother and Schlumberger called here.

Went with Britten to this concert. Alberto played divinely. Pugno is splendid.
