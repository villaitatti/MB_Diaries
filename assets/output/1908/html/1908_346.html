Thursday, Nov. 19, 1908

Somerset Hotel, Boston.

Came up from Newport and paid innumerable calls. Bernhard lunched with Clayton Johns. We met at Miss Hopkinson’s at tea, and she gave us a reception. We dined with those delicious people, the Crams. She is so pretty and charming. He is what they call in England “quite mad”, a frightful High Churchman and wild gothic enthusiast and Renaissance hater. It was great fun.

Albert wrote saying he would not go to Oxford.
