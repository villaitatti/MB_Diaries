Monday, Nov. 23, 1908

Called to see a Gagini. Lunched with Mrs. George Gardner and Isabella. I spoke of Henry Adams’ “Education” which William James lent me, and said how interesting it was, but that I didn’t dare to tell the name of the lender. Isabella promptly said, “I keep the copy he gave me under lock and key. No one even gets a glimpse of it.” We all knew, somehow that she hadn’t a copy!

Tea with Joseph Breck, and then I went on and had tea with Mr. Ole Bull and met Miss Alice Longfellow.
