Tuesday, Nov. 3, 1908

Somerset Hotel, Boston.

Jessie (Morse) Berenson took me to a concert where I heard Debussy. Jardin or something on the piano and liked it very much. We lunched with her and her husband at the Touraine. 

Bernhard pain some calls and I had my hair treated by Gersumky, who promises to make it grow again as of yore. These promises to aging parties!!
