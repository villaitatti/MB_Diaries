Sunday, Dec. 27, 1908 

New Willard, Washington.

Corrected proof. Mrs. Converse and Mrs. Harlow called in a motor and took us to the Chevy Chase Country Club to lunch, quite a party — a nice Miss Parker who studies painting in Boston, and Mr. Huntington Jackson engaged to Miss Converse, who drove me home through the pretty park. He spoke of a house we passed as “an odd line of architecture”, and of an apartment house so flimsily built that you could hear the man in the next flat change his mind! 

Bernhard and I called on Mrs. Slater, who wasn’t receiving (though she had asked us!), and walked on to see the Perry Belmont monstrous mansion. 

Dined at Mr. Parson’s, a most awfully pleasant dinner, though we didn’t like her. Mr. and Mrs. and Miss Aldis from Chicago were charming, and Mr. Putnam of the Congressional Library came in afterwards. 

I forgot to take off my overshoes!
