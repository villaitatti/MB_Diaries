Wednesday, Sept. 23, 1908

New York.

Ray, Karin, Ellie Rendel.Packed. Lunched with Mrs. Gardner who told us that Mrs. Chadbourne had let her things in without telling her as a pleasant surprise. It was Mr. Chadburne who told, wishing to make it unpleasant for his wife. Mrs. Gardner had to pay $71,000 fine, and her things are confiscated. She is liable to $150,000 more and imprisonment! 

We came by the 4 train here and kind Mr. Cannon was waiting on the platform to drive us to the hotel. He came in and paid a long call, when we refused to go to supper.
