Monday, Jan. 20, 1908

Sunday, Jan. 19, 1908

Tatti. 

Cold mist.

Agnes came to lunch. Alberto and his cousin came at 4 to look at photos, bringing a still more gushing American lady, miss Pomeroy.

Mather came to say goodbye before going to Sicily. He is thinking of taking the curatorship of the Worcester Art Museum.

I felt awfully ill with a cold coming. 

Mr. Cannon wrote that if we had left Florence, he would have sold La Doccia! He must care a good deal. How sad and funny that he can give no sign when one sees him!
