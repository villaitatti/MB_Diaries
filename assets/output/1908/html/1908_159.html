Saturday, May 16, 1908

Motoring Tour.

Scirocco.

Saw Girgenti in morning.

A little boy with beautiful eyes, who winked at the men in a very provocative way, followed us everywhere. We called him “Pasqualuccio” after Augustus Hare’s little shepherd boy at the Temples. Hare was what Placci calls an “Archi-buggar”. 

Went to the Temples after tea. The scirocco suddenly cleared away. The moonrise was most beautiful.
