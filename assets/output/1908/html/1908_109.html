Friday, Mar. 27, 1908

London.

Went up to town with Ray. Shopped, saw her off to Rickettswood. 

Scott and I called on Rothersteins. They have a really lovely house. Mrs. R. has just had her fourth baby.Scott and I dined at Pagani’s and talked at my flat till nearly midnight. He hopes his parents will permit him to go to Italy to travel with Cannon’s son.
