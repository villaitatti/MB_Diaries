Monday, Dec. 28, 1908

New Willard, Washington.

A most lovely day.

Called on Mrs. Rae and saw her pictures. Then met Mr. Barrett and the Aldises at the Corcoran Gallery and railed against the pictures. Mr. Barrett took us to see Mr. Ralph Johnson’s house, and had an assonant lunch at the de Martels!

Paid various calls in the afternoon, and dined with the Lees, charming Catholic people. I sat by nice Dr. Lee. Mrs. Randolph was there, who played the piano, Chopin of course. It is a crime.
