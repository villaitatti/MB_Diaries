Tuesday, Sept. 22, 1908

Vendome, Boston.

Ray, Karin, Ellie Rendel.

Went out to see Harvard and Rachel Perry, nice creature.

Had our hair washed by Gersumsky (16 S. Fremont St.) Then Bernhard’s brother motored us out to Dorchester for dinner. Awful. Mrs. Berenson drives me crazy with her constant petty emotional appeal.
