the grateful nation had voted Garibaldi a million francs and a life pension of 50.000 to be continued to his widow, so La Raimondi was loath to relinquish it. It was Fazzari who got up all the facts, seeking out her former maid etc etc until he got together the complete disgraceful story of her youth. He had 2 copies made and took one to her best friend a lawyer, asking him to read it, talk with her and let him know her decision. This was made so quickly that he had not yet got up the next day when the lawyer came and said she agreed and so exit La Raimondi.
