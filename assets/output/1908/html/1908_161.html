Monday, May 18, 1908

Grand Hotel, Trapani.

Cooler.

Got to Mazzara and saw a most amusing church heavily decorated with reliefs. A small boy in the crowd had an absolutely Greek profile, with those eyes set in that marvellous way. It was too wonderful!We had lunch at Marsala and saw the town and the harbour. Some tombs by Dom. Gagini. Then we went to Salemi and saw a very romantic St. Julian by *Dom. Gagini — a fascinating and romantic figure. 

The road from Salemi to Calatafimi was delicious — actually shaded in parts, a thing extremely rare in Sicily. 

Tea and sunset at Segesta ——!

The Garibaldi monument looks exactly like the remains of some cult of Phallic Worship.
