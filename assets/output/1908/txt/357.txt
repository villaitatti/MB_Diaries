Monday, Nov. 30, 1908
The Webster, New York.
Got to town about 2.30. 
We dined at Bessie’s and Elsie’s, a largish party, Mrs. Dana Gibson, Dr. Elliott Gregory, Mr. Kent,  Cotney , Mr. Frank Martin, Miss Pierpont Morgan, and Mrs Wolcott beside whom B.B. was placed to his infinite  despair . We then went on to see “The Blue Mouse” a play adapted by Clyde Fitch, and so fearfully vulgar that we got home in absolute despair and gloom.
