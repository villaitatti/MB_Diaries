Tuesday, May 5, 1908
La Floridiana.
Fine. 
Castel Maurigi came to “see Churches”, i.e., to sit in them holding Ethel’s hand, while Bernhard and I took notes on the pictures. We saw Santa Maria Nuova, the Incoronata, S. Pietro Martire, S. Marcellino (fine cloister), and SS. Severino e Sosio.
The Princess Candriano came to call, very pretty and amusing, looking much younger since the separation from her  husband . Then Ethel and B.B. and I motored up to Camaldoli and walked down by a beautiful path through the woods. Ethel said she was “studying” how to have another baby (which is what she cares for almost as much as having lovers). Unfortunately her husband, in waiving a divorce for a separation, put in the clause that there were to be no more children. It seems a pity, as she is so healthy and rich and so longs to have them!
