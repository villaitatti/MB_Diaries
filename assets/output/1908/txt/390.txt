Scheming by Menotti and Teresita, children of his first wife Anita, who wanted the noble alliance. They gave Battestina money and sent her back from Caprera to Nice, and told their father she had run off with a falegname. G. much upset at this betrayal and persuaded in his discouragement to marry Giuseppina Raimondi.La Raimondi, on her part, was induced to marry him by waking up with a fever which the doctor told her was smallpox. Caroli had been with her the night before, and she argued that he would catch it and die, or she would be so horribly disfigured that he would never marry her, so she got up and married Garibaldi in the private chapel of the villa, and went back to bed again with a high fever which however was not smallpox. G. on his side returned to bed as he had hurt his knee riding the day before. A few hours later one of his lieutenants, Torelli, come in and when G. told him he was married to Giuseppina.
