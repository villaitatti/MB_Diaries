Thursday, Sept. 24, 1908
New York.
Called on  Florence . Grace and Mary came to  lunch . Shopped and called on  Putnam’s . 
Went to Grace’s to tea to see Sarah Harlan and Bessie Taylor but was awfully sea- sick .However, I was better by 7 and we all were taken by Cannon to dine at Delmonico’s. Music but very good strange food: clams, chicken gumbo, bass, broiled turkey, ice  cream . He came in and stayed, till we were nearly  dead .
