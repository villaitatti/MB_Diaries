Saturday, Oct. 17, 1908
Buffalo.
Fine.
Saw over Larkin’s Soap Factory, where remarkable provisions are made to ensure the comfort and improvement of the employees. They have an Educator, a Systematizer, a Welfare-Worker, a Y.M.C.A. lady, a nurse, etc. It was weird to see  sons  of girls with tubes in their ears type-writing from distant phonographs, which they turn on slowly.
Lunched at the XIX Century Club with College representatives. Went to Art Museum and to tea with Mrs. Norton.
College meeting in evening at which Ray spoke. Karin and I were amused at the comments — “She looks so sweet, it doesn’t matter what she says.” “No  wonder  the Oxford and Cambridge undergraduates form suffrage societies” etc. Ray would have been  furious . She spoke fairly well, and had a great ovation, being forced to get up and  bow . Carey was pleased.  Her  speech was really very impressive.
