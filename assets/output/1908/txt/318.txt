Thursday, Oct. 22, 1908
New York.
Grace’s.
Warm. Fine.
Wrote letters. Packed. Telephoned. Lunched with  Grace . Went to Bank in Wall  St . Shopped at  Altman’s . Grace and I went out to dine with Mr. Halsey, one of the Directors of the Stock Exchange, and owner of a marvellous collection of early American furniture, silver, china, etc. He was very dull. If it had not been for Bryson I should have cried with ennui. Bryson is deliciously subtle and  sympathetic . Cannon was there, affectionate and platitudinous. He looked very ill. It was his first outing. 
I had just sent off a long letter to Edith telling her to meet Lucy’s charm with greater charm, etc. I wondered how much Bryson thought I knew.
Mounteney Jephson died.
