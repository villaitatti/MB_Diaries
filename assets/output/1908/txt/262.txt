Thursday, Aug. 27, 1908
Gave dinner to Kelly, Tom Spring-Rice, Scott and Bobby Trevy at Dieudonné’s, and we all went in Kelly’s motor to Queen’s Hall to hear “ E .  Flat  major ” Symphony (Balfour Gardiner). It was alas boring, but the Debussy L’Après midi d’un Faune was quite enchanting. [https://www.bbc.co.uk/events/e6b4wh]
