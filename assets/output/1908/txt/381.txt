Thursday, Dec. 24, 1908
New Willard, Washington.
Corrected  proof . Lunched with Henry Adams. Had a pleasant, pessimmistic talk.
Called in afternoon on Mrs. Stickney, the Lodges, Mrs. Slater and Comtesse de Martel of the French  Embassy . Miss Lea called. 
Arthur and Jessie Berenson are here, and we dined with them. He always says the wrong thing. They are both hopeless.
