Monday, Nov. 16, 1908
Boston.
Bernhard lunched with Judge Grant at the Tavern  Club . I lunched with Mrs. Hooper at the James’. Mrs. James was disgustingly bitter and venomous about Alfred Hodder and Mary Gwinn. I do think her attitude is  inexcusable . 
We dined with Arthur Berenson’s family in law, the Morses, terrible anthropological people, who think of money and eating and the Family.
