Sunday, June 7, 1908 
La Floridiana, Naples.
Scirocco.
Dreadful day.
Stayed in bed till 12. 
Castel Maurigi came to lunch, and he and Ethel had the afternoon to themselves till 5. 
Mrs. Daniel says she has broken her father all up by taking this new lover, who is a handsome, insignificant youth. The Duca d’Aosta of course rather added lustre, and Finzi, being a distinguished man, was borne, but no one will stand this boy, and she has been cut by tout Naples. She is furious and rages and waps, but won’t change her behaviour. Major Davis is compounded of pride, and feels the slights keenly. Seen closely, it is a wretched household. All the servants are frightfully discontented too, and have come begging us to get them other situations. So much for my fast perishing ideal of “living for pleasure”!
