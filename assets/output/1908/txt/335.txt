Sunday, Nov. 8, 1908 
Hotel Somerset, Boston.
We lunched with Miss Robins, Santayana and a Miss Hartshorn[e]. Santayana was delightful — a most amusing and sympathetic man to talk to. B.B. was fearfully bored by Miss R. because he could overhear scraps of our fascinating talk.
The Nelson Gays of Rome  called . Bernhard dined at Dorchester and I grappled with my speech.
