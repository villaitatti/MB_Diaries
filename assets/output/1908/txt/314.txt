Sunday, Oct. 18, 1908 
Glorious.
Train from Buffalo to New York, 9.21 p.m.
We spent the day at Niagara, where the kind Spragues took the whole lot of us, Mary and Ray and Ellie as well as Grace and Karin and me, in two  motors . Ellie informed us on the way that if there was ‘anything she loathed it was Women’s  Congresses .’ Ray said that the Rev. Anna Shaw had asked them to go West with her and see the women vote in Colorado. I encouraged her to accept, and so did Carey. Ellie decided to give up her scholarship and go with her.
