Friday, June 12, 1908
I Tatti. 
Fine.
Massage. 
Did Naples notes — very  complicated . Slept after  lunch . Cannon called to say goodbye — very  dull . Dined at Gamberaia with the Countess Serristori and Placci. It was wonderfully beautiful, but we all seemed to me very banal.
