Sunday, Apr. 5, 1908 
Paris with Scott.
Went to the Louvre. Met Hamilton Field there. He had crossed with the Cannons, and liked Harry. Scott’s spirits  rose. I lunched  with the Reinachs, who spent the time quarrelling about the telephone. 
Scott came at 2 and Reinach took us to see an unimportant private collection, and then to  Les Indépendants . What horrors!  Then , after a turn in the Bois, we called on the Fields, and then went to St. Cloud and had dinner with Neith and Hutch. Boyce was very sweet. Nothing very interesting was said. Got home at 11, rather tired. I felt very ill.
