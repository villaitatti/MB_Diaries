Tuesday, July 14, 1908
Members’ Mansions <London>
Cool. Rain.
Shopped and shopped and shopped with Mrs.  Stewart . Bernhard took Robert de Rothschild to the National Gallery with Holroyd, and lunched with Mrs. Leslie.
We dined with the Rothensteins and a Mr. and Mrs. Bell, young and nice, he paradoxical, and she earnest and good — a daughter of Leslie Stephen.
