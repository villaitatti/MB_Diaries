All Saints, Sunday, Nov. 1, 1908 
Somerset Hotel, Boston.
Fine. Cold.
I have a bad cold and so stayed in all  day . Mrs. Benson called and Joseph Breck and Mr. and Mrs. Benjamin Ives Gilman.
Bernhard went with Isabella to the High Mass in the cast-iron Gothic Cathedral, lunched with her and saw the procession of 45,000 black-coated Catholic men walk by. I saw them too, from my window. What a leaven for this “new” country, all that mass of old superstition and  usage ! He called on the Wendells.
