Saturday, Jan. 9, 1909

Warmer.

Bernhard went to see Glaenzer.

We lunched together and then he went off to Barrytown-on-Hudson, to the Chapmans’, and I worked on my lecture.

Mr. Rutgers Marshall (“Pain, Pleasure and Aesthetics”) called, and Mr. and Mrs. Eno, whom I really like.

I dined with Grace and Bond and Frank.

Sunday, Jan. 10, 1909

Warm.

Throat bad, so went to Dr. Rice. Lunched at Elsie’s with Mr. and Mrs. Ogden Codman (very nice), and gave my lecture at the Club to a very fashionable and friendly audience. I was introduced by Mr. Blackfield. It went off all right, and they all came up to say it was “the best lecture the’d ever had at the Club” and so on.
