<Hotel Webster, New York> Monday, Jan. 11, 1909
Bernhard came back, and we lunched here.Mrs. Gardner called, Mrs. Flagler, Mrs. Taylor and various people.
B.B. called on Mrs. Latham.We dined with the Blumenthals, who had Glaenzer, Judge Cohen, Mr. and Mrs. McClellan (Mayor) to meet us. I had had my nose operated on earlier in the day, and it was still bleeding, which was a bore!
The dinner was most sumptuous, and B.B. ate a little piece of canvas back duck (“canard daubé de toile”, as Miss Elkins told the Duca degli Abruzzi) that simply did for him. He was ill all night.
