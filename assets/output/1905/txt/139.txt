Thursday, Dec. 14, 1905
Started for London at 2.35.
Bernhard had a walk with Fabbri, and tea with Carlo Placci who was “full of admiration of his own happiness and all the wonderful privileges he enjoys in life.”
Friday, Dec. 15, 1905 Algar came to stay with Bernhard.
Spent night at Paris. Dined with Willy.
Florence: Mme. d’Orsay and Rizi Visconti lunched with Bernhard. Lestrange came to tea.
Wonderful day. “…  pour  entre les jours”.
Bernhard went to the Bagazzano [Baggazano] with Fabbri and Miss Blood and Princess Ghika.
Saturday, Dec. 16, 1905 Reached London 7.5. Ray, Karin and Mother in flat. Florence: Miss Blood came to tea.
Sunday, Dec. 17, 1905 Washed Karin’s hair. Took her to nursing home. Florence: Bernhard and Algar called on Miss Blood.
