 January 1905  Thursday 19
W rote home, Robertson, Lillian Prussing, Mrs. Earl, for  P ermessi , Logan, Alys.
Went with Mr. Fox to antiquity shops. Very little of interest. After lunch went with him & B.B. to wander in the delicious Boboli Gardens, and then to call on Labouchère ,  who was in great vein.
January 1905  Friday 20
Took Mr. Fox to La Doccia.
B im boni came and played Bach, Little C minor passacaglia and 2nd C major for clavichord. Present: Fox, Horne, Gronau, Cohen,  Hearn , Harwood.
January 1905  Saturday 21 
Sorted all our letters, a great relief.
Drove to Careggi and then to call on Actons.
January 1905  Sunday 22
B.B. began his first chapter of the “ North Italian Painters . ”
Calderoni came to lunch. Afterwards the Actons, Placci, and the 
Contessa Rasponi to call. The two latter walked with us to the Gamberaia which was looking beautiful.
