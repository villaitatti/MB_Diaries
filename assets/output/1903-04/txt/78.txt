Mrs. Longyear said she would “treat” Bernhard, and he thought he would let her, so as to see what it was all about. 
He came home and dressed, and then went with Barrett Wendell to the “Wednesday Evening Club” (founded 1776) which met this time at the house of Mr. Rhodes the historian. He had, as they say, “the time of his life”, with dozen interesting, interested men. It is the women, he says, who spoil American society.
Thursday Nov. 19. 1903. Hotel Somerset Boston
Bernhard spent the morning at the dentist’s, and in spite of Mrs. Longyear’s absent treatment suffered agonies from the killing of a nerve. I went with Miss Puffer to Wellesley and gave a lecture to the girls. It went off better than at Smith, but was substantially the same lecture. They seemed very enthusiastic.
