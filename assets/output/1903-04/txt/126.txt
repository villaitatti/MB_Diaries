The other guest of honour was Miss Carlyle, an English actress playing in “Admirable Creighton”. Bliss Carman came, and Mancuse Conway, the Alexanders, Truches, Mrs. Jacacci, Norman, and so forth. In the evening we dined with the Kenyon Coxes, Mr. and Miss Isham and Mr. Garrison being also there. Mrs. Kenyon Cox seems a decent sort, but loud and boisterous and not lady like very.
