Thursday Dec. 3. 03
The day was spent mostly in packing, though B.B. went to Cambridge and met Wendell and Royce and called on Miss Norton. We dined in the evening with the Sam Warrens, a Mr. and Mrs. Coolidge, Templeman Coolidge, the Robinsons, Denman Ross and Pritchard being the other guest. I had a most delightful talk with Mr. Ross at dinner, and a pleasant one with Mr. Robinson afterwards. It was one of our most agreeable dinners. Mr. Robinson said they were “considering” the Perugino. Speriamo!
