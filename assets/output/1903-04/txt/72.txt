From 2.30 to 4.30 we were at Fenway Court. Mrs. Gardner revived the Powell- Carew Holbein dispute, but in vain. It was only galling. The light was bad, so we could not see the pictures very well.
Then she drove us to a small Tea Clayton Johns gave us. Mr. Swift came, Mr. and Mrs. Adamowski, and Mr. William Blake, Gladys’ trustee. He says they are almost ruined, owing to Mrs. Baldwin’s carelessness and recklessness, and that Gladys’ private fortune, instead of being £1,500 as she and her mother always told us, is scarcely a quarter of that!
