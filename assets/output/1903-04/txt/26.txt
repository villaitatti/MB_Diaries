In the evening we went to see Owen Wister’s play – “The Virginian,” and were greatly taken with the actor, Dunstan Farnum, who is a great grandson of Webster. He spent two weeks in Virginia learning the accent. But the play of
course  isn’t  a play; it’s merely a novel dramatized, with all the emotional parts underlined. No plot, but a story.
Thursday Oct. 22. 1903. Green Hill, Brookline.
Another elysian day of sparkling sunshine. We went to see an exhibition of Japanese things, which are to be sold at auction, and meet Mr. Ross (Denman) there. The things were good, but chiefly grotesques and copies of “nature”, and a little seemed to go a long way.
