Sunday Nov. 8. 1903. Hotel Somerset Boston
We went to the Japanese wood-carving show, and met Joseph Smith and brought him back to lunch. He seemed nice and congenial in his ideas, but not amounting to much. Miss Puffer came to tea, Mrs. Gardner called, Rachel came to dine and Ralph Perry afterwards. We argued about Roger Fry’s father making him promise never to paint from the nude. Perry proved very dull and un-agile and set in his notions, utterly unable to handle a subject intellectually. He is pretty tough, and we were bored.
