Mr. Fenimore Cooper, grandson of the novelist. A splendid sleigh was waiting, and we drove to Mr. Cooper’s to see a “Holbein” (I don’t think it was one), and then to Mr. Thacker’s for lunch. In the afternoon I gave my lecture and had a “tea”, and then a very interesting dinner at the Richmonds. Mr. and Mrs. Parsons were the other guests - great friends of the President’s. She is the head of the State Education Bureau.
Here in Albany, for the first time, I have “touched America”. Their heartiness and friendliness is beyond description!
