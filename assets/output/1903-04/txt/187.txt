Thursday Feb. 25. 04.
Whistler again, and dear Mr. Freer. Mr. Fox came to lunch, and took us to call on a jolly sick widow, Mrs. Nickerson. Then he carried B.B. off to see Dr.
Langmaid, who discovered enlarged turbindles in the poor man’s nose. We dined with Mrs. Whitman. I sat by the nearly inarticulate Mr. Dove.
Friday Feb. 26. 04 
Bernhard in bed. I went to museum and argued with that silly Potter about that obviously school picture called “Veronese”.
