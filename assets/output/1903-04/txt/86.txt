Monday Nov. 23. 1903. Hotel Somerset Boston 
Mrs. Tyler and Mr. Fox, the architect, came for us at 10.30 to take us a drive among the newly build houses (some of them extremely good) in the suburbs. We ended up with the Larz Anderson’s place - a home filled with odds and ends, the rubbish from lots of curiosity shops. Mr. Anderson confessed he had no taste, but said he hoped to acquire it by indiscriminate buying!
B.B. lunched with Clayton Johns at the Tavern Club. I called on Mrs. Peabody and Mrs. Kahn and joined B.B. at Mrs. Perry’s reception  -  rather deadly. We dined with Mrs. Morss, a pretty silly woman. Interesting was Mr. I. Mackintosh. Bell, a young man who has travelled a great deal in British Columbia and in particular explored the region round Hudson’s Bay, discovering new tribes of Indians and Esquimaux .
