Tuesday Feb. 2. 04. Bryn Mawr 
Lunched with Aunty Lill. Reception at Coates’ - not half bad. Good talk in evening.
Wednesday Feb. 3. 04. 
Horace Eaton came down for the night delightful as ever. Talk on aesthetics. Carey took us over Rockefeller.
Thursday Feb. 4. 04.
Talk with  with Eaton - showed him College. Lunched with John Thomas. Afternoon tea here, “Readers”. Dined in Pembroke Hall.
Friday Feb. 5. 04. 
Alumnae lunch here - oh how depressing. Bernhard went to Johnson’s and lunched in town. Barrett Wendell lectured on “Puritanism”.
