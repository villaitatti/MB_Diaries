Then Miss Reed, one of Gertrude’s middle-aged, middle-class female friends, called with her sister Mrs. Morss, who used to be a very pretty girl in Bernhard’s youth, and who gave him a month of dreams. Alas, “Love likes not the falling fruit from the withered tree” - and rather the ripened fruit from the bulky tree. Then Mrs. Pringsheim called, and then, oh then, Mrs. Longyear, the millionaire Christian Scientist.
