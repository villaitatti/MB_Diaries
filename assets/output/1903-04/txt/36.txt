Saturday Oct. 31. 1903. Smith College
Miss Jordan and her friend Miss Bourland took us to Deerfield for lunch. A lovely sleepy place, with a quaint burial ground containing the bones of many who were “captivated by the salvages”, and of one “who married a salvage and became one”, returning home at last, it seems, to rest in a civilized grave. Senda and I came home early to see a Basket Ball game - fine sport. She is worried about Bessie and Bessie about her and both of them about Abe and all of them about the Mother and Father. I could not keep my spirits long with such life-diminishing people. The truth is Bernhard’s career has given them all a vision of life they cannot themselves attain; and the contrast makes them discontented. As life goes, they are really very well off.
