Wednesday Feb. 10. 04. New Willard Hotel Washington, D.C. 
Came here, reading in the papers of the courage and spirit with which the
people of Baltimore are meeting their terrific loss. Wandered disconsolately about,
feeling like Babes in the Wood. We thought the White House beautiful, but the Capitol disappointing when you came near. The proportions are bad, and the detail poor. As to the lamps in front, “they knock even German vulgarity into a cocked hat”, B.B. said.
Thursday Feb. 11. 04 .
A lovely day. Miss Hale came and we saw the Corcoran Gallery. Hiram Powers’ famous “Greek Slave” isn’t “half nasty”, really. The specimens of American art were interesting. Mr. and Mrs. Ross Perry, Mr. Archer, and Mr. Parmelu called, and we left cards on the Waterlows and Cotton Smiths.
