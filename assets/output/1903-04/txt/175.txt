I called on Miss Seward and Miss Upton, and then met B.B. at Miss Hale’s. Mr.
and Mrs. Gilman came, Mrs. Kennan, Mrs. Howe (the Cleveland Unitarian Minister, here at a Women’s Suffrage Congress) and Mrs. Cabot Lodge, and various others. Then we called on Secretary Hay, and came back to dine with Zorn, who is staying here. He is dull, my goodness! Poor B.B. was feeling really ill; he has taken a chill, I fear.
Sunday Feb. 14. 04 My Fortieth Birthday.
B.B. is better today. We went out to see Mr. Waggaman’s collection this morning
