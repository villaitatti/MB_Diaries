If they carry out their intention he will sell it at auction in New York, and so Washington will tax its best collection out of existence!
We came back, changed clothes, and then went to lunch at Senator Henry Cabot Lodge’s. Mrs. Adams and Miss Bigelow and Mr. and Mme Buena Varilla were there - he the deus ex machina of the whole Panama business. Mr. Lodge has a nice picture b y  Zaganelli, a Madonna enthroned with four saints, and a Cardinal by Dirck Bouts.
