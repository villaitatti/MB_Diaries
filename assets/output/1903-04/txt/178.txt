They all came, and after the lunch they asked M. Buena Varilla to make a quarter of an hour’s speech to tell them about Panama. He spoke 4 ½  hours, and they were all converted. This meant that they converted Hanna, who then “put the thing through” in the House, and started “the biggest business enterprize [ sic ] of this country”.
