Mr.
Story was as polite as pie to Bernhard, evidently regarding him as the future Director. Came home to a “tea” - Senda and Bessie, the Halls, Gans, Mr. Reynolds and Mr. Percy Griffin. Dined with the Steins, such well-meaning horrible Jews, and Gans took his cousin Mr. Sternberger and me to Zangwill’s play, “Merely Mary Ann.” It was very poor, I thought, just the Situation, and the characters running in the grooves cut for them by the situation. After the play, when I was dying to get home, Mr. Gans took us to Delmonico’s and spent $10.00 on making us eat and drink things none of us wanted. The spree must have cost him $25.00 at least, for he had a carriage. I hate that way of doing things.
