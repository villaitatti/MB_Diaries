Monday Dec. 28. 03
I lunched with Miss Isham, Mrs. Hewett and Mrs. Blashfield (a tenor) and was taken afterwards to see Bernard Shaw’s very interesting play, “Candida.” Bernhard had Norman Hapgood to lunch. In the evening we went out again to Brooklyn, to Mrs. Field’s.
Tuesday Dec. 29. 03
Ray and her friends are reading Mill’s Autobiography. It seems incredible. What  can  she think of it?!
