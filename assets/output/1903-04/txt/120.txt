Wednesday Dec. 16. 03. Plaza, New York.
Mrs. Bullard called, and Mrs. Davis, who used to be, in Italy, “the Young Lady”, now the mother of a family, and very boring - she always was - and boiled- eggish. We lunched with Rawlins Cottenett, who had Mrs. Sorchau and Mr. Robin Grey (bookseller) at Sherry’s.
