Tuesday Dec. 15. 1903. Plaza Hotel. New York
Came down in the train with Mr. Palmer, a painter of snow scenes. I got rather attached to the Albany people and felt sad to go away and never see them again. They begged us to come back. Reached home in time to go to take tea with Miss Davidge - not important.
