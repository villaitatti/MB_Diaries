Saturday Dec. 5. 03. Guernsey Hall, Princeton, N. J.

Saw the college in the morning, and got acquainted with the different styles of Mr. Potter the architect, each worse than the other! Walter Cope’s building, on the contrary, pleased us exceedingly. It is delicious weather, freezing, with snow on the ground, but somehow warm and inspiriting. Some of the art class came in the evening, and Bernhard “connoshed” Mrs. Marquand’s few pictures for their benefit. Mr. Marquand is really delightful. She is a niece of the Mr. Cross who married George Eliot.I have been resting myself by reading a lot of novels.
