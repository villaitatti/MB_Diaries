Mr. Strong, at his gorgeous home in Lakewood, a house whose supreme luxury was an automatic heating arrangement which kept all the rooms up to 70º. Santayana said he lay raging all night on the outside of his bed. Opening the window simply meant inviting an automatic rush of hot air into his room!

Monday Oct. 19. 1903 Green Hill Brookline

Walked and chatted in the morning and after lunch drove to Boston and saw the Library. The Puvis’ along the sides of the entrance hall are very fine - the large one around the door of Bates Hall less successful. The Abbeys were awful, but the Sargeantssurpassed for vulgarity and triviality and ugliness anything we ever saw! No modern Italian could be worse.
