Thursday Dec. 10. 03. Plaza Hotel. N. Y.

Bernhard startled me by asking whether he should let it be known that he would be willing to succeed Cesnolaas director of the Museum here. I saw a thousand advantages in it, but I do think it would be a waste of a man who can think. Today’s visit there confirmed me. It is a vast collection of horrors.
