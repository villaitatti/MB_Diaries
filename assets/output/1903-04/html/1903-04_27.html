We asked whom they would invite to make an interesting dinner here - William James, Mrs. Whitman, Mr. John Grey, Mrs. Bell, “Piggy” Everett,E. E. Hale … these are all the names I remember now.

Friday Oct. 23. 1903. Green Hill Brookline

Mrs. Gardner took us to Beverly Farmsto lunch with Mrs. Whitman, who had also Mrs. Parkman, Mr. Barton a young architect, and Miss Pendleton, a witty lady from Philadelphia.
