Tuesday Jan. 5. 04. Auditorium Annex. Chicago.

Bernhard went over to the Gallery and saw Mr. French. We lunched with Lillian and her husband at the Union League Club, and Lillian came back for the afternoon. Mrs. Hamill called. We had a pleasant dinner at the Herricks, Mr. and Mrs. Bartlett, Miss Day, Mr. Rhodes, the Lovetts. We liked Mr. Bartlett quite particularly.

Wednesday Jan. 6. 04

In the morning we went to see the collection of Mrs.Cyrus H. McCormick - awful rot, except a fine Constable. She fat, and sensual-looking. House pretty rotten. Mr. Deering gave us a lunch here. I spoke at the Art Institute in the afternoon on “the New Art Criticism” - a really good, interesting, clear lecture!
