44 Grosvenor Road, London, Monday March 21. 04

Six horrid days. We reached Plymouth early this morning. Too bored to speak to a soul on board. I was feeling ill with that ‘Grip’.

I Tatti, Settignano, Monday, April 4. 04 Was fearfully seasick nearly a week in London.

Bernhard has been in Paris a week, and I travelling down with Ray and Karin and Winnie Buckley.

The house looks too beautiful - we have seen nothing like it on our travels!!
