I went to see Florence Dike (Reynolds) in the afternoon, she being sadly out of health. She says that both she and her doctor have never known what seemed a

successful Christian Science case that did not come an awful smash in the end. This is “important if true”. Bernhard called on Mr. Winthrop and Norman took him to see Charles Dana Gibson, the illustrator. We had a horrible dinner at the restaurant here. George Carpenter called in the evening. He fell in love with California last summer, and would prefer going to New Zealand rather than to Europe.
