At the end of the week the healer lady heard a great noise in her front hall - a man’s voice threatening her as a cheat and fraud, taking money and doing nothing for it. She came down and found her patient in a rage. She asked him what was the matter, and he said he wanted to drink and smoke as much as ever. But, he added, this beastly country is going to ruin - can’t get a decent bottle of whisky in the whole town. “And cigars?” she asked - A volley of oaths against the rottenness of American civilization - not a sound leaf of tobacco to be had for love or money - “I guess it’s all right,” she said “you won’t need any more treatment.” He stopped cursing, gasped, and turned upon her in fury roaring, “Is this your doing, Madam?”
