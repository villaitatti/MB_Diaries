Friday March 4. 04.

Called on Glaenzer, and lunched with him. Paid other calls, many, ending up with Emilie Hapgood, who seemed to enjoyworse health than before, and more elaborate costumes. Bernhard dined with Mr. Rhinelander at the Round Table Club, and I wrote my Philadelphia speech, which he had sketched out for me in the morning. Faff called.

Saturday March 5. 04.

Jacacci came at 10, a perfect jackass of a man. Mrs. Vollmer at 11 took us to the Stock Exchange, where one of the Governors, Mr. R. Halsey, met us and showed us round. We went to his house, done in good colonial style, to tea. Bernhard lost his pocket-book, but it was found again finally. He dined with Carpenter, who was much nicer. I polished up my speech. Hodder and Caseby called.
