Then B.B. and I hurried off to meet. Billy Taylor, such a nice lad of 21, and talk to him about his future, that agonizing subject to a boy of his age. Both Bernhard and I thought what a fine fellow he was, and what a good husband he would make for Ray - if ever! But Bernhard said it would be fatal for them to meet now.
