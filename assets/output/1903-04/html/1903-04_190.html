3 Somerset Hotel. Boston Shopped, Bernhard having a “treatment” from Mrs. Longyear. I lunched with Mrs. Tyler, who had 5 very middle class dull ladies to meet me, - a very good lunch. B.B. had Mr. Bullard to lunch. I paid calls, including one on Mrs. Elliott and her dear old mother Julia Ward Howe, also one on Mrs. Higginson and a Mrs. Rogers. In the evening we went to Savin Hill
