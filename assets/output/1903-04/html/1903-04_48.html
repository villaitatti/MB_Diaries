Friday Nov. 6. 1903. Hotel Somerset, Boston

Lunched with the parents at Dorchester - going out in a snow-storm which suddenly came on top of a temperature of 70º a few hours before. Earlier in the morning B.B. had been at the Museum with Chalfin - a rather pretentious, I think, dense sort of man, who permits himself such judgments upon matters he knows little about that it makes me doubt his knowing even his own subject - Japanese and Chinese art - in a really scholarly way. He is a painter - that explains it! Mentally he resembles Roger Fry, but he is less versatile and much less charming.
