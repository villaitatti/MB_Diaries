Bond then took us over to the Court of Appeal, which is a building that, as some one said, doesn’t suggest Justice but a Cocktail. There we saw Kenyon Cox’s decorations, which were awful, but better than anyone else’s.

Bernhard went to lunch with Mr. Van Dyke at the Century, and I with Florenceat the Arts. She was a great dear, and we enjoyed ourselves together. She says I simply mustn’t call Bernhard “B.B.” for it means only one thing in America - bed-bug. This explains several queer looks and smiles over his name!
