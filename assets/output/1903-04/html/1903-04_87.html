Tuesday Nov. 24. 03

Went to Cambridge and called on Prof. Norton. Entertained chiefly by the daughter Santayana christened “Paradiso”, a sweet, pretty creature, rather over- cultured. Santayana says she reminds him of a clock ticking under a glass case. Norton has two Bassanos he calls Tintoretto etc., lots of Burne-Jones’ drawings, and an impressive Blake. Afterwards we refreshed ourselves calling on Miss Norton, who nearly fell out of her chair with surprise at hearing us say Denman Ross was a real artist. He has been slow in his development, never showing signs of anything but mediocrity until he was well over 40 - a comforting thought for some of us!

Then we went to the Union, where Santayana gave us a pleasant lunch with the Toys and Bullard. Then we called on dear old Mrs. Agassiz
