Saturday Feb. 27. 04. Somerset Boston

Bernhard rather better. His mother and Rachel came to lunch, and then came with me to Wellesley where I lectured on “The New Art Criticism” - one of my best lectures, by chance, this time. Bernhard went to bed, but I dined with Mrs. Nickerson, pleasant, but too noisy dinner.
