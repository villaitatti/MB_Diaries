Friday Dec. 11. 03. Plaza New York

We walked up and down Fifth Avenue and shuddered at the buildings, and at 1 went to lunch with Mr. Bullard’s friend, Mr. Grenville Winthrop. He has a horrible false French house filled with false XVIII century things - a soft, gentle, melancholy man. No business there. Mr. Hastings, the architect of the five million dollar new Library now being put up was there. He is a Beaux Arts man to the core, and thinks we should all go back to XVIII century art before the revolution broke it up.

I called on Carman(too ill to see me), the Dunnes (Mrs. Abbott too ill) and
