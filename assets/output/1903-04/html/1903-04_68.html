Sunday Nov. 15. 1903. Hotel Somerset Boston

Called on Miss Grace Norton a moment, and then went on to the Fogg Museum to meet Mr. Loeb and a big party and to be shown his gift, which consists of some of the dregs of what Fairfax Murray bought at the FormanSale. They all swear by that miserable cheat, Murray, and Mr. Loeb made a speech to say that the sacred name of Murray was a guarantee that every object in this collection was genuine and priceless and beautiful, and so forth.
