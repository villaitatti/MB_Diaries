We lunched at the Bullards, and then looked at Mr. Bullard’s collection of Whistler etchings and lithographs. They are, on the whole, a disappointment - thin and sometimes dull. Then I called on Mrs. Longyear and some others, ending up with Mrs. Perry, from whose studio I went to Mr. Dow’s where I found Bernhard. Mr. Dow seemed nervous as a host, and rather scatter-brained, but B.B. liked him. He showed us one of those awful drawings by Miss Fanny Alexander, Ruskin’s pet. B.B. said right out that he loathed it. After an early dinner we went to hear the Kneisel Quartette at Cambridge, sitting with Mrs. Ross. They gave the Kreutzer Sonata - delicious!
