XVIII music played by Proctor on the harpsichord and Loeffler as the Viola d’amore. It was beautiful beyond words, and the whole reception was pleasant. The Berenson family came to say goodbye in the evening, and to talk over Finances. Bernhard is really awfully generous. He gives his parents £250 a year, besides their summer holiday, and occasional present to this Mother. He supports Rachel and pays for Bessie’s and Senda’s summers, and now he is going to practically keep Senda abroad for 18 months. He has also given Abe £2,000 to begin business on.
