No one seems to remember anything about me but my horse - beloved Anticellere. One teacher recalled how Florence and I hitched our horses to a trap, neither horse having ever been driven before, and went for a “lark”- She said it was brought before the Faculty with great seriousness, but Prof. Phelps said ‘to let us alone, we’d come out all right. We knew more about horses than any of them did.’
