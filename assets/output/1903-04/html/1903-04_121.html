Neith had a tea here, to which came the Fenellosas, Steffens, Hodder and I forget who, Norman among others. We dined alone with the Alexanders - very pleasant. Their house is awfully nice.

***

Thursday Dec. 17. 03.

I was rather low, so Bernhard went alone to call on Mr. Lathrop in his studio. We met at Mr. Hyde’s for lunch, with a Miss Platt, Mr. Gregory and Cottenett. Hyde is rather handsome and bumptious, with no taste. He is very young. Paid some calls. Dined with Chapmans (Mr. and Mrs. Lawrence) at 7, and went to Concert of old Music after. Bernhard has developed a most awful cold, one of his very worst!
