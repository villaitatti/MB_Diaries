Saturday Jan. 30. 04.

Quiet day - bad headache. Mr. and Mrs. Talcott Williams to dine - he a great disappointment - only a journalist.

Sunday Jan. 31. 04.

Bernhard lunched with Mr. Johnson, who was eager to know all his attributions, and was very nice. I went out to Morristown with Will Nicholson and had lunch with Eliza and Whitall and their children. Good talk in evening about aesthetics.

Monday Feb. 1. 04.

Heavy cold. Stayed in all day. Read Awkward Age(Henry James) but eyes gave out.

Mr. Morgan (biology) and Mr. and Mrs. Hoppin(archaeology) to dine. Dull. Hoppin seems a bounder. They all went early fortunately.
