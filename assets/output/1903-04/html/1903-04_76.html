Wednesday Nov. 18. 1903. Hotel Somerset. Boston.

Bernhard went shopping for boots. I met Ellen Hale at the Museum and we had a look round. Mrs. Berenson and Miss Hale came to lunch. I went earlyish to Cambridge and called with Rachel on Mrs. Willard and Mrs. West, and there met

B.B. at Ethel Puffer’s, where were assembled Prof. Royce, Prof. Münsterberg, Santayana and various human stuffings. Münsterberg frankly said that his sole interest in the Germanic Museums was “political” - a triumph for B.B.’s insight. He is a large, heavy, hideous, genial German Jew of the type of poor Lippmann.
