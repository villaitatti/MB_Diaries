In the evening we dined at Mrs. Roger’s - in what the Italians would call the Arca Santaof Boston society. It was really rather dull, but impressively swell. Mr. Dorr, Admiral and Mrs. Crowninshield and Mr. and Mrs. Peabody were there also.

Saturday Nov. 14. 1903.

Museum and shopping in the morning. Just as we were starting to pay some calls in Cambridge, Mrs. Gardner telephoned for us, and then came and took us to Fenway Court.
