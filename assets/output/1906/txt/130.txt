Monday, Oct. 29, 1906
XIII.
Karin.
Tuesday, Oct. 30, 1906
Came to Oxford. Lunched with Logan and G.[eoffrey] Scott and “did” some of the Colleges and galleries.
Drove out to tea at Bagley Wood with G.[eoffrey] S.[cott]. Dinner and talk with Alys and Bertie.
Heard Gilbert Murray’s lecture on the manuscripts of Euripides.
Wednesday, Oct. 31, 1906
Chatted with Alys. Lunched with Logan and Mr. Haddow. Saw Court Place.
Came back to Karin.
London
