Monday, Sept. 3, 1906
Fine.
Fernhurst Scott and Keynes.
Motor to Bignor and Arundel and Littlehampton and Climping. Great sport.
Tuesday, Sept. 4, 1906
Fernhurst Scott and Keynes.
Motor to Oxford and saw the new house, Court Place. Got back late and in rain, but we all enjoyed it.
Bernhard left St. Moritz.
Wednesday, Sept. 5, 1906
Fine.
Quiet day. Washed hair of all the company. Read aloud the  Phaedrus . Weather heavenly. Keynes is a perfect dear.
