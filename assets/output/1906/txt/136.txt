Monday, Nov. 19, 1906
36 Kensington Square IV (Mr. Lustington’s)
Moved here in morning. Lunched with Rothensteins and Lady Beatrice Thymore. Good talk with R.[othenstein] about Rembrandt. He grants “no specifically artistic qualities”, but loves the emotion conveyed and the character portrayed. Met a Mr. Caleson. Dentist and B.B. tea with Lady Sassoon.
Dined with Mrs. Harrison and her father Major Davis and a Mr. Eaton who is in love with her. She looks 20 years older in two years!
