Thursday, July 19, 1906
Warm. Rain-storm
Hotel Continental, Berlin.
Saw the gallery. What masterpieces! Our only connoisseuring “ hasel ” was a head of the Virgin by Pietro di Domenico di Montepulciano!
Eyes too bad to read.
Wrote Mother, Christina Bremner, Michael.
Friday, July 20, 1906
Cold and rainy.
Berlin.
Spent 5 hours in the gallery. It is splendidly arranged. Bravo Bode, though he does hate us! Saw Friedlander and Fraulein Schottmüller. Could not read, so played a lot of Patience.
Wrote Mother.
Saturday, July 21, 1906
Cold and fine.
Gallery.
Had a terrible struggle to get dinner, trying an impossible place in Thiergarten starred by Baedeker, and ending up at Lutter’s.
Wrote Mother and Ray.
Sunday, July 22, 1906
Lunched with Herr von Kaufmann. Saw 2 Simone collections with Friedlander.
Wrote Mother, Ned Warren, Florence Reynolds.
Berlin - Hanover
