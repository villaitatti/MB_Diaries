Thursday, March 29, 1906
Rain- but a jolly ____ round fire.
Cleared - saw Spoleto - Motored to Foligno, left bags, visited Montefalco and Spello and came back to that excellent hotel for night.
The two boys are awfully nice. I like Scott particularly .
Friday, March 30, 1906
Clear but very cold.
Motored to Assisi -  delightful ! Passed through Perugia, and under Cortona (a beautiful road), wandered in plain and at last got to Montepulciano again for night. Bitter cold. Sat up till 12 talking.
Scott gave his ideas on love - analogous to enjoyment of Nature. I said it was rot. I was wrong.
*
Saturday, March 31, 1906
Clear but fearfully cold.
Motored to Pienza and saw that. On to Siena, with a jolly breakdown.Lunch at Siena. Took boys to Poggibonsi, where they went to San Gimignano, and we came home.
Nice little youngster followed motor at Pienza.
Sunday, April 1, 1906
Cold clear.
Ray and Karin
Delicious to be at home. Nothing happened except that Mrs. Streeter and daughter came to lunch. But we are greatly worried about Karin’s  other  ear.
