Monday, April 30, 1906
Pouring.
Emily, Britten, Logan.
B.B. at Venice.
Hapgoods to dine - so nice.
Tuesday, May 1, 1906
Pouring.
B.B. at Venice. Miss Isan and Miss Langton to lunch.
Called with Logan on the Huttons.
Wednesday, May 2, 1906
Pouring then fine.
B.B. at Verona.
Drove with Logan and Don Randi to see a Villa at Arcetri.
