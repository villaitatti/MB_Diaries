Thursday, Dec. 20, 1906
Sunny and windy.
In bed with cold, but cured it with Dr. Langdale’s Essence of Cinnamon!
Went over the Mantegna with B.B. and did some work.
Friday, Dec. 21, 1906
Sunny, cold and windy.
Well!! A miracle, for the cold was going to be awful.
Worked and began re-typing.
Went to town about the Insurance.
Bernhard called on Checchina D’Orsay and we both called on Mme Narischkine.
Saturday, Dec. 22, 1906
Fine but windy 
Worked. Mr. and Mrs. McCurdy came to tea. He is going to write on Carpaccio. Benn came to lunch and quoted Punch about Matthew Arnold, “He wrote Bald, Dead and Balderdash” - Miss Blood came. The Princess is away, and she is  awfully lonely .
Worked in evening.
Sunday, Dec. 23, 1906
Fine 
Worked. Agnes Steffenburg to lunch. Called on Mrs. Ross, the Herrons and Neith. Fafner came and spent the evening with us. Dear fellow!
Florence
