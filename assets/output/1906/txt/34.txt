Monday, January 1, 1906
London. Fine
Spent day with Karin.
Florence: Bernhard drove to San Clemente.
Tuesday, January 2, 1906
Adbury Lodge, Newbury. Rain.
Came to Jephsons. It rained all day. They are kind and Mounteney most affectionate, but they are so desperately unthinking and uninterested in nought that they bore me terribly - alas! Dined with Janet and Mr. Streatfield and saw Shaw’s play “Major Barbara” - did not like it.
Florence: Bernhard received a request for his biography from the Encyclopédie Larousse. Gronau and Placci lunched with him. Then he called on the Serristori.
Wednesday, January 3, 1906
Day with Karin, but lunched with Janet at Mrs. Phillpot’s.
Florence: Bernhard lunched with the Placcis; Carlo took him to see the Tolommei’s “family” pictures - i.e. job lots we have known for years in antiquity shops! He went with Horne over Davanzati Palace.
