Monday, January 8, 1906
London. Rainy.
Shopping with Grandma in morning and by myself in afternoon. Britten came to dine.
Florence: Horrid weather. Placci too Bernhard to the Max Strozzis, to see the pictures. Miss Erichsen came to tea.
Bernhard had a perfectly sleepless night.
Tuesday, January 9, 1906
London. Fine and then a  thunder  and hail shower!
Shopping ___ in morning. Called on Mrs. Rendel.
Florence: Glorious visit from Serristori. Reading Lafcadio Hearn’s “Japan” and “Gesta Romanorum”.
Wednesday, January 10, 1906
Karin had theatre party.
Florence: Horrid weather. Tea with d’Orsay, Serristori, and Muravieff.
