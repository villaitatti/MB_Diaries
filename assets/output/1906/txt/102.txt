Monday, July 23, 1906
Cool.
Museum again all day. Dined in Thiergarten (Terasse) very nice place.
Wrote Mother.
Tuesday, July 24, 1906
Warm, rain.
*Hotel Royal, Hanover.
Saw Greek things in morning. Then went to pictures where everything looked crude and barbaric and ugly - genre at the best, after Greek. Bernhard says the Greek ideal wasn’t an ideal but only a canon. I doubt this.
Came here by  splendid  train.
Wrote Mother, Ray.
Wednesday, July 25, 1906
Cool and cloudy.
*Hotel Royal, Hanover.
Saw Provinzial- und Kestner Museum. Very exciting. Quite a haul of Italians.
Wrote Mother, Mrs. Berenson.
