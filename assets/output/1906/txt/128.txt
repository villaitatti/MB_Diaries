Monday, Oct. 22, 1906
XI.
Fine.
Karin better. Spent day entirely with her.
Jephson called, and sent huge basket of fruit.
B.B. [at] Albi, Cahors.
Tuesday, Oct. 23, 1906
Fine.
Karin up first time. Day with her.
B.B. at Agen.
Wednesday, Oct. 24, 1906
Fine.
Karin up again.
G.S. came up. Very depressed. So was I.
B.B. at Bordeaux.
London
