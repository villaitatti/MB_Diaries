Thursday, July 5, 1906

Heavy.

Saw pictures in Museum in morning.Drove out to the Faniteum (St. Veit) which is a convalescent home founded by Lanckorovski in his wife’s memory. He came too to see he impression it made. I think we were “adequate”.

Wrote Mother, Ray.

Friday, July 6, 1906

Rainy, clearing.

Saw the objets d’art at Museum. B.B. went to Industrial Museum in afternoon and enjoyed the director.

Wrote Mother, Placci, Lucy Perkins, Volpi, Mr. Kelly, Emily, Venturi, Mrs. Berenson.

Saturday, July 7, 1906

Cool, cloudy.

Grand Hotel, Cracow.

Train to Cracow 7.50-2.40.Lanckoronski met us and took us to see the Czartoryski Museum and the Potocki pictures. Dined at Count André Potocki’s - very agreeable people.

Sunday, July 8, 1906

Count Miecielski took us to Puslowski’s and Prince Casinis Lubo[n]inski’s and the Czartoryski.

Rested afternoon. Drove to Camaldoli Carmel of Bialana.

Cracow, Vienna, Budapest
