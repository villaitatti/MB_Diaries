Monday, May 14, 1906

Fine.

Emily.

Ill in bed with sudden attack of diarrhoea. Read Rovey and E. Puffon on music.

Mr. Britten left.

Mr. Dent came to dine.

Wrote Mother.

Tuesday, May 15, 1906

Emily.

Got up, feeling rather ill, but did some work.

Mr. Benn came to lunch; he was somehow dreadfully dull.

Wrote Mother, Reinach, V. Hartshome, Mrs. Middlemore.

Wednesday, May 16, 1906

Pour.

Emily.

Did a good deal of work.

The Serristori and Rembelinski called. Also Mr. Washburn.

Wrote Mother.
