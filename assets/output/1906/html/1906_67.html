Thursday, April 5, 1906

Cold and grey.

Ray and Karin.

Mr. Rolfs about Laman all morning. Mrs. Halsey called, poor thing.

Mrs. Margaret L. Woods and sister, the Hookers, Mrs. W. Flower and Miss Rigby and Miss Seelye to tea. Fafner came to dine.

Wrote to Mother, Florence.

Friday, April 6, 1906

Cold, clear, windy.

Went to town to see Rolfs’ photographs of Laurana. Houghton picked Ray up in his motor and took her for a picnic. Rembelinkski and Piero Misciatelli came to tea. Also Mr. Morgan. Had a walk with Kerr-Lawsons.

Wrote Otto Gutekunst, Mrs. Fagan, Dowdeswell, Mother, Mr. Cannon, Michael, Lucy Perkins, Christina, Scott.

Saturday, April 7, 1906

Clear, cold.

Drove with Ray and Karin to call at the Villa Casona on Burn Murdochs, also called [on] Gays, Gutekunst, etc. B.B. at Serristori’s.

Wrote to Mr. Cannon, Miss Freeman, Mother, Rachel Perry, E. Trevelyan, Don Guido.

Houghtons came to dine.

Palm Sunday, April 8, 1906

Windy.

Agnes came to lunch.

Mr. Benn called. Bernhard went to woods walk with Agnes.

Wrote to Mother.

Fighting a cold.
