Monday, Aug. 13, 1906

Fernhurst

Came back from Kellys. Find that Miss Weiss now says Tovey was in love with Miss Kelly, hence her anxiety and misery at losing Kelly.

Jolly talk with Ray and Karin

JHoughton called

Tuesday, Aug. 14, 1906

Showery

Fernhurst

Wrote letters. Called on Vicar. Spent afternoon chatting with Grace and Gertrude Hatum Ellis. Houghton to tea

Talked with Ray till midnight about the unhappiness of most of her Newnham friends.

Wrote B.B., Scott, etc.

Wednesday, Aug. 15, 1906

Fine Showery

Fernhurst

Went totown with Ray and Karin. Saw Dr. Heath who was full of his new ____ operation which preserves the drum and small bone of the ear.

Saw Shaw’s Play “You Never Can Tell” and thought it poor and flat.

Wrote B.B., Britten, Cracroft, Miss Kelly, Mrs. Berenson, Mrs. Rowland, Father Brown etc.
