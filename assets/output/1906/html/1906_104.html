Monday, July 30, 1906

Fine.

Fernhurst

Settling in. Saw Grace’s cottage. Van Bridge.

Dr. and Mrs. Duke called.

Tuesday, July 31, 1906

Fine.

Fernhurst

Went to town - met Karin - saw doctor. Met Alys – shopped - Came down.

Wednesday, Aug. 1, 1906

Fine.

Fernhurst

B.B. at St. Moritz.

Alys here. Talk. Walks. Games of Patience.
