Monday, Dec. 31, 1906

Glorious

Such a day! And we spent the afternoon at poor Horne’s, who has turned his house into a dreary rubbish-shop. He is the born collector, and lives in utter discomfort, with all his objets piled around him - the absolute negation of beauty and dignity. Called also on the Serristori, and in the evening on Lina at Poggio.
