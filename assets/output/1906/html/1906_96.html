Monday, July 2, 1906

Cool.

Feeling very miserable with influenza.

Mrs. Schluringen called and we took her to lunch at the Grand Hotel, and then she took me shopping. I got a dress and coat. She was silly but rather winning. Lanckoronski gave us a dinner with Count Ziehy (Hungarian leader), count Hartl ex Minister of Education, and M. Dvorák, a Kunstfusser.

Wrote Mother, Fr. Brown, Mrs. Ross, Emily, Mrs. Sprague.

Tuesday, July 3, 1906

Fine.

Depressing morning at Academy - Bernhard at Museum after lunch - I feeling ill and resting.

From 5-8 we were at Lanckoronski’s, connoshing his Italian pictures. He has picked up quantities of odds and ends. Interesting but very tiring. Dvorák was there.

Wrote to Mother.

Wednesday, July 4, 1906

Warmer.

Feeling ill, stayed in till 11; went to dressmaker.Bernhard went to Gallery.After lunch went to Liechtenstein gallery, then to Miller Aicholz’, and arranged about buying the Van Dyck. Then to Lanckoronski’s again. Met very handsome and fascinating Count Nostitz of Prague.

Wrote to Mother, Ray, Keynes, Emily, Neith.

Vienna and Cracow
