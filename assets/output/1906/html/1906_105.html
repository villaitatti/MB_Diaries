Thursday, Aug. 2, 1906

Fine then rain.

Fernhurst

Alys here.

Drove in to meet Geoffrey Scott, who talked very amusingly in evening.

Friday, Aug. 3, 1906

Fine.

Fernhurst

Swimming – Tennis - Camp Fire. Slept out, but I came in at 3 and Scott at 5. Girls slept on till 8.30

Saturday, Aug. 4, 1906

Fine.

Fernhurst

Swimming and games. Picnic at Blackdown to salute Full Moon. All wished - secretly - but nearly all sold me their secrets. Ray, Karin and Elsie at £5, Scott at £7.10. He was dying to tell me anyhow and I half knew it. Walked home.

Sunday, Aug. 5, 1906

Fine.

Ray sick. Scott and I called on Zangwills and lost our way coming home, and had a rather melancholy time.
