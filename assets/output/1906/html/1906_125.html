Thursday, Oct. 11, 1906

VII.

Warm. Fine

Hotel St. James, Paris.

Reached Paris 2 o’clock. Washed, had tea. Bernhard called on Lady Sassoon. Met Mrs. Crawshay.

I went to Reinach’s and Bernhard joined us there, and we had dinner. Afterwards we called on M. Adolphe Schloss, who has some awful Dutch pictures and a fair Basaiti.

Friday, Oct. 12, 1906

Cooler. Cloudy.

Paris.

B.B. bothered by the Brancaccios. She must be going crazy.

We lunched with Mr. and Mrs. Latham and dined with Mrs. Sherrill. B.B. had tea with Mrs. Porter Palmer. The Sherills took us to see a play called “La plus Amoureuse”.

Saturday, Oct. 13, 1906

Rain

Paris.

Louvre.

Lunched with Sherrills and went to see an exhibition of miniatures.

Dined with Willy Taylor and his friend.

Sunday, Oct. 14, 1906

Fine.

Crossed to London.

B.B. lunched with Rembelinski and Contessa Serristori and dined with De Rudinis.

London
