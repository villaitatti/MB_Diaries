Monday, February 26, 1906

Rainy and warm.

Benn came to lunch, and the Hapgoods left soon after, to establish themselves and the children in a pension. We could not keep them longer. They tired us, and they made us nervous with their incompetence and slackness.Bernhard and I are rejoicing in being alone again! We had a delightful walk.

Wrote Mother, Senda, Christina, Guido, Mr. Murray, Molle Michel, William Sloane Kennedy, Karin, Emily Dawson.

Shrove Tuesday, February 27, 1906

Grey and windy.

Letter from Glaenzer saying he was sending cheque for £1,400 for the Lotto. Hurrah! This is quick.Conte Campello and his middle-aged overbearing Scotch Contessa came to lunch.

I called on the Platonoff and did a few errands but it was l’ultimo giorno di Carnevale.French lesson.

Wrote to Mother, Mme Terni di Gregory, Mr. Cannon and to Roger to give him £ 100 on the Lotto’s sale.

Ash Wednesday, February 28, 1906

Pour!!

Did a lot of work.Miss Blood spent morning with B.B. looking at Milanese photos.We called on Labouchère, who was in great form.

I forgot to record that Pozzolini told B.B. at the D’Annunzio lunch that they had engaged a man at 5 lire a meter to “restore” the Ghirlandajo frescoes at S. M. Novella!!!!!

Wrote to Mother, Ray, Jack Burke, Guido, Neith, Houghtons, Malle-Michel.
