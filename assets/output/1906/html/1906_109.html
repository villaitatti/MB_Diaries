Thursday, Aug. 16, 1906

Showery but fine

Fernhurst

Walked up with Grace to see Zangwill whose first son was born yesterday.

Read Matri Princes’ “Disintegration of a Personality”

Wrote B.B., Miss Quilter, Fr. Brown., Mrs Withers

Friday, Aug. 17, 1906

Rainy

Fernhurst

Morning of writing. Walked with Grace and Ray in the afternoon, very pleasant

Bernhardt writes enthusiastically of Santayana’s volume upon Religion.

Wrote Florence Reynolds, Emily, Algar Thorold, Christina Bremmer, Scott, B.B., Triulzi, Avv.Venturi, Janet Dodge.

Saturday, Aug. 18, 1906

Fine

Fernhurst

Cricket match.

Ellie Rendel and her two brothers came over

Wrote B.B., Michael, Mrs. Rowland, Britten

Sunday, Aug. 19, 1906

Fine

Fernhurst

Watched various games of cricket

Houghton motored over. Karin ear began to run

Wrote B.B.
