Tuesday, July 30, 1907
Rainy.
Called on Heath and found him quite knocked up, unable to do Karin’s operation today. It is too dreary waiting!
Karin came up. It was too late to hinder her. We went to the Acoustician place (2 o  Bucklersbury) and found out what was the matter with her machine.
Bernhard lunched with Harry Cust and called on Mrs. Crawshay.
