Bernhard and I called on the Rosses, and then had a walk, ending up with a call on the Kerr-Lawsons and Janet. Poor Janet’s finances are going down in a dreadful way. Mrs. Kerr-Lawon said that Miss Fairchild simply  detests  B.B. and me, and can’t keep it in – she hasn’t even the tact to avoid telling such intimate friends as they how she loathes us.
