Wednesday April 13. 1898.
Music lesson. Kerr-Lawson came to lunch and Bernhard gave him a sitting. Fry came to go over Venetian photographs. In the evening Lina Duff-Gordon came to dinner and Aubrey Waterfield to stay. 
Thursday April 14.98 
Bernhard, Waterfield, the Halseys, the Kerr-Lawsons, Horne and Mrs. Fry went to Monte Senario. I stayed at home and had a quiet day “all to myself.” After a lesson from  Buonamici , I bicycled to Madallena, but as they were not there I returned. Horne and the Kerr-Lawsons came to dinner, and the latter stayed all night.
