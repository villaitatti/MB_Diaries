Sunday June 26.98. Bernhard’s 33rd Birthday.
We went to the Louvre in the morning, but Bernhard was too submerged beneath his heavy cold to enjoy much. After lunch we saw M. Valton’s Collection of Drawings. Fabbri came and we had tea and a smoke with him. He seems the same as ever. He said Stéphanie had been ill, but I did not offer to see her. She’s too awful a bore! Bernhard dined with the Péreires. He is reading  Claudian  and  Catullus , and I am reading the  Fioretti di San Francesco  and the interminable love letters of Mdlle. de Lespinasse.
