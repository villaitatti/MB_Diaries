Friday Dec. 2. 1898. Frullino. Florence.
The Nowers came to lunch, and we walked out to the Tree - a wonderful day. Fairchild came to tea. Began  Hogg’s Life of Shelley . Kerr-Lawsons called. 
Saturday Dec. 3. 1898. * * * *
Quiet day of work. Dined with Fairchild in his villa at Settignano, and had a beautiful walk home. He was overcome with horror at a swell Florentine dinner he had been to where all the ladies got drunk, and the  extremest  (he says!) indecencies took place after dinner. It had made him quite ill and nervous.
