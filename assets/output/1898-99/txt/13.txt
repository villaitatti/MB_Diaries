Bernhard and I went on bicycles to I Cedri, to see the Laureate – who was out. Coming home, we got lost among bleak Tuscan hills, and arrived here by moonlight.
Tuesday April 5. 1898.
The Frys arrived at 3, and after tea I called on the Mortons, who were in bed as usual, and as striving as ever after culture. 
Wednesday April 6. 1898.
