Thursday April 21.98. Frullino Florence 
We were to have gone to Prato, but it has rained all day. Bernhard finished his work on the drawings of that beast, Lorenzo di Credi, and I made great strides forward with the list of “sacred pictures” for the next Golden Urn. Aubrey Waterfield gets boringer and boring-er! 
Bernhard can talk of nothing but the wonders of Pindar and the interest of Mommsen.
