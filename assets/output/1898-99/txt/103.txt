Mr. and Mrs. Nowers spent ten days here very pleasantly. Blair Fairchild, who is a nice boy, was also here. One evening we spoke of insanity, and he turned pale and could not eat. Afterwards he told Bernhard he had lost his mind several times, and lived in dread of it. Poor boy! Poor boy!
