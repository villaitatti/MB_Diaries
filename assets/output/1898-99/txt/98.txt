Monday Nov. 14.98. Frullino. Florence
The cable came: “Evelyn died peacefully November 2nd.” Alas! She was my most intimate friend from the days we were at College together. There has never been a cloud, never the least little shade of lack of sympathy. I shall never love another woman so much till Ray grows to be a woman. 
.  .  .  .  .  .  .
Tuesday and Wednesday – [Nov. 15-16, 1898]
Thoughts of Evelyn. I wish she could reach down to the depths of my heart and see how I loved her.
