Wednesday Nov. 30.98. Frullino. Florence.
Placci came to lunch, and in spite of his anti-Dreyfus-ism and his bigoted Catholicism, was as lovely and dear as ever, and won back our hearts. In the late afternoon we walked to Poggio Gherardo and greeted Lina and the Kerr-Lawsons on their return.
