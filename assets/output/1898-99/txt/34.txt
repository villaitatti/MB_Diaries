Saturday May 7. 1898. Frullino Florence
A rainy, windy day, so cold I had to have the calorifere started. We stayed at home all day, working and looking at photographs – chiefly Fra Angelico’s. Etta told me of the wife of the Bishop of Winchester’s mad devotion to a certain Mrs. Chapman, for whom she neglects  all  her duties. It is one of those almost pathological cases. She says it is  The Best  and that her whole life is Consecrated to this woman. It is very hard on the Bishop and everyone talks about it. Riots in Florence.
