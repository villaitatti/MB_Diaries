Wednesday Sept. 28.98. Leinfelder. Munich
Saw Nuremberg, ate some Würstlein, came here. I finished vol. II of Mommsen and we played whist in the train. 
Thursday Sept. 29.98. Munich
Gallery in the morning. Met Dr. Head and Mrs. Daniels. The latter seemed like a clever woman, fond of asking intelligent questions – not so I – intelligent always! Called on Miss Lowndes, who dined with us later, and on the Kolbs. They describe Obrist’s wife as insignificant, but a strong tincture of jealousy no doubt coloured their view. Germaine Kolb is pretty, but distinctly not agreeable.
