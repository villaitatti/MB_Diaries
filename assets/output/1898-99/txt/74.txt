Friday Sept. 30.98. Leinfelder. Munich
Gallery in morning with Maud Cruttwell. Dr. Head and Mrs. Daniels lunched with us, the latter silent, perhaps observing, perhaps too deaf for general conversation, I could not make out which. Dr. Head told me that the form of insanity Mrs. Fry suffers from is the worst of all. Not only is it the most painful – the condition of objectless Fear, but it may come on at any time, even when the patient feels well, and is so horrible that in a few seconds they may put an end to themselves. You can  never  feel safe.
