Tuesday Feb. 7. 1899.
Music lesson. Then I went to Dr. Grazzi, who discovered Adenoids in the back of my throat.Shopping with Logan and home to tea. In the evening we read the  Lysis , but Bernhard struck, and declared himself too much bored with Socrates to go on. He prefers Picquet!! He called on Benn in the afternoon, and found him in heaven because Spencer had asked Knowles to invite him (Benn) to write a 25 page article in the  “ Nineteenth Century” on his philosophy. We all agreed at dinner that we should hate to write for the “Nineteenth Century”, but that no one would believe us sincere in saying so.
