Monday Oct. 17. 1898. Frullino. Florence.
Alys and Mother and I came down and spent a pleasant week here, while Bernhard went bicycling with Bertie. They stayed a while with the Countess Pasolini at Monte Ricco. 
Bad news came from America, which weighed on both our hearts. Bernhard’s enemies are trying to persuade Mrs. Gardner that he has cheated her over the pictures he has bought her, and her husband (who was always jealous) believes it. Still, she does not, and that is the important thing. 
Alys and I took Mother a little giro to Lucca, Viareggio and Pisa, and started her home last night. We returned here. Have passed a quiet day, broken by a call from Mr. Morgan and a walk.
