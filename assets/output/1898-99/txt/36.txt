Thursday May 12.98. Frullino Florence 
Quiet, nice day. Horne was amusing in the evening, talking about Arthur Symond’s love affair with an Empire dancer, and Selwyn Image’s unfortunate engagement to a ballet dancer. 
Friday May 13. 1898. 
Bernhard lunched with Benn, and Morgan turned up to lunch here. He says there is talk of proroguing Parliament and putting Italy under military rule for a year.
Saturday May 14. 1898. 
Placci came, tremendously excited about the Riots and the condition of Italy. He played a little – but he has fallen off. I had a music lesson, and then went “Topladying” with Horne.
