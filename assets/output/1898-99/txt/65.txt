x Thursday Sept. 8.98. Dom Hotel. Cöln [i.e. Köln]
Saw the Brussels gallery and the principal buildings, and came here. 
Friday Sept. 9.98
Did the Gallery pretty thoroughly, with the Director. Enjoyed Stephan Lochner. In the afternoon we saw the rest of the sights, and had a splendid bottle of Rhein wine for dinner (Steinberger Cabinet, 10 m.)
Saturday Sept. 10.98. Angleterre. Hildesheim
