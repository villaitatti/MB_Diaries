Sunday Dec. 4.98. 
Percy Atherton (musician) and Fairchild lunched here. Then we called on Mr. Fiske, who was boring, and on the Rosses, who were very nice. Began our opus magnum of the Minor Painters in the evening. Looked at the Rembrandt volumes I and II which Bode is bringing out. 
Monday Dec. 5.98.
Music lesson, and Bernhard took away Masaccio portrait (alas!) to send to Mrs. Gardner. I called on Miss Cruttwell and Miss Lowndes. Fairchild dined with me, and told me his  strange history , while Bernhard dined with Madame Rasponi. 
Tuesday Dec. 6.98. 
Bernhard called on Miss Blood. Finished Hogg.
