Thursday Dec. 15. 1898. 
Lunched with the Placcis. Carlo spoke very bitterly of Miss Blood as a parasite living on the bounty of the Princess Ghika and pretending that the money, or a large part of it, was hers. It was  so unlike  him to speak in this way that we suspected Madame de Montebello behind it. He and Buonamici played an organ and orchestra concerto by Rheinberger, and then we went to a concert to hear Buonamici and Faini play a Bach sonata (la maggiore). Bernhard had tea with Benn and I had my music lesson. Benn was saying that England had never been much influenced by France, and that individual men, like Gibbon, who were influenced by French things, never had themselves much influence in England.
