Saturday Oct. 1.98. Leinfelder. Munich
Saw the Glaspalast and Secession - horrible, horrible! Maud lunched with us. I called on “Puck”, and met that awful Endell. We took supper with the Kolbs. It was very dull, as Annette was not there, being still laid up in the country by a bicycle accident. Germaine is quite detestable, and there was a horrid Philistine, Mr. Saunders, as the other guest. Germaine sang – it was  awful !
