Sunday Dec. 11.98. 
Beatrice and Herbert Horne to lunch. Walked with them to Settignano and all called on the Fairchilds and then on Miss Blood. There we found the Kerr-Lawsons, who thereupon accompanied B.B. and me back to the Rosses. So we saw all our hillside. Miss Fairchild, the far-famed, is a great disappointment. 
Worked in the evening. B.B. read Burckhardt on Greek culture. 
Monday Dec. 12.98. 
Went to Buonamici and Quartette concert: liked Boccherini quartette. Afterwards went on a wild goose chase after a “Botticelli”. Then I called on Mrs. Madeau and found her flirting with a lot of middle aged men, all of them at the “table rapping” stage. The people were awful. 
We bought (for £500) a little Crucifixion on gold ground, perhaps by Giottino.
