Thursday April 7. 1898. Frullino. Florence
Bernhard described Pennell’s art as “taking a photograph and translating it into chicken-tracks.” He lunched with Count Hochberg, and afterwards I walked over with the Frys and we were shown over the house and given tea. The situation is lovely, but the Graf has Germanized the building as much as possible, and within he has turned it, as Fry said, into a superior sort of Savoy Hotel. But now having finished it, he wants to sell it, and begin fresh operations somewhere in England!
