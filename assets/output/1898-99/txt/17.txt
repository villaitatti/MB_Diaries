Sunday April 10.98. Easter Sunday
Horne, Horne, the silent Horne came to lunch. We called at Poggio and found a huge reception [written:  deception ] in full swing and poor Lina looking miserable, and then we had a walk through the woods. Horne stayed to dinner.
