Tuesday May 16. [i.e. 17.]98. Frullino. Florence.
Scirocco and languor. I spent the afternoon with Etta, and pasted a great many things in The Children’s Book. The Countess Rasponi called on Etta. Bernhard called on Mr. Fiske. We looked at Signorelli photos in the evening. 
Wednesday May 17. [i.e. 18.] 1898. 
Still scirocco. Music lesson. The Rasponi called. Horne left.
Thursday. May 18. [i.e. 19]98. 
Miss Cruttwell and an Indian poetess – very beautiful – came to lunch. Kerr-Lawson also dropped in.
Friday May 19. [i.e. 20.]98. 
Rained all day. Bernhard called on Miss Priestley.
