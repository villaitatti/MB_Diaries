She told me too that since Fräulein Reuter had her baby she has become immensely popular at Munich. Everyone goes to see her, and takes her up, to show their superiority to old-fashioned prejudices.
Puck’s most intimate friend at present is a Russian girl (of 33) named Lou Salomé, who was Nietzsche’s mistress from 18-28, but finally left him as she “saw through him” and found him a charlatan. I expect his madness was beginning.
In the evening we heard the  Zauberflöte , very well given, and then the two Kolbs, Mr. Saunders and the painter Ross came to take supper with us. Dull.
