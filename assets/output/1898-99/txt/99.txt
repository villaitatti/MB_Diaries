Thursday, Nov. 17, 1898 
Wilfrid writes me that he has fallen desperately in love, at first sight, with an Italian girl named Magda Sindici, who has written a book, “Via Lucis” ,  under the name of Kassandra Vivaria. The “Chronicle” has been publishing her life – she is a “revolted daughter” of a swell Roman family. He says she is very beautiful. I hope it may turn out happily for him. A month ago he was in love with Lina Duff Gordon - a year ago with me. But  this  “is final” he says!  Speriamo .
