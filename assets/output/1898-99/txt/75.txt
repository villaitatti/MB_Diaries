A letter from Wilfrid Blaydes is very despondent about getting to Italy. He literally has not a penny, and of course he is utterly unfit for making it at journalism. 
We called on the Hildebrands, a charming family, in their beautiful new house, built by him. It is really excellent, in the best taste, inside and out.
