Tuesday Oct. 18.98. Frullino. Florence
Had a singing lesson. Chatted in the evening. 
Wednesday Oct. 19.98 
A quiet day of work. Miss Hamilton called on Bernhard, whom she insisted on calling “Professor Berenson”. Bernhard and Alys and Bertie called on the Benns.
Thursday Oct. 20.98, 
Music lesson – haven’t lost so much as I feared. Shopped with Alys. Read Goethe in the evening. 
Friday Oct. 21.98
