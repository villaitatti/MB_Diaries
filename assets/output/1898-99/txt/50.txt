Sunday June 19. 1898. Europa. Turin.
With the Cooks we spent the morning in the Gallery with the Director, Count Vesmè. In the afternoon Bernhard and I met him again at the deadly Exposition. 
Monday June 20. 1898. Train to Paris.
We went to the Academy and Duomo in the morning, and left by the 2.20 train. Bernhard has caught a most awful cold.
Tuesday June 21.98. 3 rue de Beaune – Hotel du Quai Voltaire. Paris
Reached here at 7 and after breakfast went to the Louvre. Placci came at 3 and we went to the Salon and met the Reinachs. We liked almost nothing, least of all Rodin’s famous “Balzac”. We dined with the Reinachs. M. Joseph Reinach was there, apparently calm and unconcerned with his  procès .
