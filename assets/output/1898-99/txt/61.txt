In the morning we went to the Louvre, and after lunch to the Luxembourg, where, outside the Manets and Puvis, we found everything had sunk to the “Ce m’est-égal” category! Ou sont les neiges d’autan?! Then we went with Miss Blood to her dentist’s (Vian, 47 Bd. Haussmann) to see his modern things. Outside a few Daumiers and Degas, they were all  less  than  égal .
