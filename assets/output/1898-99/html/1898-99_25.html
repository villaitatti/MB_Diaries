Saturday April 23. 1898.

Count Papafava came to lunch. He had liked that story of D’Annunzio about Lazarus - what a gulf between us and even the nicest Italians! I called on Madame Grottanelli, and came back to find Mr. Davis of Newport here, and then came a queer Dane named Andreas Aubert, sent by Miss Taylor.
