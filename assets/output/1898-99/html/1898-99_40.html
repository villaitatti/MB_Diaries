Tuesday May 24.98. Il Frullino. Florence.

Bernhard had a walk with Papafava. I shopped and called on the Huntingtons. Read some more of Shaw’s silly plays.

Wednesday May 25.98

Music lesson. The Placcis called on Bernhard, and Morgan on me. Read the “Golden Urned” parts of the Bible.

Thursday May 26.98.

My last lesson with Buonamici. Benn came to lunch and Bernhard had a walk with him.

X X X X

Friday May 27. 1898.

Quiet rainy day. Bernhard in despair over the Corsini tondo – at last gives it back to Filippino.
