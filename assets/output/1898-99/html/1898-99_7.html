Thursday March 31. 1898. Frullino Florence

Logan writes: “I must tell thee of my visit to Miss Sellers. They are living at Garlant’s Hotel, as they were turned out of their flat, and have not yet decided about the House of Lords. She received me with shouts of joy. She was so bored, she said, and thy not answering her letter made her fear we had dropped her. Then she began a tale of woe; how desperately bored she was, how she hated London, how dull she found society, no talk, no frankness, no congenial soul, but smart ladies all going to Stephen Phillips’ lectures, and all South Kensington to Miss Harrison’s, and how she would end by going to Miss Harrison’s lectures herself, and how the feeling of struggle was taking hold of her, and how furious
