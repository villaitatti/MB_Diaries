Thursday Dec. 22.98

A magnificent lunch at the Rosses – a walk with Lina, who is, I think, quite ready to fall in love with Trevy. Wind still high.

Friday. Dec. 23.98

The day of the children’s “First Communion”. I sent them little breast pins to wear, and I hope some of the anti-prayers I sent along with them will neutralize this terrible Catholic virus with which they are being innoculated. Alas! alas!

Maud Cruttwell came to lunch full of excitement at having been asked to write on Signorelli for that Loeser - Strong - Williamson series. Bernhard was very nice and said he would help her.

He spent the afternoon at the Cenacolo di Sant’Apollonia and Santa Maria Novella with Miss Blood, and had tea with her, and called for me when I was taking a singing lesson of Blair Fairchild, and we walked home together.
