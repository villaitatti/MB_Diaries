Bernhard, after Horne was gone, began a Lamentation over the dullness and unimportance of his work, his longing to do nothing but read and enjoy Greek, his desire for a life completely given to aesthetic experiences. The book he is doing on Florentine Drawings is a great weight upon him.

I had a letter from Wilfrid Blaydes, who is in London, asking me to write to him.

Monday April 4. 1898. Frullino Florence
