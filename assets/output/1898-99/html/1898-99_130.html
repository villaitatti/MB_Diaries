Friday Dec. 30. 1898. Frullino. Florence

I had caught a ferocious cold, and it kept me miserable in the house all day. Janet Dodge arrived in the evening, by the same train Trevy took to go to Naples. He left under a cloud, feeling he had been horrid, so I wrote to him telling him we counted him so genuinely “one of us” that he might feel free to come and go as he liked. Horne came to dinner. Lord Balcarres called.

X X X

Saturday Dec. 31st. ’98

Cold still miserable. Fairchild and Kerr-Lawson came to dinner.
