Sunday Sept. 25.98. Grossherzog von Sachsen. Eisenach

Saw Gotha, and met the Holroyds there, with whom we lunched. They went on to Frankfort am Main and we came here. Visited the Wartburg, ruined by modern restoration and visitors.

Monday Sept. 26.98. Bamberger Hof Bamberg

We went to Meiningen and saw the rather charming sympathetic Schloss and the pictures – some of them “Sacred” – and met Alys and Bertie at a junction and came here. There was time for a general look round, and in the evening we chatted.

Tuesday Sept. 27.98. Bayerisher Hof. Nuremberg

Saw Bamberg and came here, and had a look round. Heard the Fliegende Holländer.
