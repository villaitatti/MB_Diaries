Monday Oct. 31. 1898. Frullino. Florence.

Music lesson, a concert with Kreuzer Sonata and Beethoven’s Andante with variations and the début of a young American singer named Mary Duff. Then tea with horrible Miss Halsey, shopping and home. We read aloud Pater’s Demeter in the evening. Bernhard and Bertie bicycled to Pontassieve. The Dreyfus revision is decided!

Tuesday Nov. 1. 1898.

Mr. Power and Mr. Price, with their friends Mr. and Mrs. Harold Boulton, came to lunch. It poured and they had to stay on to tea, but Mr. Price was so very amusing, in his genial Irish way, that he kept us all in high spirits. Alys and I had lessons from Mr. Power. The weather has broken. We read Pater’s Demeter aloud in the evening.
