Saturday Jan. 28.1899. Frullino. Florence

Music lesson, and then we all walked to see the Frosino, which is a gem of a place.

Sunday Jan. 29.99.

Bernhard drove with Mr. Hamilton Aidé to lunch with the Rosses. Janet and Mr. Morgan lunched here, and we walked to the Frosino, which looked lovelier than ever. I called on the Rosses, and saw dear Mr. Ross, who was looking indescribably changed – alas! – since his paralytic stroke. Horne came to dinner.

X X X

Monday Jan. 30. 1899.

Rainy day. I stayed in bed all the morning, and read Maeterlinck and Mommsen and Macaulay. Bernhard lunched with the Placcis to meet Ferrero. Maud Cruttwell lunched here.
