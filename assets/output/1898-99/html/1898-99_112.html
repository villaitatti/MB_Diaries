Saturday Dec. 10. 1898. Frullino Florence.

Mrs. Fairchild with Blair and Satey came to lunch. The far-famed Satey struck us as [written: a] rather ugly, with a touch of vulgarity in her looks, and not interesting – a great disappointment. Earlier we had been with Placci to see the Alessandri pictures. Lina came to lunch too. Bernhard went with Miss Blood to the Boboli Gardens, and then took Blair to call on Madame Rasponi.

Michael Field writes that she has been so gloriously creative this autumn – “I could have sung with the sons of morning when they arose and sang together.” And to write this to me, when all our quarrel came from our saying we didn’t care for her poetry – !
