Bernhard arriving at the same time on their bicycles. Mrs. Scott-Barber told us many stories of the “closeness” of the little Poet Laureate. He arranged with a stable that he should have a carriage and horses for £310 a month, and he insisted on having a reduction for months that hadn’t the full complement of 31 days. Miss Cruttwell called.

Saturday Oct. 22.98.

Mr. and Mrs. Benn came to lunch, and Benn and Bernhard and Bertie went for a long and delightful walk. Alys and I bicycled down to Florence and shopped.
