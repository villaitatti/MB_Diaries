Friday Dec. 16. 1898. Frullino. Florence.

We had a most divine walk – an old Fiesole “rampart walk”. The day was en - chant - ing. A telegram came from Alys saying that Benjamin Frank Conn Costelloe [written: B.F.C.C.] will not make trouble if I put off my home-coming till the 10th. As I might not have been able to go at all, this is a pure gain, and it made me happy. But I was happy anyhow with Bernhard and the sunshine.

Fairchild came and gave me a lesson, and at 7.15 I met Trevy who arrived from England with a heavy bag of books. He was very nice in the evening.
