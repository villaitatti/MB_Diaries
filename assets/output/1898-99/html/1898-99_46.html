Monday June 6. 1898. Frullino, Florence

Mr. Arthur Galton came to lunch, and we liked him very much. He is the nearest to an “Altamuran” we have come across. I took Etta to Santa Croce.

Tuesday June 7. 1898.

Mr. Ward called, and we all spent the afternoon, with the Kerr-Lawsons, at the Gamberaia. ’Twas most beautiful. Mr. Galton came to dinner.

Wednesday June 8. 1898.

Music. Took Etta to Santa Maria Novella and called to say goodbye to Lina. Maud Cruttwell came to dinner. Very hot.

Thursday June 9. 1898.

The Kerr-Lawsons and Mr. Galton to dinner.
