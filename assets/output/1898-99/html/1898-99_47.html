Saturday June 11. 1898.

Bernhard’s dealer from Rome (Cesare Magni, by name!) came bringing a lovely Rembrandt he bought, and some Japanese bronzes. Later came Torrini with a Sienese portrait, which Bernhard also bought. The Rosses came to tea – also an American named Rush and his wife and a friend.
