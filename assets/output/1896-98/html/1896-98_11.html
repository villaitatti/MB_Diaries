Sunday Nov. 1.96.

Bernhard planned out a good deal of his Central Italian Painters in the morning, while I worked on the lists. He is going to begin with a dissertation upon the pleasure of recognizing our own visual images — the secret of all minor painting — and apply this to the early Sienese - who please no longer, because our furniture of visual imagery has changed since the days when they painted. Coming to Perugino, he will have a dissertation on space composition - and the Finale will be Raphael, who gives us our still current visual imagery and ideal space composition.

We are much worried about the noisiness of the gardeners’ children, and hardly know what to do. The place suits us so well otherwise, and it would be hard to find another.
