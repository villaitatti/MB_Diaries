Sunday Sept. 19. 1897. Karlsruhe

Went to the Gallery in the morning and confirmed our conviction that the mysterious picture was painted by Pier Francesco Fiorentino. Finished Miss Paget’s article, and had great fun laughing over it. In the evening we heard The Trojans at Carthage, and enjoyed it very much – though on the whole, not so much as when it was revived with [Marie] Delna as Dido in Paris. Her voice made a great difference. Then in Paris it was judiciously shortened – and the setting was more gorgeous and more classic. One improvement they had here was the ballet of wood and water sprites and fauns during the exquisite Cave scene. The orchestra was perfect here - it was ten minutes of pure and intense joy.

Saturday Oct. 30.97. Villa Rosa Fiesole

I had a week here with Mother and Grace [Worthington] and her children. Bernhard was in Sicily and Rome with the Gardners. He arrived this evening, looking well.

Sunday Oct. 31.97

Settling in and unpacking notes. We walked to the Tree in glorious weather. Bernhard is truly good.

Monday Nov. 1. 1897.

We talked all morning and walked round the Caves in the afternoon. I slept for a few moments in a hollow of the ground. I am indescribably unhappy.
