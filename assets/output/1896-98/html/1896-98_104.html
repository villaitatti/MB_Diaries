Thursday April 1st. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.

Quarrels with Kraus over the intolerable nuisance of his Brass Band kept us peppering hot. Herbert Horne came to dinner and to spend the night, quiet but nice as usual.

Friday April 2.’97.

I had my music lesson, and then hurried up to have tea with Placci, who was there longing for persecution and the martyr’s crown. But we only laugh at him for his Catholicism – which is a great disappointment. Mr. Morgan called.
