Wednesday June 9. 1897.

Placci, Count Papafava, and Mr. Jeffreason called. The latter [The sentence breaks off here and the entry was left incomplete.]

Thursday June 10. 1897.

Finished our business with Mr. Eyre, and took the two Villas in the Via Camerata for 3 years.

Friday June 11. 1897.

Lunched with Placci. He and Buonamici played us Tschaikowski’s Symphonie pathétique – a beautiful thing – but I am not sure we should really like it when we knew it.
