[Thursday April 29. 1897.]

We saw the museo of Forlì, and made acquaintance with the painter, Baldassare Carrari.

Bernhard says my idea of freedom is “the lack of inhibition to an impulse.”

Friday April 30. 1897. Risorgimento. Bari

Saw Trani and Giovinazzo, and came here. At Giovinazzo we found a Lotto for sale – the middle panel of a triptych, a seated bishop. Late and in poor condition. Saw Bari.
