I went in to a concert, with Janet –a trio, giving Mozart, Haydn and Beethoven – good music but poor performers. In the evening we read the Bible – Genesis ix-xviii, and laughed over it a great deal. Then I read Placci’s new book of stories, Mondo Mondano, very vulgar and poor - absolutely fausse route.

Sunday Dec. 19. 1897.

Mr. Arnold Dolmetsch and ‘Elodie’, came to lunch, and Janet after, with whom, after the others were gone, we had a gloriously beautiful walk round the Caves. Continued our Genesis in the evening.
