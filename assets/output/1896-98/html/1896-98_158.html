Thursday Nov. 11. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.

Mr. Benn came to lunch. He said that from the letters of Mrs. Browning it came out that she was six years older than her husband – “and now I understand” – he said – “why she loved him so much”. He then went on to say that the reason husbands usually love their wives so much more than wives their husbands (!) is because the wife is usually younger.
