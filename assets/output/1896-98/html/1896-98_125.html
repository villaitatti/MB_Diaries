no entries after May 6 until May 27:

Thursday May 27.97, Villa Rosa.

I will not say anything about this interval, which has beenfull of sorrow. We are now busy house-hunting, as we are driven out by Kraus’ Brass Band, and today we looked at villas at La Pietra, Montughi, and Castello. But I think we shall stick to our own Fiesole hill. Mr. Morgan came to call, and remained to dinner. We read Pater’s “Demeter and Persephone” after he had gone.
