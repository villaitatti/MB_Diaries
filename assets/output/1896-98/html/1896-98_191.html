Thursday Dec. 23.97.

Moving continued. Four men at work all day, both days. The books are “un vero patrimonio”. I called for a few moments on Horne, who is up, but not yet well, and then met Bernhard at 6.

Friday Dec. 24.97.

The smell in the Frullino turns out to be – rotten eggs. We predicted many evils – but this we could not predict! We “moved” all day, but are still sleeping here.
