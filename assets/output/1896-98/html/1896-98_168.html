Saturday Nov. 20. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.

We grappled with the new Villas.

Sunday Nov. 21. 1897.

Bernhard called at Poggio and Logan and I at the Gamberaia. We all met at the Kerr-Lawsons, Villa Mazzaratta [i.e. Mezzaratta], on the Settignano road. Coming back we found Edith Thomas just arrived, her letter having missed.
