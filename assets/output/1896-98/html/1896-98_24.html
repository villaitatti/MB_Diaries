Thursday, Nov. 12 [written: 11]. 1896, Fiesole

I raged about Florence, trying to find paper to mount our photographs. We had a nice talk in the evening.

Friday, Nov. 13 [written: 12]. 1896

Still raging about for paper. I had my first music lesson from Buonamici, he is so dear and kind, he made it pass off very well. I came up in the 4 o’clock tram and found Bernhard showing Verrocchio photographs to the Countess Rasponi, and Mr. Morgan spoiling the fun. They all came over to tea. The Rasponi uttered some art theories which sounded like Paget-cum-Pasolini prescriptions. Placci came to dinner.
