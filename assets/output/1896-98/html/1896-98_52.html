Friday Dec. 18.96. Fiesole

We thought afterwards of the time-honoured warning “Lay hands suddenly on no man.”But it is done. Che sarà, sarà. After my music lesson, Logan and I called on the Jeaffresons at the Angeli. They have made the house most comfortable, modo inglese. In the evening we worked to brutalizing over the new edition of the Venetians. It is an awful task!
