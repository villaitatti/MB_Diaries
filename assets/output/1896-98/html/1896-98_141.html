Tuesday June 29. 1897. Paris

We went to Pierrefondsto see the Reinachs, and spent the day with them – not getting home till midnight! Reinach drove us over to Compiègne, where we found, in the museum, a Niccolò d’Alunno and a Giannicolo Manni.

Wednesday June 30. 1897. Paris

Bernhard dined with Mme. de Montebello, and enjoyed himself very much. Tomorrow we cross to England.
