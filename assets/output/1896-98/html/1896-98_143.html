Sunday Sept. 5.97. Preussischerhof. Limburg am Lahn

Practically all the day travelling, but we had a splendid view of the Cathedral (in the rain) and were very much impressed. Resolved not to drink German Sect-wine again. It is bad champagne.

Monday Sept. 6. 1897. Bahnhof. Darmstadt

Pouring rain all day, but we went to Wiesbaden and saw the miserable gallery. Our only discoveries were a Scarsellino and a Jacopo del Sellaio. We arrived here about 6 and had a nice talk about the books of our youth.

Tuesday Sept. 7. 1897. Germania. Karlsruhe

We “did” the Galleries of Darmstadt and Mannheim and came here just in time for the opera “The Magic Flute” – a most bewildering affair to hear for the first time.
