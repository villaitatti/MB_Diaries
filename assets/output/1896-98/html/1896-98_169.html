Florence. Bernhard called on Villaris.

Tuesday Nov. 23.97

Mr. Kerr-Lawson came to lunch. He told of a New Englander who was found drunk, and explained his state by saying he had “seven generations of Puritan ancestors to drown out.” Edith went to the Uffizi and I did errands.

Wednesday Nov. 24.97.

Ill with a cold – grappled with the cypresses at the Frullino.

Thursday, Nov. 25, 1897

Music lesson. Took Edith to Poggio Gherardo.
