Wednesday Nov. 10. 1897.

With Janet and Horne we went again to see the Pollaiuolo fresco. Bernhard had tea at the Villari’s, and then dined at the Gamberaia. Janet and I dined at Monte Fiano. The Morgans were dull, but the walk there and back in the moonlight was enchanting, in spite of a cold wind, which has sprung up. I find Janet, on the whole, quite as nice as the average person, and to me nicer, for I feel so at ease with her. And in a kind of way I love her.
