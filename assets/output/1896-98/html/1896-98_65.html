Wednesday Feb. 3. 1897.

We called on Miss Blood, all three of us, and found her ill. I think she has the same trouble that I had last spring, and I offered to bring Dr. Poggi, and to insist on his examining her. The poor unmarried women on the Continent have to endure all their internal ills without hope of remedy because it isn’t etiquette for doctors to examine them. It is a strange relic! In the evening Bernhard dined with the Ogilvys and discussed the relative merits of Marion Crawford and Rudyard Kipling – !
