Friday March 12.97. Villa Rosa. Fiesole

I had my music lesson, and then Eugénie and I had tea at Miss Godkin’s with the two deadly Robins – chickens of the intellect, unhatched in taste – lively ladies who aren’t so grown up as Ray and Karin.

* Saturday Mar. 13. 1897.
