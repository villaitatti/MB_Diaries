The discovery, like Newton’s apple, Watts’ tea-pot, etc., came by accident. A plasterer happened to be at work in the Capitol when Kit was looking at a certain statue there. She noticed that she enjoyed looking at it more while the plasterer was tapping than when he was not. The tapping heightened her enjoyment. Hence the way to enjoy statues and pictures was to tap while you were looking - to tap rhythmically.
