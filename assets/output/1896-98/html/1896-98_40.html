We laughed a good deal over Maud Cruttwell’s finding “Aglavaine et Sélysette” a “problem play”, in which the “teaching” was that a ménage à trois would be a very beautiful thing “if you could rise above the body.”

The walk home in the starlight with a lantern was delicious.

Tuesday Dec. 1st. 1896. Villa Rosa. Fiesole

The Signora Triulzi is doing Bernhard’s photographs, mounting them. I heard from home that the children are to be taken to Paris for Christmas. Poor dears! They hate it. They would infinitely rather be in the country with me. Their father does it simply to be hateful. But I think Grandma and I can make them have a very good time there, all the same.
