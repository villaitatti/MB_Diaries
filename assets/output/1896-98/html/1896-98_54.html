Pleasant as the day was, the evening was best of all, for Logan read us “The Poetry of Shakspeare” (selected for the Golden Urn). It was delightful to be lifted away at once from everything sordid. Then he read us his arrogant introduction – a delicious bit of writing – and some of his “Tragic Walks”, which we really liked. We decided on the contents of the first number, which will soon be issued.

Tuesday Dec. 22. 1896. Fiesole.
