Monday March 1. 1897.

We worked on the Lists. In the afternoon Bernhard and Miss Sellers took a walk, and I called on Miss Taylor and Miss Robins – both terribly boring. Miss Robins has a horrible way of beginning a new sentence before she has finished the old – so that her hearer cannot get in a word. She is perhaps the most awful bore I know – though Mrs. Buttles and Mrs. Horne run her hard.

Miss Sellers told us about Miss Jane Harrison once lecturing on “Pelus and Tantalops.”
