Blake and Milton. Mrs. Perry and Mrs. Tyson came to tea. We went to a charming concert of the Rosé quartette in the evening. 
Sunday April 11. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.
We had a morning of woe through Kraus’ Band, but as Ferri, the keeper of the Uffizi drawings came to see Bernhard, the Band didn’t so much matter. After lunch we called on Mrs. Ross. Then Bernhard walked over to the Gamberaia and had a pleasant flirtation with Miss Blood. I had a headache and came back. Mr. Blaydes met me on the way and we had a little talk – chiefly consisting of silence on his part. But he said he stood in such mortal terror of boring us, he never dared to say anything.
