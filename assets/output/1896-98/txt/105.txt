Saturday April 3. 1897. 
I went to town with Mr. Blaydes, and among other things saw a lawyer who said that legally I might sue Kraus to return my money, or stop the nuisance of his Band. Fabbri came to dinner, the same as usual, serious and hermit-like – a  real hermit. Depressed, too, as usual, but particularly nice.
Sunday April 4. 1897.
