Saturday, March 20. 1897 Fiesole
Bernhard lunched with the Perrys, and found Mrs. Perry more agreeable than he had anticipated. I called on Miss Blood and Miss Paget - the latter was out. Herbert Horne came up to dinner and to spend the night, and we had a quiet pleasant evening talking “shop” and music.
Sunday March 21. 1897. 
Terrible discovery that the Morelli “Christ” and Donaldson’s Madonna are not genuine Botticellis!! Studied photos in the morning.
