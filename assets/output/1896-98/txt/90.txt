Monday Mar. 15. 1897. Fiesole
My hand is rather bad, but I was distracted from the pain by Eugénie’s most entertaining accounts of her various “affaires”. I saw her off to Rome at 5 o’clock, with the most serious and persistent of these, Arthur Strong. 
Tuesday Mar. 16.97
