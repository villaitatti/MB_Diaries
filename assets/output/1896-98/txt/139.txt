Sunday June 27. 1897. Paris
Went to the Louvre, but I had to come back and lie down, not feeling well.
X   X 
X
We lunched with Burke at the restaurant Maire (Boulevard Strassburg) and had some special Burgundy, de la maison – called, I think, Charbonnier. Then I came back to rest, while Burke and Bernhard talked and settled, I hope, a career for Bernhard’s brother.
