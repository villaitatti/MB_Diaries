Afterwards Bernhard said it was as if some one had told Buonamici “How much this  plain-chant  sounds like Gypsy music!” Janet Dodge came to spend the night. She reminds me of Frau von der Hellen, and is almost as  ungezogen  in the naive way she shows her instinctive jealousy of every other female, carefully choosing out their most vulnerable points for attack, or minimizing any praise they may chance to receive. However, her sexual instincts stand her in better stead with men, for Bernhard said she was rather nice in a walk she took with him. But we both feel it would be rather a relief never to see her again. 
Wednesday Nov. 11 [written: 10]. 1896. Fiesole
