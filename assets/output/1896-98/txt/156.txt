Tuesday Nov. 9. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole. 
In the morning I wrote to Wilfrid Blaydes, who is at Siena, telling him I had told Bernhard all our story. In the afternoon we went, with Janet Dodge, to the new Villas, and Miss Blood and Horne met us, and we consulted about furnishings.
