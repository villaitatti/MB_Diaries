In the evening we started by being low, but we had a long talk, and one of the sincerest we have ever had. It cleared away a little of my despair, and it made me understand and love Bernhard more than ever.
Friday Nov. 19. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.
We went to the new house and raged at the workmen. Logan arrived at 5.30 and we had a most amusing evening of chat. Miss Fletcher had told him all sorts of amusing stories of people in Venice, and in what she calls “Hell on The Hill” – Asolo. In one palace in Venice is a man living who tried to cut his throat.
