Sebastian Melmoth. He says “he is very susceptible to personal influence … At present under the feeling that every man’s hand is against him, he is utterly demoralized, and is going as straight as he can to the devil. He is provided with money for the present (which he uses chiefly to that end), and just now he is making a very foolish trip to Taormina for a few days. He says that when he comes back he does really mean to try to pull himself round – but he is mixed up with rascally people, and I fear he will find great difficulty.” Poor Oscar! 
In the evening we read  Wordsworth , and the first 8 chapters of  Genesis , which we enjoyed vastly.
The illustrated edition of Bernhard’s “Venetian Painters” came today, and was nicer than we feared.
