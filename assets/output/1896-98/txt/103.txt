Then I called on another such, Miss Robins, and then came gladly home. Mr. Blaydes arrived from Rome, thinner and nicer looking than before, and we had a pleasant little chat.
Tuesday March 30. 1897.
Worked as usual. In the afternoon I had a walk with Mr. Blaydes and Bernhard went to drive with Miss Robins. 
Wednesday March 31. 1897.
Miss Robins, D. S. MacColl and his charming French wife came to call, but first Dr. Richter and Miss Taylor came to lunch. Afterwards we had a walk.
