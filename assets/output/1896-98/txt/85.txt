Saturday March 6. 1897 
Lists etc. in the morning. Janet Dodge came to lunch to see the dressmaker – Rosa’s niece, who is here. We chatted a little after lunch while Logan and Bernhard went over the manuscript of his “Central Italians”. After tea they called on the Countess Rasponi, and Miss Sellers and I took a long walk. In the evening we revised and re-enjoyed our selections from Milton.
* Sunday March 7. 1897 
The Miss Robins came to lunch and we were bored mad and blind with their incessant double river of talk. Whew!!! Mr. Morgan called and seemed sad when we said his offered contributions  would not do  for the Golden Urn. If he only knew the comic of his having sent us such things and how we roared over them! Read Milton in the evening.
