Thursday April 15. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.
I slept very late and enjoyed my bath, Christina [Bremner] assisting. Miss Priestley came to lunch and was perfectly fascinating – so witty and entertaining. She told such good stories – the English clergyman travelling who described himself as “un prêtre anglais avec deux femmes” and the clergyman’s wife who wanted “un chapeau très tranquille, parce que je suis la femme d’un prêtre. Je le veux avec une rose en avant et un plumeau derrière.” She said that when she was little she always thought the curate in black gown was meant for the devil, while the chaplain in white meant God.
