Wednesday March 10.97.  Karin’s 8th birthday
We went to see the Cantagalli factory with Horne and met and liked very much Signor Cantagalli. We got awfully tired, and  Mrs. Horne  at tea was anything but refreshing!
Thursday March 11. 1897.
We went with Eugénie to the Pitti and the Boboli Gardens, and enjoyed it very much. She has been enchanting all day.
