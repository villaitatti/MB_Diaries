Friday May 28. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole
Mr. Morgan came to lunch and went with Bernhard to see the Villa Guarnieri, while I went down to my music  lesson . Afterwards I met Mr. Head by chance in the Annunziata and had a pleasant chat with him, and then I called on Roger Fry and his wife. Bernhard called on Mr. Benn, and we came up together in the 6.40 tram. 
Saturday May 29. 1897. 
Bernhard went to be painted by Mr. Kerr-Lawson,  and I called for him about 5 and we drove to the Gamberaia. Miss Paget and Mr. Graham were there.
