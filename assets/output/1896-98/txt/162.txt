Dear Bernhard comforted me very much, and he could not help saying it was an original situation for  him . But he was equal to it. The train time came and went, and Wilfrid was gone. He said I should never see him again.
Mr. Morgan came in as I was weeping, and I had to pretend it was a cold. 
Monday Nov. 15.97. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.
