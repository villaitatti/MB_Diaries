Wednesday June 2. 1897. Fiesole
The Kerr-Lawsons came to lunch. It was awfully hot. I went down later with Mr. Kerr-Lawson and chose a piece of brocade for the dress in which he is to paint me. Bernhard called on the Jeffreasons, and then walked to the Tree.
Thursday June 3. 1897. 
We had a miserable day talking. In the evening we went to the Morgans to supper, and walked home by the flashes of lightning and the dance of fireflies.
