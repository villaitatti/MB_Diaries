I could not write down the simplest thing from hearing it. Poor Janet, hearing that Herbert Horne was coming to dinner, determined to stay and drive home with him. First she complained about going out in the rain, and bored me to death about the weather. Finally, she got a chill and was sick at her stomach (whether really, or only in report, to melt my heart, I don’t know, but I suspect the latter). It seemed cruel to turn her out, for she lingered on and on to the last tram.
