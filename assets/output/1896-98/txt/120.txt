Sunday April 18. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole
The whole party, with Horne and Perry, went up to the Monte Senario, but I did not feel very well, and so stayed at home and led a quiet life. I called to see Reno, who is awfully ill, poor beastie.
Monday April 19. 1897. 
Bernhard took the Reinachs, etc., to the Pitti, lunched with them, and drove with them to La Quiete. I drove to town with Blaydes to get medicine for Reno, and errands for myself. I was awfully sleepy in the evening, and Cook played so horribly on the piano it nearly drove me crazy.
