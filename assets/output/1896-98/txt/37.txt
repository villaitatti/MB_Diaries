Saturday Nov. 28.96. 
A telegram came from Mrs. Gardner saying she would take the Velasquez. I rejoiced in the thought of the money, but Bernhard did not feel any  real  pleasure in it. Mr. Morgan came to lunch, and stayed hours afterward capping commonplaces with me. I like him, but a little goes a long way. Then came the Lovetts, and finally to my horror,  Mrs. Horne  and her daughter. Bernhard was down in town hunting for a picture-frame and calling on Mr. Benn. When the Hornes had finally gone, Logan and I walked down to the Palmerino. Miss Paget was  en veine  and very charming.
