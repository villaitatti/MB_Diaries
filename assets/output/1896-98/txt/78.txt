Friday Feb. 26. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole 
Bernhard called on the Perrys and the Benns, and came home with an awful cold. Logan and Miss Sellers chatted and bicycled. I was much worried by letters saying Miss Clare was disagreeable to mother, and so I spent most of the day writing to her and Alys and mother to try to improve matters. We chatted in the evening. Mrs. Gardner wants the Rembrandt Landscape!
Saturday Feb. 27. 1897. Fiesole
Both our colds awful. We stodged away over lists, being fit for nothing else. 
Sunday Feb. 28. 1897. 
The same tale. In the evening Bernhard and Miss Sellers ‘schimpfed’ Mr. Arthur Strong.
