Friday Nov. 5. 1897.
Worked in the morning, cataloguing notes etc. Bernhard called on Dr. Baldwin and Mr. Benn in the afternoon and I had a walk. The weather glorious. In the evening we read long letters from Miss Anstruther-Thomson and Miss Paget denying that they had ever taken anything from Bernhard’s conversation or writings.
