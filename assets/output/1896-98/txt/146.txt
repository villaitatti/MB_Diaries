Saturday Sept. 11 1897. Germania. Karlsruhe
While Bernhard had his lesson, I took a lovely ride in the forest, exploring narrow shady paths, haunted only by squirrels and birds. It was enchanting! Bernhard had another lesson in the afternoon, and then finished  Flaubert’s Théatre . I began Prescott’s  Phillip II .
Sunday Sept. 12. 1897. Karlsruhe
Read  Egmont  and  Gibbon  – walked, heard  Tannhäuser  in the evening – but did not care for it much as music. As drama it is excellent, except that certain situations are unduly prolonged.
