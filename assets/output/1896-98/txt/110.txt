We had an enchanting walk to our Tree, the air and sunshine perfect – “A day in April never came so sweet.” Coming back we found Miss Taylor and her deadly, deadly parents, who paid an unduly long call. Worked in the evening. 
* Thursday. April 8. 97. Villa Rosa. Fiesole
Bernhard went with Horne to the drawings, lunched with him, then to the Corsini and Palazzo Vecchio, and then to call on Benn.
