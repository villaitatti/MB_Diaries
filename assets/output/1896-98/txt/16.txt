He has had two suggestive ideas already, both suggested by analogies in painting — namely that harmony is like modelling, and that the conventional endings of music (often a page or two long) are like the framing in of pictures. I decided to take music lessons from Buonamici, and went to bed quite excited with the idea. 
* Friday Nov. 6. 1896. Fiesole.
Still rain and rain and rain! A quiet day, with no people, but books and work.
Saturday Nov. 7. 1896.
We went down to Placci’s at 11 to hear him play with Buonamici - who, however, did not turn up. So we talked about music and he played from “The Magic Flute”. I asked why one wants a “frame” in time. The field of vision, being limited, naturally suggests a frame, but why time, or breath?
