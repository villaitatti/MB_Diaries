Wednesday Nov. 17. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole
Bernhard had his massage, and then called on Miss Blood, who told him he had made an unfavorable impression on Mme. de Montebello and the Princess Ghika the night he dined there because he spoke slightingly of Amiel and Marie Bashkirtseff,  whom they were praising. I had the day alone, practising and being unhappy. I read Dr. Bucke’s  Man’s Moral Nature . Bernhard is reading  Lucretius  and  Pindar .
