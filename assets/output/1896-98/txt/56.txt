Monday Jan. 18. 1897. Villa Rosa. Fiesole.
We spent the day in Pisa seeing the sights, and reached here for a late dinner. Logan was eager to hear all Alys’ and Bertie’s American gossip, which I had picked up from them in London. 
* Tuesday Jan. 19.97.
Began a regular life again, music, photographs, printing Bernhard’s manuscript, etc. In the afternoon we went to see the “Eremo della Schifa-nebbia” - an enchanted spot.Bernhard was much taken with the idea of passing as ‘le bel hermitte’ [i.e. ermite]. In the evening we read Keats. 
Wednesday Jan. 20.97. 
We walked up to the Morgans in the afternoon. The little boys were so nice.
