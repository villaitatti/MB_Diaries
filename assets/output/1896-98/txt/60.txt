Wednesday Jan. 27. 1897. Fiesole.
I went to see Janet, and listened to Bach on the spinet for a couple of hours, enjoying it keenly. We then called on the Hornes. Bernhard walked over to the Gamberaia to see Miss Blood, to whom he is teaching Greek. As usual, we read Milton all the evening. Logan and I are learning “Lycidas”.
.
Thursday Jan. 28. 1897. 
Mr. Marsh (Bertie’s friend) and his sister came to lunch, also Miss Blood, whom Logan and I took to call on Miss Paget. The call went off very well, though the Sibyl Palmerina plunged into deep discourse the moment we entered and quite took Miss Blood’s breath away. Walking home Logan and I saw over a Villa with a divine facade and loggia which is for sale. Milton in the evening.
