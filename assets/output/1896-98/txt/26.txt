With this bee in their bonnets they revisited the galleries and found the tunes for their favourite pictures. Unfortunately, Miss Thomson being thoroughly unmusical and Miss Paget very limited in her acquaintance with tunes, most of the pictures sang to Gluck and Mozart. 
And this they talked of for nearly a year with bated breath as a “great discovery”, and pitied poor us because we were still stumbling in outer darkness. But it is too funny. I can hardly laugh. 
Saturday Nov. 14.96. Fiesole
