Friday May 8. 1903. I Tatti Settignano

Lunched at Mrs. Ross’ to meet a rich American, Mr. Rogers, whom Bernhard positively abhorred. While we were there a telegram came from Jephson saying he was coming to us tomorrow. We both think he is in trouble, engagement fallen through, or something. But I hopenot. I hope it is only friendliness. Still, I can never have any confidence again that he is really frank with me, and his not telling me of his engagement until after 5 months has put a complete barrier between any intimate even friendship, although
