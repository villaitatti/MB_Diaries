under the shadow of some heavy sorrow, poor man. On Friday I rode and drove with the children from Burgess Hill to Billingshurst. Horne dined with Bernhard. On Saturday we went to the Blunt’s annual sale of Arab horses at Crabbet Park. Sultans and Maharajahs and Royal Princesses and Duchesses were there, but also Dr. Williamson and Bernhard Shaw. We consorted with our kind, with Fry and Percy Feilding, the Trevys and the Lyttons. We met the children at Petworth and drove here, they riding. Yesterday laziness - today ditto and Maude Robertson to dinner, who, this time, was really charming.
