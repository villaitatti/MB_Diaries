Yesterday I began to read Ricci’s “Pintoricchio” for review. Bernhard is soaking himself in Franciscan literature. I called on the Chapmans (out), and Gertrude, and on Mr. Cannon; and in the morning we called on the Duse’s and had little chat with her nice daughter.

Today I called on Mrs. Ross, who came back with me. Mr. Lestrange came, and Mr. Cannon with Mrs. Volmer. Then came the Duse, her daughter and the daughter’s friend - both of them studying to be nurses. The Duse was very gracious to Mr. Cannon, and when he went she was fascinating.
