Norman said at lunch that the only interesting thing to him about sculpture was to be told the precise muscles that were brought into play in certain poses, and be shown how the artist treated them; and, in regard to pictures to have it explained to him how the paint had been put on --!! B.B. remained discreetly silent.

After lunch we walked to La Doccia, which was lovely. B.B. and Don Guido drove. Don Guido told me that Alice Blaydes said to him that she didn’t care at all about her husband; and said that as soon as she left here she wrote to him begging him to come down to Florence to see her!!! If her silly husband knew that --
