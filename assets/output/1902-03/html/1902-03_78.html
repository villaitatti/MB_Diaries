I Tatti, Wednesday, Nov. 5. 1902. *

Wrote to Roger Fry, Miss Toplady, Don Guido, Mother.

Mrs. Wilfrid Blaydes arrived early in the morning. It is an experiment having her here, as we really do not know her at all - only her looks which are very pretty. Once I should have hated her! Now her husband is no more to me than a tree - except that as a human being I wish him well, and that I would do a good deal for him in a friendly way. But as an intelligent man, I regard him as finished. He is so hopelessly lazy and self- indulgent. Talented he was, but the talent is wrapped in a thicker napkin every year of his life, and boundless arrogance and intellectual impatience alone remain.
