Wednesday May 27. 1903. I Tatti

D’Annunzio called this morning to thank B.B. for a letter he had sent him about his new book, the Laudi,which he had sent with the dedication: “A Bernardo Berenson - all’alto e libero spirito che naviga i mari lontani questo poema navale è fraternamente offerto”.

He said he was resting till June and was then going to write 3 plays, one called “Atalanta Baglioni”(founded on Matarazzo), another a problem play, and another a peasant, legendary play of unconscious swiftly reacting people.
