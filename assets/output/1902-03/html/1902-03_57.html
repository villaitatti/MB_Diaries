Wednesday June 25. 1902..

Weather still heavenly. King evidently dying, but the people must eat their feasts. Bertie is grown much older with the responsibility of Alys’ illness, and has become very charming and interesting. He feels intolerably alone, for no one understands his longing to find the art, a symbol, a religion which shall reconcile the intellect of man with the universe. He talked to Bernhard about it for hours, and was, B.B. said, most interesting and winning. I am afraid such a solution is out of our grasp - at any rate now. Yet we can’t go back to the compact, clear mediaeval system.

The happy person does not need it, and the wise person, growing older, does not dwell on his need.
