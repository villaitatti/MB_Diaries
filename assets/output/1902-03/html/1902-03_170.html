Thursday March 12. 1903. Settignano

Busy writing B.B.’s letters. Mr. Murray drove down with me to town. I called on the Hornes and told Herbert that Bell had written to us to say they were going to publish his famous long-delayed “Botticelli”as a Fragment. They have had the plates and part of the text ready for nine years.

We met Miss Jane Harrison at 5.30. It was quite flat. I felt at once she was not “our kind”, in spite of Alys and Bertie’s enthusiasm. The evening seemed long and dull, and a moonlight walk I took her before tea was deadly!
