* * *

Saturday July 4. 1903. Promontogno. Hotel Bregaglia

Another month! I am lazy. It was delicious weather up till about the 25, and then it became hot. B.B. went to Bagni di Lucca and persuaded the Hapgoods to take a house there. Mrs. Ross and I followed him, after I had got the Villa in order. We saw various people during that last month Horrible Jews named Friederwald, dull well meaning Van Dyck, writer on Art, the dear Houghtons, Kitty Hall and her sister, etc. A pleasant 4 days together at Bagni, and then B.B. and I drove across to Pracchia and came on to Milan, taking to Cavenaghi our Turas and Giottino.

Yesterday we came here, snatched from the fire of Italy. Bernhard’s book is out!!!
