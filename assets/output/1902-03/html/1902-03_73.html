Saturday, November 1. 1902.

Wrote to Mother, Don Guido, Donna Carmelita, Grace (about Costa), Maude Robertson, Lina and Reinach. Bernhard also wrote a lot of letters chiefly business.

Scott speaks of “that hysterical passion which forces unbidden sighs and tears, and falls upon a contented life like a drop of ink on white paper, which is not the less a stain because it conveys no meaning.”
