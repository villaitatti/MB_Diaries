Pensione Saracco, Via Sal[l]ustio Bandini, Siena, Wednesday, March 26. 1902.

I do not seem very successful in keep a journal. It is partly that Bernhard talks less, and partly that things don’t seem so particularly important.

The event of this month as been the reappearance of the radiant Gladys, so beautiful, so brilliant - “with her soft elixir ways”, her hard clear youthful logic, her gaminerie, her lively imagination, her moods, her daring. It would take volumes to describe her, and I don’t feel up to it.
