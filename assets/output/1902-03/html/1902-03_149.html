Monday, Feb. 2. 1903.

Worked on Index.Emily and I like it. B.B. dreamt he heard Dr. Giglioli say, “Yes - poor fellow, he was making all his plans, but I knew all the time he was going to die.” But he isn’t. I wrote a long letter to Roger Fry explaining his position about that disgusting Langton Douglas.

Called with Emily in the afternoon on Miss Ede at Novoli, a nice girl. Called also on Miss Nixon and Gertrude and Mrs. Ross. Mrs. Ross was regretting her son’s recovery from pneumonia. I wrote to Gertrude’s cousin in desperation. Dr. Giglioli stayed to dinner.
