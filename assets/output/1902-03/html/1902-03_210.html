His little wife is still waiting to find out her value in the world. She is ready to revolt, but until her book comes out, she doesn’t quite know whether by herself she could make anything of life. She bothers Hutchins about the narrowness of the life he gives her, and he says it’s herfault, because she won’t exert herself to attract and interest people. She is in truth very silent. We had a long, friendly talk about their matrimonial situation, which is strained, there is no doubt. But I think they will pull through.
