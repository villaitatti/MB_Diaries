Tuesday, January 13. 1903. I Tatti

Rain, rain. Worked on proofs, etc. Houghton came to call after lunch, and drove down with me to see a picture - which turned out to be wretched - and help me shop. I accomplished a call upon the Contessa Niccolini - due for more than a year, since her extremely kind hospitality to Bernhard, the wretch, who never went near her, after staying a week at her place near Pisa!

We have been trying to read Celestina(Mabbe’s translation)but are fairly stuck with ennui. Bernhard finds the Cambridge Historypretty boring, but Walpole’sLettersdelightful.
