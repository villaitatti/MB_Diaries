Alice’s head has been rather turned by Don Guido. She actually thinks he would marry her if he were free! Poor child - but how should she know the world? On the whole, I am glad he has gone tonight. Things were getting uncomfortable. He even asked her if she thought it would be honourable of a man to make love to a fellow guest in a friend’s house. Alice has a frankness that is partly naiveté.
