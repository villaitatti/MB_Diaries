Saturday May 10. 1902., I Tatti

Bernhard went to the Gallery in the morning, and I went across to the Via Maggio to see if the antiquary there had found out anything about Mr. Davis’ picture. He said that the cameriereof Count Alberto told him that it was a forgery by Constantini and had been sold about 15 days ago to a rich American.

On the way back I called on Gertrude Morton, who has been ill in bed nearly 3 months. She said she had come to the end of her rope with regard to Rob, and must consult us about what to do. Poor thing, married to that hysterical, boring, uneducated, conceited fool!

In the afternoon we had music, all Bach. It was raining, so only a few people came, but it was delightful.
