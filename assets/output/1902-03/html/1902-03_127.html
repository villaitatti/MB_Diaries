Tuesday, Dec. 30. 1902.

Bernhard is not feeling well these days. The scirocco seems to undo him. I took Alys and Bertie up to Settignano, and Miss Cracroft played to us Bach’s Toccata and Fugue in G minor, the Pastorale, and the Passacaglia. It was pure bliss. Then Ray and Winnie called for me and we drove up to the Morgans’ for lunch, and the girls had fun with the wine-vats, etc.

We read Lamb in the evening.

Last day of 1902. Universo. Lucca

Alys and I came with Ray and Winnie, tired of waiting for sunshine, and saw Pisa, and then came on here. How much did Ray take in of the beautiful things I showed her?
