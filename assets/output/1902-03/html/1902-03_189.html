Was she acting for us? Well, if she was, she did it well. The daughter and friend listened silently grinning like Ray and Winny. I could have kissed them.

In the evening Houghton dropped in, and was very nice.

Mrs. Ross said her one desire was never to see Lina again.

It is delicious to be home again. Bernhard is so nice, and so interesting.
