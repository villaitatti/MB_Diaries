I Tatti Nov. 17. 1902. Monday-

Weather overcast, but warm. Miss Cracroft came to lunch. Had a music lesson while Alice and Zangwill went to the Medici Tombs. Took Zangwill to see Mrs. Ross, who seemed to like him. But then, as she said, ‘She was so lonely she would welcome the devil’, it may not have been a great compliment!

In the evening one or two lights were thrown of Alice’s past. Among other things she said that on Saturdays she always bought “The Pink Un”, and “religiously” saved it up to read in bed on Sunday mornings. Yet she has the speech and the manners of a lady. Zangwill finds her awfully slow in learning Italian. He is good - he devotes hours a day to teaching her. Still she is remarkable good-looking!
