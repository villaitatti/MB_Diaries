Sunday, March 22. 1903. I Tatti

Logan and I called on Mrs. Ross. Mr. Morgan and the Cracrofts called, and then Mrs. Johnson whom we met at Mr. Cannon’s, bringing Miss Macdougall and Jo Smith, the Boston painter.

Mrs. Johnson had had the same impression from Mrs. Wharton that we had received, intolerable sniffiness, rudeness, self-absorption. In the evening we took Horne over with us to dine with Mr. Cannon, who had three young American women, two daughters of Senator Jones, and a Mrs.

Wohner, all interested in education. They looked unpromising, but turned out to be interesting to talk to.
