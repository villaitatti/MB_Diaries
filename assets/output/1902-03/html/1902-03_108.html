I Tatti. Dec. 4. 1902 Thursday.

Calderoni and Guido Pasolini came to lunch – the same age but what a difference. Her head has nothing but sex in it, theirs had that doubtless, but a thousand other things. Calderoni is fairly intelligent. Afterwards I took Alice to her pensione, where she is to learn Italian. It is a great relief to have her away. Neither B.B. nor I had anything left to say to her. Miss Erichsen called here, and Bernhard saw her, but I was at Gertrude’s listening to her sordid tale of woe quarrels all round. I am awfully sorry for her, but I have had almost all I can stand.

Friday Dec. 5. 1902.

Took Mrs. Toy to the Bargello, Uffizi
