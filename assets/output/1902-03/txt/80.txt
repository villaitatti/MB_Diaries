Lina and her husband arrived in the evening. Horne also came to dinner. Lina seemed well and happy, but she entirely takes the lead. It is curious to see B.B.’s and Venetia Cowper’s view justified, that in her marriage she had followed her Aunt’s example and taken another Carlo Orsi.
