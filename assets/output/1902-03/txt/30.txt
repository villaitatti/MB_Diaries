April 19. 1902.. I Tatti, Settignano, Saturday
On the day before we left Siena, April 4, as we were starting out on an excursion to Montisi, Bernhard slipped and fell, and then fainted away on the Siena platform. I thought he was gone - it was a dreadful five minutes. I think it was nothing but the shock, but he has been tired and not himself since …
