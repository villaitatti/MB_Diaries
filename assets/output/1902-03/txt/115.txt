I Tatti, Friday, December 12. 1902.
Corrected proofs. Shopped with Mrs. Trevy and got at last that Aubusson carpet we have been eying for five years! Houghton came with us. He says he wants to give up their new apartment, it is too grand, and  own  nothing he can’t pack into a small hand bag.
Alice Blaydes came up for the night, all agog with a call and a bunch of flowers from Calderoni, and the ogling of the Count of Turin in the Cascine. She really thinks of nothing but such things, and the practical side of life. Called on the Lawsons.
