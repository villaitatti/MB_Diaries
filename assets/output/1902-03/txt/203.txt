She went at 3, and soon after a delicious refreshing storm passed over the country cooling its hot surface and bring out all the hidden dramatic odours of the earth and woods. A Mrs. MacMahon with 2 daughters, one an archaeological student, came to tea, the Thorolds, and Mrs. Hooker (Evelyn’s friend) and her daughter came to dinner.
I quite forgot yesterday - how could I? that Placci came to lunch, and the Serristori and M. Rembelinski for a  4 hours’ call afterwards!
