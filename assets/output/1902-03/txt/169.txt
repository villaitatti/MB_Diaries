Tuesday Mar. 10. 1903.
Miss Ogilvy to lunch. Music afterwards. Miss Hastings and Mrs. Forbes- Mosse sang, but I prefer to hear the Bach.
Wednesday, March 11. 1903.
Zug, melancholy, stupid, nervous Zug came to lunch and poured out his woes. He has been appointed Instructor in Art at Chicago, and now he wants to throw up the job, out of nervousness and incompetence. Gertrude and her nice nun called, and the Brocklebanks, and B.B. had a walk and interesting talk with the Serristori.
The pages of proof came. Vinci - see Leonardo!
