What value, I wonder, does he give to those early, faint memories? They are a part of one’s stream of consciousness”, and a pleasant part. One can never have two first loves [a truism!]
For the rest, we had a quiet day of divine sunshine. Benn called in the morning. In the afternoon I drove with Alice to town, shopped, called on Mme. de Platonoff and Gertrude.
