Wrote to mother, Lina, Miss Hall, Miss Hopkinson, Dowdeswell, Roger. The children and their Grandma are jubilant over Ray’s having won the Cup at the swimming contest. Ray herself writes a letter full of bliss. She seems really very happy. Harold Worthington overheard her, at a cricket match, talking to some of her friends about the tyranny of Ideas. She said if you once got the  Idea that you didn’t care for childish plays, you had to obey it, no matter how you hated it. Harold pities her very much. He thought it was  so sad !
Friday July 10. 1903.
