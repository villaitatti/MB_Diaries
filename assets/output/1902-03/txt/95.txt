Tuesday Nov. 18. 1902
We had a lunch of Virgins of the Rocks, Miss Cruttwell, Miss Cracroft, Christina Bremner. Miss Cracroft played Beethoven to us afterwords - the D minor sonata and some Bagatellen.
The weather has turned bitterly cold, and it snowed a little today.
Zangwill keeps on being nice, and making puns. His latest was a motto for Magna “To thyself be true - And it must follow as 1 night 1 day, Thou can’t nor then be false to Heinemann.”
Wednesday Nov. 19. 1902.
Still cold with high wind. Worked all day. Houghton to dinner. Another story of Forestiere Italian. Lady wanting to drive to “Indian”. “Andate in fondo dell’Arno a poi giurate.” “Ma Signora posso giurare lo stesso qui.”
