Bernhard took Mrs. Toy for a drive. Donna Rezia Corsini and Guido Pasolini called. My cold awful. Bernhard read  Syria and Palestine  by  Pater . Played “Bridge” in the evening.
I gave Alice Blaydes a little wedding present, and she burst into tears and threw herself in my arms, and said no woman had ever been kind to her before. I wonder what her history is? She is only 24 and has been living 2 years with him. He would not have married her, but B.B. and I put on all the pressure we possibly could, and simply made him. He meant well by her I am sure (later: I am not so sure. I think he often meant to throw her off.-  she  is sure of it), and is exceedingly fond of her, but had a doctrinaire hatred of Marriage.
