Gazzada. Tuesday, Oct. 20 
[28]
. 1902..
Last week we made a charming excursion to the Sacromonte, a town that gleams white on the spur of the great blue mountains. The Church at the top is approached by a long Via Sacra, leading past ten or twelve exquisite little chapels - some are like Greek Temples, and others like Raphael’s in the Sposalizio. The view of the white chain of Alps, the lakes spreading at our feet, and “the waveless plains of Lombardy” was too beautiful for me to attempt to find words.
