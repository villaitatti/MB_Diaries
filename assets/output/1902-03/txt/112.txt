We had a Sonata (d) by Corelli (violin), and then the great Passacaglia, which is the most splendid thing I have ever heard. Then Beethoven 111, some Schumann violin, and Bach’s Pastorale. How I do enjoy music. It is my greatest  pleasure . Afterwards, when the people had gone, they played a Brahms sonata and one by Mozart, a composer whom Miss Cracroft abhors!!
Christina remained on for the night. We read a very diverting paper of advice to Ray on how to conduct herself as a step-daughter. I have begun Ranke’s Popes - a  bigger  book than Creighton.
