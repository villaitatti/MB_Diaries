Monday July 7. 1902.. Friday’s Hill
We had a pleasant week here with the Webbs and Bertie, Grace and the children, and then went up. Bernhard lunched on Monday with Sidney Cockerell and Emery Walker. On Tuesday was Lina’s wedding. She was a marvellous vision of beauty. On Wednesday Ray had her swimming match. She won by brains, really, not mere power of swimming, for she thought out her problems, and dived at the most advantageous places and so on. In the evening we dined with the Cooks and were taken to - the Hippodrome! It was clear that Marny Cook owes her “awakening” to Mr. Schiller.
