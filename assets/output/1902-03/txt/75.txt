I Tatti, Nov. 2. 1902. .Sunday
Wrote to Karin, Dent (for Donna Laura’s book), Miss Cracroft, Mr. Leisenring, Linotype Co. and Emily.
Mr. & Mrs. Toy (of Harvard, Prof. of Arabic) came to lunch - a wonderful day. They were agreeable.
Afterwards came the Contessa Serristori and Carlo Placci. She was delightful, so vibrating and graceful, so thoughtful, too, and expressing herself so very well. She is an unusual woman.
Then came Mrs. Ross’ little doctor, Giglioli, who made a thorough examination of B.B., and found nothing organically wrong with him. He hopes to cure him. Read Mrs. Ady’s “Madame” in the evening. A cold coming on, worse luck.
