Bertie told several good stories of mixed metaphors. “The floodgates of irreverence and atheism are stalking hand in hand throughout the land.” “Gentlemen, we are trembling on the verge of a cul-de-sac …” and so forth. We read a little more “Pudd’nhead Wilson” in the evening. After tea I read B.B.’s “Renaissance Churches” to the girls. What did they think? Before they went to bed, they both crawled under the bed, and when I came in to wish them good night, they stuck out their heads with peals of laughter.
