Today we started (after a call from Prof. Uzielli) at 10, and got to Incisa for lunch. At San Giovanni we found a lot of pictures, among them Sellaio’s masterpiece! The day was cool and beautiful for driving.
Albergo Fiorentino Borgo San Sepolcro Tuesday May 19. 1903.
