Sunday June 7. 1903. Settignano
Wrote my review of MacColl’s book on XIX Century Art. Called on Aunt Janet, who was superb. Miss Cracroft.
Monday June 8. 1903.
Looked at the Lawson Villa. Benn to lunch. He says the Lee-Hamilton baby has no proper division in its heart between left and right. It is very serious.Nice drive with Neith and Fafner. I like them.
Tuesday June 9. 1903.
Finished MacColl. Saw Caulfield’s villa. Called on Miss Cracroft and heard Chopin and Bach. Thorolds to dine.
