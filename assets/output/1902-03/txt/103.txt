I Tatti. Tuesday Nov. 25. 1902.
Rain once more, after several enchanted days. Still, Alice and I went over to the Thorolds and took Mrs. Thorold to call on her neighbour, Mrs. Gregory Smith. We also called on Christina Bremner, who is freezing in Maud Cruttwell’s apartment.
Alice is trying to “keep her perpendicular” by arguing that she has raised herself as far above her natural condition as B.B. and I have intellectually raised ourselves. She said that her natural future was that either of a prostitute or a peasant-girl. I find her very nice and sincere and sensible to talk to.
