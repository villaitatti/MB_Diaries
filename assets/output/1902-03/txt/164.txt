Gertrude at the Blue Nuns’  house  of repose. She seemed much better and more composed; but full of her delusions about Miss Lipps. I really do not know what to think. What I feel alas is that I distinctly loathe poor Gertrude. The walk home was too inconceivably beautiful. I never saw a more marvellous red glow in the sky. The day has been  matchless  for weather! On my return I found a telegram from Mounteney saying he sails on the 13th and expects to reach Brindisi on April 24th. I am afraid it would have been wiser for him to stay a good deal longer and complete his cure.
Thursday March 5. 1903. Settignano
