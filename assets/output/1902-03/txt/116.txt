Was in a rage; which was not appeased by the Countess Gravina bringing two horrible German art students to call on B.B. I had them for an hour before he came back. They smelt horrible, and looked worse. I suppose they pass as gentlemen in Germany. I had absolutely nothing to say to the Gravina, nor she to me. After they went, I walked, and called on Mrs. Ross.
Sunday, Dec. 14. 1902. I Tatti
Horne came to lunch. He said his Mother was dangerously ill, and, in fact, when I went in to see her in the afternoon, I found the fat, jolly old lady terribly changed. To both Mrs. Trevy and myself she seemed to have death writ [ten]  on her face.
