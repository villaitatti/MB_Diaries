Monday June 23. 1902.. London
The preparations for the Coronation make London nearly impossible. And who knows if the King will be well enough to be crowned? We had tea with Douglas Ainslie and Bernhard went with him to a Garden Party at Mr. Graham Robertson’s, and then came back to call on Gladys, finding Forbes Robertson literally at her feet. I had tea with Mrs. Baldwin and took her to see Mother. She spent all the time speaking against Gladys’ cruelty and selfishness and strangeness.
