I read Donna Laura’s chapter on Fogazzaro, and found it rather dull and futile, but perjured my soul out in praises of it in the evening. Tu l’as voulu - I might reply if she reproached me, for she and her sister put me in a position where I could do nothing but praise. Donna Carmelita told me that a word of adverse criticism would make her sister take to her bed. They are the most awful, tactless, bad-mannered, embarrassing people I ever came across. But they aren’t bores, so I prefer them greatly to il Casto Naso!
