We drove Winnie down to the English Church and I took Ray to San Marco, and then called on Gertrude. In the afternoon we all walked. Ray seems perfectly happy. Christina came over for the night, and Ray (very shy!) read her paper on the Education Bill, and we had an interesting discussion about it. The paper was not at all bad. It was clear and fairly well arranged, and very un-sectarian.
The Sunsets all these days are very wonderful. They fade at last to the glowing emerald and then dull mauve.
Monday, Dec. 22. 1902.
