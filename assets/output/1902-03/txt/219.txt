Bingham (Minister to Mexico) to Celerina and had tea. Ray is wavering between Classics and Mathematics, and I wrote her a letter in favour of the former.
Tuesday July 14. 1903.
Walked separately in the morning, owing to the usual quarrel about my leaving Bernhard in the lurch and going to the children. It would have been a quarrel, but I walked away.
In the afternoon we walked round by the Meierei to Celerina - a long walk - with our usual companions. M. de Bingham is not remarkably intelligent.
Wednesday July 15. 1903.
Drove with Clyde Fitch and Mr. Noyes to have lunch at the Belvoir. Clyde Fitch will have 19 plays running next autumn! Bernhard and I walked back to the Bad by Pontresina.
Thursday July 16. 03.
Same party walked up along the Morteratsch to a hut - a long walk.
M. Gaudarax, a fat Basque of mild literary leanings was with us.
