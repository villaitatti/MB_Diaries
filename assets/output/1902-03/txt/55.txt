Saturday June 21. 1902.. London.
Bernhard went to the British Museum and I joined him there. We went on to the City and had a “Turtle lunch” with Burke. Bernhard went down to Dorking to the Trevelyans, and I went to bed with a raging headache.
Sunday June 22. 1902. .
Spent the day at Dorking with the Frys. Helen just recovering from her second baby - Pamela- but surprising and charming as ever. The Trevys came over too. Finished  Buckle , Bernhard finished  Bury’s History of Greece .  I have hardly ever enjoyed a book more than Buckle.
