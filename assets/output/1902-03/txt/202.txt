Sunday May 24. 03. I Tatti
Went to see Maud and Gertrude. The Houghtons with Mrs. Bishop their “Beauty” came to tea. Then we three called on Mrs. Ross and had a walk. Lina has had a boy. I received a wire in the morning. Eugénie’s husband, Mr. Strong, appears to be dying of “pernicious anaemia”.
Monday May 25. 03.
Christina Bremner came for the night. We all dined at Mr. Cannon’s, were it was extremely beautiful. But we ate too much.
