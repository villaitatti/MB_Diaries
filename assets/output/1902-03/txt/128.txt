Jan. 1. 1903.. I Tatti Settignano. Thursday
Saw Lucca, took train to Serravalle, walked (in mud) to Pistoia, saw Pistoia and came home, very glad to get here and fully appreciative of the comforts. I was stricken down with a fierce headache.
We read nearly all of “Huckleberry Finn” on our journey.
Friday, Jan. 2. 1902 
[1903]
.
Lovely day. Lunched with Labouchères - a vast villa furnished like a hotel. Miss Labouchère seems a vulgar little demon; it turned Bertie fairly green to talk with her. Then we had a delicious walk in the Careggi woods, and so home to tea and proof-correcting. Sat up late, all of us, finishing “Huck Finn”, and laughed and laughed. Am fighting a cold.
