Thursday May 21. 1903.
Went with Bernhard to see Gertrude, and told her we feared she had the morphine habit, which she stoutly denied. We may be wrong. Mr. and Mrs. Sedgwick came to dine and Horne, and the evening was rather dull.
Friday May 22. 3.
Gertrude again - shopping - I forget what. Bernhard settling down to his Sassetta.
Saturday May 23.
Houghton and Mr. Cannon to dine - not quite so dull, but moderate.
