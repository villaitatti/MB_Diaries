I Tatti, Monday, Nov. 11 
[10]
. 1902.
Don Guido arrived, suffering from lumbago. I put him in an easy chair and showed him photographs, while Alice and B.B. drove down to town, went to Costantini’s and had tea with Gertrude. Mrs. Ross called. Wrote to Karin. Bernhard wrote to Otto Gutekunst. Finished  Le Rouge et le Noir .
