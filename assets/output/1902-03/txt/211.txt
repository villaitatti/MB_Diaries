We had music again, and another visit from May Morris, who is very interesting. Henry Van Dyck of New York came also, and to dinner, and had what he thought was a glorious talk, but which we found interesting simply and solely because at each move he displayed so clearly that he wasn’t “in it”, although he thought he was!
One evening Bernhard used a nice expression - about the qualities a man in love will “infatuate into” his lady.
