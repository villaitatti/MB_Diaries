Then we made the acquaintance of the rich American, Mr. H. W. Cannon, who has bought La Doccia, under Fiesole, a charming, modest, refined man, in spite of his millions! He had staying with him the popular American writer “Mr. Dooley” (Mr. Dunne) whom we really liked - a young man, a friend of Norman Hapgood’s.
Bernhard finished his last chapter on the 3rd of this month. We are now busy with odds and ends.
