A letter from Loeser says a great many petty and disagreeable things, showing he has been cherishing envy and malice for years, regularly storing up venom. The one funny thing is that he says he regards Bernhard’s “self-blinding conceit” as pathological, and at times feels more pity than contempt - precisely our attitude to him! He says the “Lotto” is a “treatment of an Italian master that would disgrace even an Ullmann or a Venturi!” Ma.
