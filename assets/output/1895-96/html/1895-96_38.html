As he persisted in this, I got angry and said, “Well, in other days, you would have been challenged to a duel for trying in this sneaking way to take away a man’s honour. I shall tell Berenson to have nothing more to do with you”- “Well, his behaviour about my photographs was the last straw,” Loeser began again, but I cut him short, “Your accusation of lying is enough to make us refuse ever to have anything more to do with you”, and I left him to chew on that.
