Tuesday, Feb. 4, 1896, Fiesole

Again a heavenly day. Bernhard spent a large part of it writing to Reinach a tome upon a certain Jacopo di Barbari drawing denied by Müntz and Ravaisson - (!!) to be such. In the evening we wrote an unfavourable review of Chaude Phillips’ “Collection of Charles I”.
