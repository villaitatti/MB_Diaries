Sunday, May 24, 1896, Bellevue, Naples

Packed in the morning. Bernhard went to St. Peter’s. Came here. How wonderful is the Campagna! I mean to pass my old age with it in view.

x Monday, May 25, 1896

Went to the Museum and Pompeii.

Tuesday, May 26, 1896

Museum - drive to Posillipo.

- [NOTE the hyphen] Wednesday, May 27, 1896

Museum, study of Pompeian paintings.

I was rather ill.

x Thursday, May 28, 1896

Bernhard visited churches, and had a walk with Miss Sellers, while I went to Capri and paid a visit to Frau von der Hellen, who is living there. I heard all manner of interesting things about the difficulties of starting the English line of boats - Italian camorra - etc.
