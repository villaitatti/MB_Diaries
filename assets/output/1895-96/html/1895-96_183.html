x Monday, June 8, 1896, Fiesole

Mrs. Hooker and her daughter and Mr. von Glenn came to lunch. It is hard to mix real Americans and Europeans. Their range of general conversation is entirely different. In the afternoon Bernhard went to call on Mr. and Mrs. Theodore Dwight.

Tuesday, June 9, 1896

Bernhard packed in the morning and went to Lodi in the 2.30 train.
