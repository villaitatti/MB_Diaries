x Monday, Sept. 14, 1896

Obrist article. Tea with Miss Sellers. Miss Lowndes came to hear the IV Symphony with us. It was badly given.

Tuesday, Sept. 15, 1896

Finished the Obrist article. Afternoon at Schwabing as usual. We all went to Fidelio in the evening, and Logan joined us there at 9.30, having just arrived from London.

Wednesday, Sept. 16, 1896

Scurried through the Glaspalast and the Secession, and saw nothing that was art, except Hildebrand’s Old Faun. Obrist’s bust was a disappointment. We were confronted with a 5 years’ ago admiration - Skredvig’s
