And the first thing they did on arriving was to go out and buy her two diamond rings… All the same, she detests Florence, and longs to be at home again where she can have waffles and hot cakes for breakfast. And she will drag them home, too, and what is worse, to California. It is perhaps the worst case of mother tyranny I have come across.

In the evening Bernhard read Padmore’sbook on Thought Transference and I read Goldwin Smith’s Jane Austen - delightful chiefly for its quotations from the sprightly Jane.

We are both “under the weather”, in spite of glorious sunshine and warmth. Bernhard feels his liver, I my “insides”.
