[Illness. Mrs. Sharp]

Sunday, Apr. 5, 1896

But England is not the only deadlifeyer. Enrico Costa came to see us, and in the course of a couple of hours we had completely dried up every one of the arts as subject of conversation or enjoyment.

Monday, Apr. 6, 1896

Mrs. Sharp came to lunch, and was very boring. Good nature and energy and affection for her dreadful husband appear to be her leading characteristics. The doctor came and examined me, and found chronic inflammation, relaxation, and displacement of the womb, coming from troubles at the time of Karin’s birth, but - thank heavens! - no tumour.
