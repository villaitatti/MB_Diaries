x Saturday, Dec. 21, 1895

Sunday, Dec. 22, 1895

Bothering news about the Guardi.

Reinach writes saying the “tactile values” theory can’t be true, for, if it were, panoramas would be the highest art. Fabbri came to lunch bringing a Degas (horsemen) which he will leave with me while he is in Paris. Miss Buttles came and sang “Lohengrin”. I went in to meet Logan, and we had some pleasant talk in the evening, especially about books. Here is Bernhard’s answer
