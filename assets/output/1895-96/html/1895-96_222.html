He says his mind is constantly reverting to that puzzling problem, “What is the difference between a vulgar and a fine time?”

Monday, Oct. 12, 1896, Belle Arti, Orvieto

Saw Cathedral and Cambio and went to Chiusi. We lunched there and then drove to Città della Pieve - a charming drive thorough oak forests, view superb. Found some Peruginos. Dined at Chiusi and reached here at 9.
