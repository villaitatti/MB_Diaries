But it was hard to forgive him at dinner, when he made out to Maud that he had been enjoying the scenery and drinking in the calm beauty of the landscape. Why? His face was almost purple with wrath the whole walk.In the evening he regaled me with an account of his grievances against Aunty Lill and Uncle James. I “went to bed” early, but really stole over to Bernhard’s to let off my feelings.

Monday, Mar. 2, 1896, Fiesole

Maud turns out to have the influenza, and this frightened father, so that he went off at 11 - to our great relief!

Bernhard went to town and got me my medicine, and I am really ill.
