Monday, Oct. 5, 1896, boat to Fiume

We met Mr. Phillip Stanhope at Sebenico and chatted with him on the boat all day. Although his chief preoccupation is women, he is a very intelligent administrator and practical politician, and he told us many interesting things about the Austrian régime in Bosnia and Herzegovina, where he had just been travelling. We had five hours at Zara, and I had a most delicious swim.
