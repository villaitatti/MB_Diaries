Thursday, Sept. 17, 1896, Leinfelder, Munich

Went to the Glyptotek with Miss Sellers, and then to the Pinacotek. She lunched with us, and was very amusing. I called on Mrs. Furtwängler and Bernhard went with Eugénie to see Dr. Traube, whom he liked.

Logan dined at Schwabing, but we went to hear Tannhäuser, and then quarrelled violently because Bernhard accused me of changing my opinions too quickly. But I believe he thinks exactly as I do about Wagner!!
