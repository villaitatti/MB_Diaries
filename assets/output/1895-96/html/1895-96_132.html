[Miss Sellers]

Saturday, Mar. 28, 1896, Fiesole

Pretty ill all day. Mr. Bla[y]des came to lunch. He leaves me indifferent. Beatrice Horne called and Mr. Hamilton came bringing a young Frenchman named Halévy.

Catalogued Gazette of 1882.

Sunday, Mar. 29, 1896

Read Cymbeline and Pericles and finished Wendell’s interesting chronological study of Shakspere. [sic]

Monday, Mar. 30, 1896

Rather ill. Did not do anything but read. Janet and Bernhard walked. The Buttles called.

Tuesday, Mar. 31, 1896

Miss Sellers arrived in the evening, awfully tired, but still fascinating. She had much to say of Obrist.
