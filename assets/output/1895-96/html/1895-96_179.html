In fact, she is always disappointing, when any emergency arises. We certainly were too precipitate in thinking we liked her very much. However, she is not actively disagreeable, and has some good points. I think she can manage for herself. But I told her she might come back here, unless Logan comes to live with me.

Friday, June 5, 1896

Bernhard lunched with Placci and the Papafavas, and afterwards went to see a Botticini (ascribed to P. dei Franceschi!) in private possession. Mrs. Hooker and Mr. Illsley (friends of Evelyn’s) called, the former rather beautiful and very nice, the latter charming. Later, Miss Paget and Miss Sellers came, and Herbert Horne came to dinner. With him we talked Botticelli, Botticini and the Maestro della Morte di Lucrezia until nearly midnight.
