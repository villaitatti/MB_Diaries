[Perrys. Davis. Lord Acton]

Sunday, Apr. 19, 1896, Fiesole

Mr. and Mrs. T. S. Perry came to lunch. She was more amiable than we had feared, but he was too facetious to be really interesting. I liked his face. Bernhard came home from Forlì with a rather perturbed liver, to find a nasty letter from Mr. Davis, saying that the dealers were up in arms, and suggesting that Bernhard was taking to tricky picture-dealers’ ways.

I read Lord Acton’s Inaugural Address at Cambridge on the study of modern history, and Henry IV Part II.

Monday, Apr. 20, 1896

I read over with Miss Sellers her Preface to the Pliny, and read Henry V. I am getting an absorbing taste for Shakspere! [sic]vWe read part of Acton’s address aloud in the evening, and discussed it until 11.
