Sunday, Feb. 2, 1896, Fiesole

Miss Buttles came up and sang divinely: Schubert, Rubinstein, Glück, Beethoven, Weber. Above all, “Hark! hark! the Lark”, without accompaniment. It was so warm and sunny, we had tea in the ‘cupola’.
