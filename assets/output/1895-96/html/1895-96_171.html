[Rome]

Saturday, May 16, 1896

We explored the Museo Cristiano, and had a most glorious time recognizing and baptising all sorts of early Sienese and even Florentine panels. We were quite excited! In the afternoon we went to the Borghese, and got thoroughly tired. But it was worse in the evening, when Eugénie and I went to a dreary American party at the Stillmans‘ - too dreary to describe! Bernhard chose the better part, and took Enrico Costa and his friend Gamba to dine at the Café de Roma, where they had a long talk on connoisseuring. We sat up till 1 on our roof cursing American parties!
