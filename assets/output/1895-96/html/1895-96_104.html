* Tuesday, Feb. 18, 1896, Fiesole

Still the weather of paradise! Zangwill introduced the serpent by discovering Longfellow as a Poet.

A lovely afternoon, sun, sweet air, blue distances. Mr. Morgan called, and I had an Italian lesson.

Read Lamb, Jane Austen, Wendell, the accounts of the new photographic discovery, etc.

Helped Zangwill a little (chiefly by being dictated to) in his “Without Prejudice”-

Wednesday, Feb. 19, 1896

Worked over photos of Jacopo Belllini’s drawings in the morning. Shopped and called on Miss Halsey in the afternoon.

Dined with Zangwill at the Palmerino.

Thursday, Feb. 20, 1896

The Burroughs came up to lunch, and we had a walk over to Poggio.
