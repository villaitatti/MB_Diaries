Friday, Nov. 15, 1895, Villa Rosa, Fiesole

I worse in health and spirit. Bernhard still studying Lo Spagna photographs. The younger Miss Buttles and Miss Whaling came to lunch, and nothing of the least importance was said. Maud and I had an Italian lesson, and in the evening we read “Attila, my Attila”, which Michael had just sent. Bernhard said it was like a bad smell he wanted to run away from as fast as possible. Yet
