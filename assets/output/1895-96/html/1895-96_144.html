[Two ways of describing an afternoon tea]

We all lunched in town. Bernhard at the Scotts, Janet and I with the Herricks (Mrs. Herrick is charming) and the others at Gilli e Letta’s. Lion and Bobby stayed to dinner. The doctor came and pronounced me much better.

Thursday, Apr. 16, 1896, Fiesole

Bernhard showed Lion and Bobby some photos in the morning, and I worked on Pliny proofs with Miss Sellers. Maud Cruttwell came to lunch, and dear Carlo Placci afterwards, who played “Tristan” to us Bernhard and Bertie came in to tea from a long climb with Mr. Benn.
