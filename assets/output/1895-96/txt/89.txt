In the evening we read Lamb and Ribot. 
Thursday, Jan. 30, 1896 
Read the proofs of Bernhard’s article on Italian painters in the U[nited] S[tates], which is to come out in the March Gazette des Baux Arts .  Wrote endless letters. Began to walk to Morgans’, but I felt too ill to go on. Read an essay of Lamb.
Then Edith and Bryson came to dinner, and after we took a walk in the most glorious moonlight ever seen. Edith said once she had peeped over a rock to see Bryson painting, when he did not know she was there.
