 Hamilton
Saturday, Nov. 9, 1895, Fiesole 
Finished copying, and sent off to the Atlantic, the series of “Thinking Lessons”. Bernhard worked on Lo Spagna - and got a feeling of utter nausea for the whole business of the connoisseur. He declared he was going out of the trade, but when the question came up of Maud Cruttwell’s taking and making use of his discoveries in that line, he said he wasn’t going out quite so soon as all that!
The “bald-headed butterfly”, Mr. Hamilton, came to lunch. He was full of indignation at the English law that prevents the use of naphtha tricycles, on the ground that they are “traction engines”, and must be ridden only at a walking pace, with some one in front carrying a red flag - literally! He is thinking of writing a Tragedy on Ezzelino.
