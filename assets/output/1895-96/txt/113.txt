[ Z[angwill] and poetry. Father]
Friday, Feb. 28, 1896
Made up our quarrel and worked on the Mantegna. Bernhard lunched with the Hagues, and I took Zangwill down to town, where he saw sights and I shopped with the Signora Triulzi.
We called on Miss Paget. Zangwill really got on my nerves with his inability to see the difference between bad and good poetry. 
Saturday, Feb. 29, 1896
Finished the Mantegna. Miss Horne called in the afternoon. 
Bernhard dined with Carlo Placci. 
Sunday, Mar. 1, 1896
