Thursday, Sept. 24, 1896, Tegetthoff, Vienna
Albertina in the morning, but very boring drawings. In the afternoon we went to the gallery, and in the evening to the ballet “Excelsior”, very vulgar and dull, we thought it, but Cook was in rapture and called it “simply divine”. I think it was the legs and arms, not the “beauty of motion”.
