Friday, Mar. 6, 1896 
Still ill. Finished “La Peur”. Selected photographs to send to Jaccaci of “Scribner’s” who wants Bernhard to write some articles. Miss Cruttwell appears furious. We think the Buttles have repeated to her the things Bernhard said about her to them before they made such friends. 
Saturday, Mar. 7, 1896 
Edith and Bryson came to lunch - dear things, and they had a long walk with Bernhard.
