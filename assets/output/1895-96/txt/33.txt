[ Final Quarrel with Loeser]
Lizst and Henschell. He was good humour itself and very pleasant. It rained hard all day. 
Monday, Nov. 25, 1895 
Worked, went in to Library, called and heard Miss Buttles sing, read in the evening. A quiet, pleasant enough day.
Tuesday, Nov. 26, 1895
