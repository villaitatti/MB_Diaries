[ Snow. Mantegna]
Tuesday, Feb. 25, 1896, Fiesole
Still snow - bound, and still discussing poetry. However, Miss Buttles came up and sang - she sings Handel very well - and Mr. Baring came to lunch, which was a diversion. The poetry warfare is still raging.
Wednesday, Feb. 26, 1896
The sun shone fitfully, but I was ill and did not go out. Bernhard finished his Mantegna.
Thursday, Feb. 27, 1896
I am worse. Bernhard and Zangwill had a long walk. When they came in, I told Bernhard how much I liked his Mantegna, and then gave him a few criticisms. These made him furious, so the day ended in gloom.
