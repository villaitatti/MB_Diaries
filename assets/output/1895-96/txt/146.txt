[ Ambitions]
Friday, Apr. 17, 1896 
I forgot to say that we were confessing our ambitions last night. Bertie owned to the modest desire to write “a dialectic logic of all the sciences, and an ethic that should apply to politics”. Alys followed with the hope that she might bring the woman’s movement and socialism into closer rapport. Bernhard seemed to be bent on writing a psychological aesthetic of the Fine Arts. My wishes soared no higher than writing “a classic “Guide to the Italian Pictures in the Louvre.” Janet wisely said, “I am too young to know”, and Miss Sellers did not quite seem to know, though she said she was terribly ambitious.
