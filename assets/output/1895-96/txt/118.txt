[ Janet, Maud]
Sunday, Mar. 8, 1896, Fiesole
Mr. Baring, and Beatrice and Herbert Horne came to call. The latter was less appalling than I had been led to expect. Bernhard and Zangwill had much talk and argument about the psychology of art, especially at dinner, where we talked at a great pace to cover Miss Cruttwell’s fury at the news that Miss Dodge was expected soon. A letter from the latter confesses practically to being in love with Dr. Singer, and I have wired to her to come at once. He is  au fond  a Philistine, and she would be unhappy with him. 
Zangwill said that the Celtic Spirit is made up of Gloom and Doom, but Mr. Sharp’s book about it of  Boom !
