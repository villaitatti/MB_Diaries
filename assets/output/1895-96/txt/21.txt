[ Vernon Lee and Pater ]
Thursday, Nov. 14, 1895, Fiesole 
A day of days! And we wasted the sunshine and caressing air by going to lunch with Loeser and talking stupid gossip. Afterwards Bernhard went to the Uffizi, and I went to Dr. Baldwin to get some medicine to stop an undue loss of blood which is taking away my spirits, and making my wickedness and my woes seem too great to be borne.
In the evening I began  Voigt  again, and got on better. We talked of the desirable subordination of painting to architecture from melody to the harmony of a symphony. Still, there are songs!
