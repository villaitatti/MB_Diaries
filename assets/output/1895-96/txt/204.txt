In the evening we went to the opera and saw 3 ballets - one of which contained charming costumes and a gavotte of 1830. The debutante ballerina was an Italian, with all the Italian fatal dexterity and skill - and lack of art. Her poses were marvellous, but meaningless and ugly. Herbert Cook arrived. 
x Tuesday, Sept. 22, 1896, Tegetthoff, Vienna
Called on Cook and went to the Albertina and after lunch to the Lichtenstein. We heard  Lohengrin  in the evening, and thought it would have been perfect if it had stopped at the Ouverture! Bernhard said that Music, like the other arts, had fallen into the clutches of the two dragons that guard the portals of real Art - dexterity and expressiveness.
