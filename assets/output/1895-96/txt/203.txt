Saturday, Sept. 19, 1896, Zum Stein, Salzburg
Reached here at 11.45 and after lunch went up the Gaisberg - a most lovely view. I was feeling so ill I had to go to the Gasthof and lie down, but I enjoyed the ride there and back. 
Sunday, Sept. 20, 1896, Tegetthoff, Vienna
It was raining hard, and I felt ill, so I did not go out. Bernhard went to the Museum. From 12 to 7 we were in the train. We read Creighton’s little pamphlet, “English National Character”.
Placci arrived about 10.
