Then, Bernhard thinks, he ought to have remembered that it was he, Bernhard, who really put him on the track of discriminating among his own productions. When we went to Munich last summer, Obrist was inclined to value all his things equally, perhaps the ones with what he called “Stimmung” above the others, if anything. He did not realize the splendid possibilities of his art, and delighted in representation. As to his rug, he was overcome with delight when Bernhard suggested his not finishing it. And he promised again and again we should have the first choice of pieces to reproduce.
