[ Paris.  Madame André]
Saturday, June 19 <20>, 1896, 3 rue de Beaune
Went to Louvre and in the afternoon drove on the Bois. I was  wretchedly  ill.
Sunday, June 20 <21>, 1896  
Bernhard spent the day with Miss Farnham at Rambouillet and I wandered over the Louvre and read “ The Heart of Midlothian ”, an awfully boring book for Scott. We walked a great deal in the evening, along the Seine, although I felt ill. Met Miss Lowndes. 
Monday, June 21 <22>, 1896
Went to see Madame André’s collection, but she would scarcely show us any of Italians. She has a good eye for the effective and much courage, rather than real taste. Tea with Reinach. 
* Tuesday, June 22 <23>, 1896
Saw the Pourtalès things. Not many Italians, however.
