Sunday, Oct. 11, 1896, Grand Hotel Brufani, Perugia
The sunrise was indescribably lovely! We got up and came here, and worked in the Gallery in the morning. We met Miss Edwardes, who has lodgings in the Ansidei palace, and I had tea with her after Placci went at 4. We like him more than ever.
