Gronau came to stay with Bernhard. 
Wednesday, Apr. 22, 1896 
Rainy and cold. Bernhard lunched with Placci, who had Gabriele D’Annunzio, whose real name turns out to be  Anafeto  Cipollone !! After lunch they had a splendid talk, and D’Annunzio Cipollone was marvellous. He dropped his pose, and became serious, impersonal and modest, besides being astonishingly eloquent. Dear Placci was enchanted with the success of the interview. Beatrice Horne came to lunch, and we read aloud Creighton’s “Renaissance in England”. 
Gronau was rather dull in the evening, but we were all tired, and most of us had colds in the head, so we retired early.
