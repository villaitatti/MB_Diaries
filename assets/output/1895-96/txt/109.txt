These experiences we do not regard as ends in themselves though they may have their use. But the point of our being here is to have leisure and opportunity to cultivate our taste so that we may appreciate all the achievements of European art. Mr. Berenson has made up his mind to read (for pleasure) all the Greek that is considered good literature, and he is urging me to take up my Greek again for the same purpose.
