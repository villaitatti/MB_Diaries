Friday, Oct. 9, 1896, Posta, Foligno
We left Ancona at 7 this morning, and reached Gualdo Tadini [sic] about 10. The only thing to see there was a ruined Nicola da Foligno, so we were rather bored. 
But we had a nice lunch and walked to the train at 2 greatly refreshed. At 2.45 we reached the station of Nocera-Umbra and then drove to the town. It is magnificently set on its hill, but it is one of the most run-down and miserable towns we ever entered. the only thing to see was a fine polyptych by Nicola da Foligno. After seeing that, we drove here  - a charming drive of two hours. We talked of poetry, and at dinner of the old subject of Freemasonry versus Clericalism.
