Wednesday, Oct. 14, 1896
Letters and settling in, in the morning, shopping in the afternoon. Still letters at night. Magazines positively  appalling . 
Thursday, Oct. 15, 1896
Notes and letters in morning. Maud Cruttwell and Binney Dibblee and his sister called at 3. After tea and a walk, still struggling with the magazines. 
Friday, Oct. 16, 1896
