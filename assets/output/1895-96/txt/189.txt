* Wednesday, June 23 <24>, 1896 
Met Reinach at Bonnat’s and afterwards went to Count Robert Pourtalès where there was a Benozzo.
Reinach took tea with us, and Miss Lowndes sat with us where we dined, she having dined already.
Still ill. 
Thursday, June 24 <25>, 1896 
Browsed about among French and Renaissance sculptures. I felt better, so though it rained we were happy. Dined at Weber’s, and then I started for London. 
It has been a week that has drawn us very close together. I feel as if I never half appreciated Bernhard’s goodness before. I am sure I can never make him seriously unhappy again.
