[ Miss Sellers’ lectures!] gave me what is so rare, a perfectly friendly adverse criticism of Bernhard and myself. She thinks that to take up people with such enthusiasm and to drop them with such indignation as we do, is undignified. If one must “schimpfen”, one should do it with humour, in a lively society way, not treating it over-seriously. Furthermore, one must be aware that people usually impute to jealousy and goodness knows what mean motive the sentiments kindled in one’s breast by the pure fire of righteous indignation. Bernhard has the reputation of being a man who cannot bear other men, as he is furiously jealous of their reputation.
