Tuesday, Oct. 27, 1896
Finished first draft of Brea article for Gazette - Bernhard lunched with Sidney Colvin, and rather enjoyed him. He is an Englishman at any rate!  I called on a young woman American doctor, Alice Littell, who is setting up here. She seemed pleasant. I described Maud in a letter to Alys as taking every idea that chanced to graze the top of her cranium to be a divinely inspired dogma, an attitude I sympathize with when the cranium has a few cracks to let the ideas in. She thinks Pater weak and complicated, and won’t admit Rossetti, Keats or Baudelaire any more as “Art”.
