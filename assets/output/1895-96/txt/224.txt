Saturday, Oct. 17, 1896, Villa Rosa, Fiesole
We both “pottered”, but separately, in the morning, arranging books, papers, etc.
In the afternoon we put away the summer’s collection of photographs - a tough job. In the evening Bernhard read pre-Dantesque Italian poetry, and I began  Matarazzo’s Chronicle of Perugia .  (not well.)
