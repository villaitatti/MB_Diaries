We had our “rampart walk,” and read Charles Lamb and Montaigne in the evening. Bernhard is beginning to care more for English prose style. I need no longer mention here all the books we read, as I put them now in a separate volume. What joy to be in sunshine again!
Wednesday, Jan. 29, 1896, Fiesole 
Miss Hay came to lunch, and Bernhard took a walk with her.
Later, we walked down to the Palmerino and I got Mr. Hamilton to supervise the translation of Bernhard’s article on the Caen Sposalizio for the Gazette.
