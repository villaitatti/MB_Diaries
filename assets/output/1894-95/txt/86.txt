Monday Jan. 14.  95. Lion d’Or. Reims
Still here, and still happy. We have enjoyed the Cathedral  au fond , and each other very much.
Tuesday Jan. 15.  95. Victoria. Bâle
Bernhard and I left at 7.0 in the morning, and HO was going to Paris in the 8.12 train. We came here, reading  La fin du Paganisme (Boissier ) and  James’ Psychology .  Arriving here, we found the line broken by an avalanche, and so have to stay over.
