Saturday  Jan. 12 Reims
The Cathedral --! Impossible to express the overwhelming impression of that interior. During all the day a continuous flow of interesting talk, on hundreds of subjects, but centering generally about some aesthetic or critical question, or the position and future, or real nature of women. 
Sunday Jan. 13. 1895  Reims
We have stayed on to get a full impression of the Cathedral, and to enjoy HO ... who is himself simply overwhelmed with the architecture and sculpture. We all get on very well together. Saw the fête of S. Remi at the Church of S. Remi
