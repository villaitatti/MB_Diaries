Tuesday Oct. 15. 1895. Roma. Savona.

It took us a long, hot, indescribably tiresome journey to get here - and there is only one thing here to see – and so many not to see - or smell - or hear. Still the one thing, a fine altar-piece by Foppa, had to be seen, and we have done it. Our consciences are at rest, and we shall forget the bad dinner, the dirty town full of squalling brats and discordant brass-bands.

Wednesday Oct. 16. 95. Nettuno. Pisa
