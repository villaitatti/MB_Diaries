Sunday April 1. 1894. Maiano

Again walking, talking, driving, talking.Vernon Lee this morning declared that she thought Berenson “absolutely insensitive to literature”, she thinks he is nothing but a “Scientist”, without a ray of aesthetic appreciation for anything but his own “Fach”, and as for that, well, it isn’t worth spending much time on.
