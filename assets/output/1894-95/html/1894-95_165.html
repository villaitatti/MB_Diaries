Friday Sept. 13.’95 3 rue de Beaume. Paris.

We called on Reinach, who is unfortunately just leaving. His way of approaching art threw us both into gloom. It is so deadly. But he was personally most friendly. He went over the whole Louvre with us in the afternoon, and then took us to tea in the rue Royale, and sat and chatted a long time. I had to write letters all the evening.

Saturday. Sept. 14. 1895. Paris.
