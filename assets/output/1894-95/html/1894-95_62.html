We discussed “Manon Lescaut” a good deal, and found it peculiarly wonderful because it was the first psychological presentation of a woman who could love and yet be “unfaithful”, regarding her body as a thing apart. Fafnir has the faculty of drawing B.B. out in a delightful way. I am reading D’Annunzio’s Trionfo della Morte, and B.B. “Nell Horn”.

[London. Thursday. August 23. 1894]
