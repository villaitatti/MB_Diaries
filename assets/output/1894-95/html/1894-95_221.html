Grammar.

When they found out that the life could not be imitated, then all their enthusiasm, their élan of passionate interest, went into grammar and archaeology - where it has remained ever since.

Perry, in working out the formal development of Greek literature, and connecting it with psychology even, has gone a step ahead of the mere grammatical and political interest. But of course he is no nearer the point than Morelli or Berenson in “the Venetian Painters”--!!

Wednesday Nov.. 6. 1895. Fiesole
