[Sept. 11, 1895] Hotel de France. Bourges.

Our enjoyment of Tours was almost rapturous, the stained glass, particularly in the west rose window, was ecstatic and also our enjoyment of each other. Bernhard has real hope for me in the future, and the gratitude and love I feel for him is indescribable.

Thursday. Sept. 12. 1895. Paris

This was our best day of all. The Cathedral of Bourges is simply unsurpassable! A great monster, really alive, from the outside, and inside
