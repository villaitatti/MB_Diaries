There remains the question whether these “architectonic effects” are not equally life-bearing with the suggestion of movement, etc., which Bernhard calls the heart of hearts of art??

We went to the Palmerino and heard a rather platitudinous lecture from Vernon Lee on “Art and Life”. Mr. Benn said that “the people quaked more than the earth”- Still it was a severe shock. The old earth shook herself like a Newfoundland dog coming out of the water.
