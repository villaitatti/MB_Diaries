Sunday, Dec. 9. 1894.

Met Reinach in [the] Louvre and looked at drawings in the Salle des Boites - then lunched with him, meeting his wife - an ex-medical student - Then we went to this concert and afterwards Loeser and Sturges came to dine and spend the evening.
